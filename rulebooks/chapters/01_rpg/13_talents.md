# Talents

Talents represent a character's training, experience, exceptional abilities, and personal development. Characters acquire Talents by spending Experience Points (XP). Talents provide permanent improvements, new capabilities, or Command Abilities that may be used during combat.

Talents are divided into three categories:

- Generic Talents: Available to all characters.
- Class Talents: Available to characters belonging to the specified Class.
- Specialization Talents: Optional Talents representing particular combat techniques, social capabilities, or other specialized training.

All Talents contribute to the same Talent Pyramid.

## TALENT PROGRESSION

All Generic, Class, and Specialization Talents share the **6/5/4/3/2 trapezoid**, costing up to **250 XP**. Each additional Talent at Tier N requires one additional Talent in Tier N−1, after two lower-Tier Talents unlock the first slot. These lower-tier Talents are **not exclusive pairs** or consumed. See [Experience and Advancement](03_experience_and_advancement.md#2-talent-tiers-and-costs) for the complete table, caps, ranked purchases, and examples.

Skills improve one threshold step at a time (6+ → 5+ → 4+ → 3+ → 2+) unless stated otherwise.

## COMMAND ABILITIES

Talents may grant new Command Abilities. A character permanently learns any Command Ability granted by a Talent they possess. Characters may know any number of Command Abilities, but may normally prepare only four optional Command Abilities for battle. Subclass Signature Commands and the Artificer's Field Specialty are always prepared and do not occupy these slots. Passive Talents and Weapon or Spell Attack profiles do not occupy prepared Command slots.

### Activating Commands

Command Abilities specify their CP cost and timing. Common timings include:

| Timing | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Movement | During the character's Movement activation |
| Assault | During the character's Assault activation |
| Combat | During the character's Combat activation |
| Reaction | In response to the stated trigger |
| Passive | Applies automatically under its stated conditions |

Abilities may normally be used once per Turn unless their descriptions specify otherwise. A Reaction may be used during another Unit's Turn when its trigger occurs. The reacting Unit does not need to be the active Unit. Abilities explicitly limited to once per Round, once per battle, or another frequency retain those restrictions. Repeatable abilities explicitly permit additional uses, with each use paying its normal CP cost. Continuous passive bonuses apply normally and are not expended.

### Skill-Based Commands

Some Command Abilities require a Characteristic + Skill roll.

These follow the normal Skill Check rules:

- Roll D6 equal to the specified Characteristic.
- Each die meeting or exceeding the Skill rating generates one success.
- Resolve the ability using either the number of successes or the highest die, as specified.

There is no additional Difficulty requirement for these Commands.

### Successes — X

When an ability uses X, X equals the number of successful dice. An ability might Move X inches, affect X Hits, or remove X conditions. Zero successes normally means the scaled effect is zero. The number of dice rolled and the character's Skill rating naturally determine the possible results.

### Highest Die

When an ability uses the highest die, use the highest result in the rolled pool. Unless otherwise stated, at least one successful die is required for the effect to occur.

### Other Rules

Command Skill Checks may generate Surges and Blunders normally. A Command does not receive an additional Surge benefit unless a rule grants one. The CP cost is paid even when a Command Check produces no successes. Command effects affecting an Attack pool modify the Unit's combined Attack pool unless explicitly stated otherwise. Repeated applications of the same temporary effect do not stack unless a rule permits it.

## GENERIC TALENTS

Generic Talents are available to all Classes. They represent broad improvements in physical ability, combat skill, resilience, leadership, and general expertise.

### Combat Prowess

#### Ranked — Tier 1+ — Passive

Choose one Weapon or Spell Attack when acquiring Rank I.

Every Rank:

- Increase that Attack's Attacks by 1, or improve its Hit by 1 step.
- Improve one Skill by 1 step not improved by a lower rank of this skill.

The same Attack is improved by every Rank of this Talent. Prowess applies to the chosen Attack profile regardless of whether it is used during Assault or Combat. Prowess may not improve the same Attack as Martial Prowess or Spell Prowess.

### General Improvements

| Talent | Tier | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mobility Training — Ranked | 1+ | Each Rank: +1" Move and improve one Skill not improved by a lower rank of this skill. |
| Toughened — Ranked | 1+ | Each Rank: +1 Wound and improve one Skill not improved by a lower rank of this skill. |
| Commanding Presence — Ranked | 1+ | Each Rank: improve Influence 1 step and increase friendly-targeted Command ranges by 1" |
| Sharpeye — Ranked | 1+ | Each Rank: choose one ranged weapon or ranged spell attack: it gains Accurate +1. Each rank must choose a different weapon or spell. |
| Critical Prowess — Ranked | 2+ | Choose one Attack; on Surge, gain Critical X, where X equals Rank |
| Long Range — Ranked | 2+ | Choose one ranged Weapon Attack or ranged Spell Attack; increase its Long Range value by +3" per Rank. |
| Favored Enemy — Ranked | 2+ | Gain Anti-[TYPE] X, where X equals Rank + 1 (Anti-[TYPE] 2 at Rank I, 3 at Rank II, and so on). |
| Evasive Training — Ranked | 3+ | Improve Evade by 1 step per Rank |
| Nimble — Ranked | 3+ | Gain Cover +1 for each rank |
| Weapon Mastery — Ranked | 3+ | Each Rank: +1 Damage to one different melee Weapon Attack |
| Warded | 4 | Gain Ward 6+, or improve an existing Ward by 1 step |
| Exceptional Development — Ranked | 4+ | Increase one eligible Characteristic by 1 and gain its related improvement |
| Greater Ward | 5 | Requires Warded; improve Ward by 1 step against Physical or Magic Attacks |

#### Critical Prowess

Choose one Attack when purchasing Rank I. **When it generates at least one Surge**, gain **Critical +X** for that Attack, where X equals the Talent's Rank. It triggers **once per Attack**, even if multiple Surges are generated, unless another rule expressly permits repeated activation. This stacks with inherent Critical and converts eligible failed dice after rerolls. Ward still applies.

#### Exceptional Development

#### Ranked — Tier 4+ — Passive

Each Rank increases one Characteristic not previously improved by this Talent by 1, to a maximum of 6. The improved Characteristic also grants:

| Characteristic | Additional Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| Body | +1 Wound |
| Agility | +1" Move |
| Mind | Improve Evade by 1 step |
| Spirit | If the character possesses Warded, improve Ward by 1 step |

Each Characteristic may only be selected once through Exceptional Development.

### Additional Generic Abilities

#### Silver Tongue — Tier 1

**Passive**

Narrative: When an Influence Check Surges, choose one:

- Gain +1D6 on the next Influence Check against the same target.
- Learn something useful about the target's motives, fears, or intentions.
- Improve the target's disposition where appropriate.

Structured Combat: Once per Round, after using a Command Ability on another friendly Unit, that Unit gains Accurate 1 on its next Attack this Round.

#### Composed — Tier 1

**Passive**

Narrative: Once per scene, reduce the severity of a complication caused by an Influence Check Blunder. The Blunder still occurs.

Structured Combat: Once per Round, reroll the first natural 1 you encounter on a check, attack, charge, or save.

#### Perfect Opening — Tier 3

**1 CP — Reaction**

When making an Attack roll, Save roll, or Skill Check, but before resolving its result, reroll up to three dice. The new results must be kept. A die may only be rerolled once.

## CRITICAL SPECIALIZATION

Critical Specialization represents the ability to exploit weaknesses and inflict particularly dangerous attacks. These Talents do not require an Attack to possess Critical beforehand.

| Talent | Tier | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exploit Weakness | 3+ | When your Attack Surges, add one additional damage per rank of Exploit Weakness to the damage pool. |
| Perfect Strike | 4 | When your Attack Surges, deal Direct Damage 1 to its target **for each Surge generated by that Attack**; Ward applies |

Critical Specialization effects may activate alongside Critical Prowess or inherent Critical. Deadly Opening is removed from this specialization to avoid overlapping with existing accuracy and Attack improvements.

## SOCIAL SPECIALIZATION

Social Talents improve a character's ability to influence, deceive, intimidate, organize, and understand others. They also provide limited battlefield applications.

### Social Talent Reference

| Talent | Tier | Combat Benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Menacing Presence | 2 | Successful Charge worsens the target's Hit on its next Attack |
| Tactical Deception | 2 | Allows access to Tactical Feint outside the Rogue Class |
| Rallying Voice | 2 | Reaction removing temporary penalties through Influence |
| Read the Room | 2 | Gain Accurate 1 on your first Attack against a selected enemy |
| Master Orator | 3 | Extend a Command's secondary accuracy benefit to another ally |

#### Menacing Presence — Tier 2

**Passive**

Narrative: When an intimidation-based Influence Check Surges, the target may hesitate, reveal information, back down temporarily, or expose something it fears.

Structured Combat: After successfully Charging an enemy, worsen its Hit by 1 step on its next Attack this Round.

#### Tactical Deception — Tier 2

**Passive**

Narrative: When a deception-based Influence Check Surges, establish one minor false assumption that the target accepts until contradicted.

If the Check Blunders, you may choose which part of the deception creates suspicion.

Structured Combat: You may purchase Tactical Feint as a Talent even if you are not a Rogue.

#### Rallying Voice — Tier 2

**1 CP — Reaction**

Choose yourself or one friendly Unit within Short Range. Roll Spirit + Influence. Remove up to X temporary penalties or conditions from that Unit, where X equals the number of successes. Eligible effects include temporary Hit, Move, and Evade penalties, and Off-Balance.

Narrative: When an Influence Check to encourage, inspire, or organize others Surges, one affected ally gains +1D6 on its next related Skill Check.

#### Read the Room — Tier 2

**Passive**

Narrative: When an Awareness or Influence Check in a social situation Surges, ask one additional question concerning motives, deception, relationships, discomfort, or leverage.

Structured Combat: During the first Round, choose one visible enemy Unit. Your first Attack against that Unit gains Accurate 1.

#### Master Orator — Tier 3

**Passive**

Narrative: When an Influence Check Surges, extend one beneficial Surge consequence to another relevant person or group.

When the Check Blunders, choose one ally present who is not affected by the resulting complication.

Structured Combat: Once per Round, after using a Command Ability on another friendly Unit, choose a second friendly Unit within 2" of the original target.

The second Unit gains Accurate 1 on its next Attack this Round.

## ANIMAL COMPANION TALENTS

Any Class may learn these Talents. The animal uses **its own NPC Unit profile**, not the owner's PC weapon/spell profiles, and is an independent friendly Unit. A companion may use a Command only when its profile or an acquired upgrade expressly grants it one; such Commands use **the controlling player's CP** and follow normal personal-Unit activation timing.

#### Animal Companion — Tier 2

**Passive**

Gain **one** bonded Small or Standard Animal Companion from the [Bestiary](17_bestiary.md#7-animal-companions), **as printed**, without free Training upgrades. Add that companion's listed Points to the PC's battlefield value. It:

- Activates independently during Movement, Assault, and eligible Combat Phases, following the chosen combat mode's normal Unit order.
- Makes its own Move, one Assault action, Charge, and Melee Attack when eligible; it does not attack alongside its owner's activation for free.
- Maintains its own Wounds, Saves, Keywords and equipment/Attack profiles.
- Remains within Short Range of its owner whenever reasonably possible.
- Cannot control objectives independently, contribute Command Points, or use Commands that it has not explicitly learned.
- Does not receive the owner's personal Prowess, equipment improvements, or Talents unless a rule states otherwise.

If Defeated, it is unavailable for the remainder of that encounter; recovery or replacement is settled between adventures. Only one bonded companion is gained through this Tier 2 Talent.

#### Greater Animal Companion — Tier 4

**Prerequisite:** Animal Companion. **Passive**.

Choose one of the following options when acquiring this Talent:

1. **Develop one companion:** Keep the existing companion and give it **two different Companion Training upgrades** from the table below.
2. **Bond a second companion:** Gain a second Small or Standard Animal Companion as printed; give **one Companion Training upgrade to each** of the two companions.

Both companions remain separate friendly Units with **independent activations**, and both have their listed Bestiary Points added to the PC's battlefield value. Training benefits are part of this 20-XP Talent and do not add an extra Point surcharge. A PC may have at most two bonded companions from these Talents.

| Companion Training | Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| **Hardy** | +1 maximum Wound |
| **Swift** | +1" Move |
| **Ferocious** | +1 Attack die on one chosen Melee Attack profile |
| **Precise** | One chosen Attack profile gains Accurate 1 |
| **Resilient** | Gain Tough 1 |
| **Protective** | Gain Cover 1 against eligible ranged Attacks |

A specific Training benefit may be selected only once per companion. Separate Bestiary upgrades purchased for Points remain subject to their own limits; identical benefits may not be taken twice merely by changing their source. Training does not allow an animal to use a Command it does not normally possess. Creature-specific CP Commands, such as a bird's Spot Prey, may be bought when allowed by its Bestiary entry.

### Warrior Talent Abilities

#### Hold Fast — Tier 1

**1 CP — Reaction**

When targeted by an Attack, gain Tough 1 against that Attack.

#### Press Forward — Tier 1

**1 CP — Movement**

Choose yourself or one friendly Unit within Short Range. Roll Body + Athletics. Move the chosen Unit up to X inches, where X equals the number of successes. This movement cannot enter Engagement.

#### Guarded Advance — Tier 1

**1 CP — Movement**

Choose yourself or one friendly Unit within Short Range. Move the selected Unit up to 2". It gains Tough 1 against the next ranged Attack targeting it this Round. This movement cannot enter Engagement.

#### Extended Shot — Tier 1+

**Ranked — Passive**

Choose one non-Firearm ranged Weapon Attack. Increase its Range by 2" per Rank.

#### Counterattack — Tier 2

**1 CP — Reaction**

After surviving an Melee Attack, gain +1 Attack die on your next Melee Weapon Attack against the attacker. This bonus expires at the end of your next Combat activation.

#### Bodyguard — Tier 2

**1 CP — Reaction**

When an allied Unit within 2" is targeted by an Attack or Charge, you may move up to 2" toward that ally. Roll Body + Athletics. After the incoming Hits or Charge Impact are determined, but before Saves, redirect up to X Hits to yourself, where X equals the number of successes. Resolve your own Saves against the redirected Hits. Hits not redirected remain allocated to the original target. A redirected Hit cannot be redirected again.

#### Challenge — Tier 2

**1 CP — Assault**

Choose one enemy Unit within Short Range. Until the end of the Round, that Unit worsens its Hit by 1 step when attacking another target, provided you are a legal target for its Attack.

#### Driving Blow — Tier 2

**1 CP — Combat**

After your Melee Weapon Attack deals Damage to an enemy, roll Body + Athletics. If the Check generates at least one success, push the target directly away up to X inches, where X is the highest die rolled. You may follow the target by up to 2". This is forced movement. The follow-up movement does not count as Disengaging.

#### Breakthrough — Tier 2

**Passive**

After defeating an enemy Unit in Engagement, move up to 2". This movement may enter Engagement with another enemy.

#### Dig In — Tier 2

**Passive**

If you did not Move during the Movement Phase, improve Armor by 1 step against ranged Attacks until your next Movement activation.

#### Mighty Impact — Tier 2+

**Passive, Ranked**

Gain **Impact X**, where X equals the Rank of Mighty Impact. This **adds X Hits to the normal highest-die Charge Impact pool** following a successful Charge; Armor and Ward apply, but Evade does not.

#### Brace Training — Tier 2

**Passive**

When you receive a Charge, gain **Brace 1** against that Charge if you did not voluntarily Move during the preceding Movement Phase. This stacks with other applicable Brace X.

#### Second Wind — Tier 3

**1 CP — Movement — Once per Battle**

Heal 2 Wounds to yourself.

#### Unyielding — Tier 3

**1 CP — Reaction**

When Damage from an incoming Attack against you is about to be applied, after Saves have been resolved, roll Body + Survival. Reduce the Damage of up to X unsaved Hits by 1 each, to a minimum of 0, where X equals the number of successes.

#### Last Stand — Tier 4

**2 CP — Reaction — Once per Battle**

When you would become Defeated, remain with accumulated Damage equal to one less than your Wounds characteristic instead.

**Passive**

**1 CP — Reaction**

#### Hamstring — Tier 2

**1 CP — Combat**

#### Retaliatory Step — Tier 2

**Passive**

After dealing Damage to an enemy in Engagement, move up to 1". This movement may leave Engagement without making you Withdrawn, but cannot enter Engagement with another enemy.

#### Opportunist — Tier 3

**1 CP — Reaction — Once per Round**

When another friendly Unit deals Damage to an enemy you are Engaged with, make one Melee Weapon Attack against that enemy.

#### Close-Quarters Shooter — Tier 3

**Passive**

When making a ranged Weapon Attack against an enemy you are Engaged with, gain Accurate 1. The target may still use Evade normally.

**1 CP — Reaction**

**1 CP — Reaction**

When targeted by a non-Instant Melee Attack, immediately perform **Blink 2**. If the entire defending Unit is outside the Attack’s Range after repositioning, the triggering Attack fails. Follow the normal legal-placement, elevation, and Coherency restrictions.

**1 CP — Movement**

Choose yourself or one friendly Unit within Short Range that is not Engaged. The Unit immediately performs **Blink X**, where X equals your Mind. Use the leading-Model and Coherency procedure in [Keywords — Blink X](10_keywords.md#blink-x); the destination must be legal and outside Engagement.

#### Arcane Rupture — Tier 3

**1 CP — Assault**

Choose one visible enemy Unit within Medium Range. Roll Mind + Mysticism. #### Arcane Penetration — Tier 3

**Passive — Once per Round**

When one of your Spell Attacks Surges, replace one normal successful Hit with Direct Damage 1 against the target. Ward applies normally. The replaced Hit is removed from the normal Attack resolution.

#### Effortless Channeling — Tier 4

**Passive — Once per Round**

Reduce the CP cost of your first Mage Class, Subclass, or Talent Command Ability by 1, to a minimum of 0. This does not reduce the CP cost of unrelated Commands granted by other sources.

#### Arcane Storm — Tier 5

**1 CP — Movement**

Instead of making your normal Move this phase, place a 25 mm token within Medium Range of this Character. **At the end of the Round**, determine which Units have **at least half their current Models within 3"** of the token, rounding up. A Model counts as within the radius if any part of its base is inside it.

Each qualifying Unit suffers **X separate automatic Magic Hits at Damage 1**, where X equals your **Mind** characteristic. **X is applied once per Unit, not per Model.** Resolve each Hit against that Unit using **Armor → Ward → Damage; Evade does not apply** unless another rule explicitly permits it. Allocate resulting Damage following the normal Unit rules, without limiting Damage allocation to Models inside the radius.

The storm affects qualifying friendly and enemy Units alike. Remove the token after resolving all Hits. See [Area Effects Against Units](10_keywords.md#area-effects-against-units).

**2 CP — Movement**

Choose up to two friendly Units within Short Range. Heal 1 Wound to each selected Unit. Renewal cannot restore removed Models.

#### Miracle Worker — Tier 3

**Passive**

Your Healing Command Abilities may be used one additional time during the same Turn. Each use requires its normal CP expenditure and is resolved separately. Additionally, once per Round, when one of your abilities Heals a Unit, increase the amount Healed by 1. This additional healing applies to one Unit affected by that use of the ability. Miracle Worker does not remove any other restrictions on the Healing Command.

#### Effortless Channeling — Tier 4

**Passive — Once per Round**

Reduce the CP cost of your first Priest Class, Subclass, or Talent Command Ability by 1, to a minimum of 0. This does not reduce the CP cost of unrelated Commands granted by other sources.

## ARTIFICER TALENTS

Artificer Talents expand a limited pool of prepared field inventions. They use the normal Talent tier prerequisites and XP costs, rather than a new progression track. The complete [Alchemist, Machinist, and Runesmith Formula lists](18_artificer_formulas.md) contain five Formulas each.

| Talent | Tier | Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Formula Study — Ranked | 2+ | Passive |
| Formula Prowess — Ranked | 2+ | Passive |
| Improvised Formula | 3 | 0 CP, Movement, Once per Battle |
| Cross-Discipline | 5 | Passive |

### Formula Study — Tier 2+

**Ranked — Passive**

Each rank teaches **one additional Formula from your own Artificer Subclass**. You must not already know it. The Formula is not automatically prepared unless selected as Field Specialty or granted an explicit exemption. You begin knowing two of the five, so a maximum of **three ranks** normally allows you to learn all five.

### Formula Prowess — Tier 2+

**Ranked — Passive**

Each rank improves **one different known Formula**. Choose **two different Minor Enhancements** or **one Major Enhancement** and apply them to that Formula whenever it is used. Record the affected Formula and enhancements on the Character Sheet.

| Enhancement | Size | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extended Delivery | Minor | Increase the Formula's target, placement, or Attack range by **1"**. Does not increase Blast radius or number of targets. |
| Fine Calibration | Minor | An eligible Formula Attack gains **Accurate 1**; add to other applicable Accurate values. |
| Focused Utility | Minor | Increase one printed movement bonus, Slow value, or Brace value by **1**. Applies only to an eligible number in the Formula, and does not bypass any printed maximum. |
| Reliable Trigger | Minor | Increase one trap's trigger radius by **1"**. |
| Forceful Attack | Major | Add **1 Attack die** to a Formula Attack **or** improve its Hit by one step (minimum 2+); choose one. |
| Potent Remedy | Major | Increase a healing Formula's printed **Heal cap by 1**, to a maximum of 3; does not revive Models or remove Critical Injuries. |
| Amplified Charge | Major | A Formula trap that inflicts automatic Hits gains **one additional automatic Hit** when triggered. |
| Wide Application | Major | Increase a nondamaging area Formula's radius by **1"**, without changing how many times affected Units resolve it. |
| **Refined Technique** | **Major** | For an eligible Formula with a **Characteristic + Skill Check**, after a successful roll, count **one additional effective success** when resolving that Formula's scaled effect. Does not make a failed Check succeed, generate Surges, or override its printed cap. |

You cannot select the same named enhancement twice on one Formula. Each Formula may benefit from **one rank of Formula Prowess**, but later ranks can improve other known Formulas. Enhancements never remove the Formula's CP cost or any Assault action requirement. They cannot increase printed Damage or grant permanent free equipment. If a modification cannot legally affect the chosen Formula, choose another.

### Improvised Formula — Tier 3

**0 CP — Movement — Once per Battle**

During your own Movement activation, exchange one **optional prepared Formula** for another Formula you **already know** from your own Subclass. The newly selected Formula becomes prepared immediately; the displaced Formula is no longer prepared. You do not gain another Command slot or extra activation. Your always-prepared Field Specialty is unchanged.

### Cross-Discipline — Tier 5

**Passive**

Learn one Formula from another Artificer Subclass. It is **always prepared as an additional Field Specialty**, without occupying an optional Command slot. Pay the Formula's ordinary CP and Assault action costs when using it. This does not grant access to the other Subclass's full Formula list, equipment, or non-Formula features. It can be chosen for a future eligible Formula Prowess rank if not already improved.

## TALENT AND ATTACK INTERACTIONS

### Prowess and PC Attack Profiles

Player Characters use the **complete printed Attack values** in [Equipment](11_equipment_and_crafting.md), [Starting Spell Attacks](06_spells_and_equipment.md), and [Spell Catalogue](12_spell_catalogue.md). Prowess and Martial or Spell Prowess improve the selected PC Attack beyond the printed profile, as specified by each Talent. These personal improvements do not apply to summoned Units, Animal Companions, or allied Units unless a rule explicitly states otherwise.

### Critical and Surges

Attack rolls may generate one Surge or Blunder **per complete pair** of natural sixes or ones, after permitted rerolls. An ability worded **when the Attack Surges** triggers only once per Attack, whereas an effect explicitly worded **for each Surge** may scale with every Surge. Ordinary Skill Checks and Charges normally generate at most one instance of each.


After all rerolls, printed/passive Critical X converts failed Hit dice into Critical Hits only on Surge; a CP-paid Command explicitly granting Critical X does not require a Surge. If a Talent grants **Critical +X on Surge**, that bonus stacks for the triggering Attack. Ward applies to these Hits; Armor and Evade do not. See [Keywords — Critical X](10_keywords.md#critical-x).

### Skill-Based Abilities

When a Talent refers to X successes, X equals the number of successful dice in the specified Characteristic + Skill roll. No additional Difficulty is imposed unless an exceptional rule explicitly requires one. Effects remain limited naturally by the number of dice rolled, the number of eligible targets or Hits, and the normal rules governing the affected characteristic.

### Prepared Command Abilities

Personal phase Commands require **that Unit to be the one currently activated**. A Command targeting another friendly Unit does not let the owner activate an unselected Unit's own personal Command. Faction and Doctrine Commands instead require controlling-side priority; Reactions follow their triggers. Command use does not consume the selected Unit's one Assault action unless the Command explicitly replaces it.


Optional Command Abilities learned from Generic, Class, or Specialization Talents all share the same four prepared Command slots. Subclass Signature Commands and the Artificer's Field Specialty remain automatically prepared. A character may exchange which optional Commands are prepared before a battle, following the normal preparation rules.

### Talent Availability

Generic and Specialization Talents are available to all Classes unless otherwise specified. Class Talents are restricted to their corresponding Classes, except where another Talent explicitly grants access. Animal Companion Talents are Generic Talents and may be acquired by any Class.

### Spell Acquisition

Talents no longer directly grant additional known Spell Attacks unless explicitly stated. New Spell Attacks are acquired through scrolls, magical texts, training, or other sources described in the Items and Spells rules. Spell Attacks do not occupy prepared Command slots and may be improved through appropriate Prowess Talents.
