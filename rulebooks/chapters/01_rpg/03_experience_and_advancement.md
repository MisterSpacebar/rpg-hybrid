# Experience and Advancement

## 1. Experience Points

Characters earn **Experience Points (XP)** through adventures, achievements, and campaign progression. **Spent XP** purchases Talents; **unspent XP** remains available for later purchases. The rules for awarding XP depend on the play mode: see [General Adventuring](07_general_adventuring_rules.md#6-experience-and-advancement). XP expenditure does not require a Company Develop Order.

## 2. Talent Tiers and Costs

| Talent Tier | XP Cost per Talent |
| --- | ---: |
| Tier 1 | 5 XP |
| Tier 2 | 10 XP |
| Tier 3 | 15 XP |
| Tier 4 | 20 XP |
| Tier 5 | 25 XP |

### Talent trapezoid: prerequisites

Talent prerequisites are **cumulative, not exclusive pairs**. To purchase the **first** Talent in Tier N (N > 1), own **two** Talents in Tier N−1. Each **additional** Talent in Tier N requires **one additional** Talent in Tier N−1. Lower-tier Talents are not consumed and can support multiple higher-tier purchases.

**General requirement:** To own `k` Talents in Tier N, you must own at least `k + 1` Talents in Tier N−1. You must also satisfy any Talent-specific prerequisite. Tier 1 has no preceding-tier prerequisite.

For example, two Tier 1 Talents unlock the first Tier 2 Talent; three Tier 1 Talents unlock a second Tier 2 Talent. Two Tier 2 Talents unlock the first Tier 3 Talent, provided their own prerequisites were met.

### Complete 6/5/4/3/2 trapezoid

| Tier | Maximum Talents | XP each | XP subtotal |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 6 | 5 | 30 |
| 2 | 5 | 10 | 50 |
| 3 | 4 | 15 | 60 |
| 4 | 3 | 20 | 60 |
| 5 | 2 | 25 | 50 |
| **Total** | **20** | — | **250 XP** |

The maximum of **250 XP spent on Talents** assumes the Character fills all these slots. Each tier also has its shown cap. Talent ranks occupy slots and count toward requirements at the tier of that particular rank.

### Ranked Talents

Ranked Talents may be purchased more than once. Each additional Rank is purchased at the next Tier above the preceding Rank (up to Tier 5) and must satisfy that Tier's normal slot and lower-tier prerequisites. For example, a Tier 1+ Ranked Talent costs 5 XP for Rank I, then 10 XP for Rank II, 15 XP for Rank III, 20 XP for Rank IV, and 25 XP for Rank V. Ranks are bought in order, with each Rank occupying one Talent slot at its Tier. Individual Talent text determines the effect of each Rank.

## 3. Skill Advancement

When a Talent grants a Skill improvement, improve one eligible Skill by one step unless specified otherwise: **6+ → 5+ → 4+ → 3+ → 2+**. Skills cannot normally improve beyond 2+. A Characteristic cannot normally exceed 6. Class, Generic, and Specialization Talents share the same trapezoid.

## 4. Character Battlefield Point Values

The 100-point starting base is a **floor**, not an extra cost added to talent progression. Once a PC has spent at least 100 XP on Talents, **replace** the base with the total XP spent. Unspent XP is not included.

> **Final PC Points = max(100, XP spent on Talents) + currently equipped Equipment Points + other explicitly additional Point costs.**

| XP spent | XP unspent | Equipped items | PC Point Value |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 0 | 20 | 120 |
| 45 | 15 | 20 | 120 |
| 100 | 0 | 20 | 120 |
| 125 | 25 | 20 | 145 |
| 250 | 0 | 20 | 270 |

XP-funded Talent effects are **not charged again** individually above the base. Equipment does **not** cost XP; acquire, replace, or remove it through play, adjusting Point Value to match the equipment that currently counts. Additional known Spell Attacks and Animal Companions use their explicitly assigned Point costs. See [Equipment and Crafting](11_equipment_and_crafting.md) and [Talents](13_talents.md).

This avoids making a fully developed 250-XP PC cost 350 points **before equipment**, which would crowd other Units out of a campaign army. It does not guarantee balance against ordinary Units; the updated Attack profiles remain provisional for testing.
