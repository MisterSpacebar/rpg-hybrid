# Character Creation

Player Characters use the same underlying rules as other Units and may participate directly in Structured Combat. A newly created PC has **100 base battlefield Points**, plus equipped items and any other explicitly additional costs. The base is **replaced** by XP spent on Talents only once that XP expenditure reaches 100; it is never added on top of the 100-point base.

**PC Point Value = max(100, XP spent on Talents) + Equipment Points + other explicit Points.** Unspent XP does not increase Point Value. See [Experience and Advancement](03_experience_and_advancement.md) for examples and the 250-XP Talent structure.

## Step 1 — Choose an Ancestry

Ancestry determines the Character's:

- Starting Characteristics.
- Size.
- Ancestry Traits.
- Any additional features specified by that Ancestry.

### Size and Model Bases

| Size | Typical Model Base |
| --- | --- |
| Small | 18–25 mm |
| Medium | 28–32 mm |
| Large | 40 mm or larger |

Model base sizes are approximate guidelines rather than strict requirements. Particularly unusual creatures may use different bases where appropriate. Size and any associated effects are determined by the Character's Ancestry or Unit Profile, not solely by the miniature's physical base.

## Step 2 — Choose a Class

Class determines the Character's basic combat capabilities and areas of expertise.

Each Class provides:

- A starting Combat Profile.
- A Class Ability.
- Starting equipment options.
- Access to Class Talents.
- A selection of associated Skills.

Choose two Skills from the Character's Class list and improve each from 6+ to 5+.

| Class | Associated Skills |
| --- | --- |
| Warrior | Athletics, Survival, Awareness, Influence |
| Rogue | Stealth, Awareness, Craft, Influence |
| Mage | Mysticism, Lore, Craft, Awareness |
| Priest | Medicine, Mysticism, Influence, Lore |
| **Artificer** | **Craft, Lore, Awareness, Mysticism** |

These Skills represent the Character's initial professional training. A Character may develop Skills outside their Class through Character Creation and later advancement.

## Step 3 — Choose a Subclass

Subclass represents the Character's specialization within their Class.

Each Subclass provides:

- One Signature Command Ability, or an equivalent always-prepared Subclass feature (such as the Artificer's Field Specialty).
- Access to Subclass Talents.
- Any specialized weapons, Spell Attacks, or additional features listed in its description.

### Signature Commands

Each Character receives one Signature Command from their Subclass. An **Artificer instead selects one known Formula as its Field Specialty**, which is always prepared and serves the same function. A Signature Command or Field Specialty does not occupy one of the Character's four optional prepared Command slots. Characters may prepare up to four additional Command Abilities acquired through Talents or other rules. Signature Commands follow the normal Command Ability rules unless specifically stated otherwise.

## Step 4 — Choose Equipment

Choose starting weapons and other equipment from the options identified by the Character’s Class and Subclass, or other options allowed by the GM. Use the **complete PC Attack profile and listed Point cost** in [Equipment and Crafting](11_equipment_and_crafting.md); the Class does not change its printed Attacks, Hit, Damage, or Keywords simply by selecting it. A Standard weapon is a recommendation, **not free equipment**. Add its listed equipment Points unless an explicit rule says Included. Starting Spell Attacks granted by Class/Subclass are found in [Starting Spell Attacks](06_spells_and_equipment.md) and are included in the 100-point base unless specifically marked as optional/paid. **Artificers** instead select starting weapons and two learned Formulas from their chosen Subclass; the Formulas are included Class abilities, while equipped weapons add their normal Points. See [Artificer Formulas](18_artificer_formulas.md). Acquired Spell Attacks use the profiles and Points in [Spell Catalogue](12_spell_catalogue.md).

### Attacks and Combat Phases

Characters may possess Attacks usable during different phases of Structured Combat:

- Assault Phase: Ranged Attacks and other eligible Assault abilities.
- Combat Phase: Melee Attacks and other eligible Combat abilities.

Characters are not required to possess Attacks for both phases. A Warrior may begin with only melee weapons, while a Ranger or another versatile Character may possess both ranged and melee options. The Character may only make Attacks during phases permitted by the relevant Attack Profile. Spell Attacks and other learned Attacks follow the same phase restrictions as ordinary Attack Profiles. Spell Attacks do not occupy prepared Command slots. Spell-based Command Abilities do occupy those slots unless they are Signature Commands or another rule specifies otherwise.

## Step 5 — Choose Additional Skills

Choose three Skills that were not improved in Step 2. Improve each selected Skill by one step, normally from 6+ to 5+. These additional Skills represent the Character's background, personal interests, and experience outside their primary Class.

After completing normal Character Creation, most Characters will possess:

- Two Class Skills at 5+.
- Three additional Skills at 5+.
- All remaining Skills at 6+.

No Skill may begin better than 4+ through Character Creation unless a specific rule grants an exception. Further Skill improvements are obtained through Talents and other advancements.

## Step 6 — Finalize the Character

Record the Character's completed Profile, including:

- Ancestry, Class, and Subclass.
- Characteristics, Skills, and Size.
- Combat Profile and Class Ability.
- Ancestry Traits.
- Signature Command or Field Specialty.
- Equipment and Attack Profiles.
- Prepared Command Abilities, if any.
- Final Point Value.

The Character now also gains the Hero tag. The Character is now ready for play.
