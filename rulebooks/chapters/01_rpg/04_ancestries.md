# Ancestries

Ancestry determines a Character's starting Characteristics, Size, and Ancestry Traits. Ancestry Traits apply in addition to the Character's Class and Subclass features.

## Starting Characteristics

| Ancestry | Body | Agility | Mind | Spirit | Size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | M |
| Dwarf | 4 | 2 | 3 | 4 | M |
| Elf | 2 | 4 | 4 | 2 | M |
| Orc | 4 | 3 | 2 | 3 | L |
| Halfling | 2 | 4 | 2 | 4 | S |
| Gnome | 2 | 3 | 4 | 3 | S |
| Goblin | 2 | 4 | 3 | 3 | S |
| Tiefling | 2 | 2 | 4 | 4 | M |
| Beastfolk | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | S–L |
| Kobold | 2 | 4 | 3 | 2 | S |
| Undead | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | S–L |

## Human — Adaptable

At Character Creation, increase one Characteristic from 3 to 4.

## Dwarf — Stout

Gain +1 Wound.

## Elf — Graceful

Gain +1" Move.

## Orc — Ferocious

When a melee Attack Surges, that Attack gains Critical 1.

## Halfling — Fortunate

Once per session, reroll one natural 1.

## Gnome — Ingenious

Once per session, before making a Mind-based Check, gain +1D6.

## Goblin — Scurry

After completely Evading an Attack, Move up to 1".

## Tiefling — Otherworldly

Gain Ward 6+ against Magic Attacks.

## Beastfolk

Beastfolk begin with Body 3, Agility 3, Mind 3, and Spirit 3. At Character Creation, choose **one Adaptation** from the following list.

| Adaptation | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Scaled Skin | Gain Tough 1. |
| Aquatic | Ignore swimming movement penalties and remain underwater indefinitely. |
| Climber | Treat vertical terrain as normal movement unless a specific Check is required. |
| Leaper | Ignore the first 2" of Difficult Terrain during each Move. |
| Keen Senses | Improve Awareness by 1 step. |
| Predator | Improve Stealth by 1 step. |
| Tracker | Improve Survival by 1 step. |
| Sure-Footed | Improve Athletics by 1 step. |
| Herbal Instinct | Improve Medicine by 1 step. |
| Spirit-Touched | Improve Mysticism by 1 step. |

## Kobold — Pack Fighter

Weapon Attacks gain Accurate +1 against enemies Engaged with another friendly Unit.

## Undead

All Undead gain **Deathless** and choose one **Undead Archetype**.

**Deathless:** Undead do not need to breathe, eat, drink, or sleep and are immune to ordinary disease.

### Skeleton

Gain Tough 1 against ranged Weapon Attacks.

### Zombie

The first time each session you would be reduced to 0 Wounds, make a Hard Body + Survival Check. On success, remain at X Wounds, where X is your Body Characteristic.

### Spirit

Gain Ward 6+ against non-Magic Weapon Attacks.

### Vampire

Once per Round, when you destroy an enemy Unit with an Melee Attack, Heal 1.

## v0.7 Ancestry and Creature Tags

At Character Creation, PCs gain the **Character** designation and their selected **ancestry tag**. Most living Humanoid ancestries also gain **Humanoid, Mortal** (for example Human, Elf, Dwarf, Orc, Goblin, Gnome, Halfling and Kobold). This does not automatically make Beastfolk **Beast** or Tieflings **Infernal**. Undead ancestry adds **Undead**; its Spirit archetype additionally gains **Spirit**. Physical/creature classification and faction allegiance are independent. Armor grants **Armored** only when an explicit equipment upgrade says so. Ancestry tags alone grant no new saves, damage, or recruitment slots.
