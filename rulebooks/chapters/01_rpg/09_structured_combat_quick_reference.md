# Structured Combat — Quick Reference

- Alternating Unit Activations • Exact Movement • Tactical Objectives

### Battle Setup

- **Staggered deployment:** Half of purchased Units, **rounded up**, must begin in Reserve (count Units, not Points or Models); the rest deploy wholly in friendly Deployment Zones. At least one Headquarters deploys normally if present.
- **Reinforcement allowance:** Beginning in Round 1, each player may bring in up to **the current Round number** of Reserve Units (R1: 1; R2: 2; R3: 3; R4: 4; R5: 5). Unused allowances do not carry over.

### Round Sequence

- Priority: Determine first Movement activation.
- Movement: Alternate Unit activations.
- Reserves: Deploy reinforcements at the end of Movement (up to the Round allowance).
- Assault: Alternate Unit activations.
- Combat: Alternate eligible Unit activations.
- Scoring: Resolve objectives and Victory Points.
- End Step: Place designated Deep Strike arrivals after Scoring, then expire effects and advance the Round.
- Reserve deployment is an end-of-Movement procedure, not a separate combat Phase.

### Priority

- Movement: Side with fewer VP chooses First Activation.
- Tied VP: Each Player rolls 1D6; GM rolls 1D6 per PC. Higher total chooses. Reroll ties.
- Assault and Combat: First side Finished in the preceding Phase chooses who activates first next.
- A side becomes Finished when all eligible Units activate or it forfeits remaining activations.

### Movement

- Alternate Unit activations.
Each Unit may choose one eligible action:
- Move up to M.
- Engage an enemy.
- Disengage.
- Use eligible Movement abilities.
- Remain stationary.
- Combat Skill Action (1 CP): A Character may attempt one ordinary Skill Check during its Movement activation. Maximum once per Character per Movement Phase.
- Engaged: Enemy Models within 1".
- Coherency: Models remain within 3" of another Model in the same connected Unit.
- Difficult Terrain: Every 1" costs 2" of Move.
- Withdrawn: Cannot Charge, Assault Attack, voluntarily Engage, or control objectives. May move and use non-Attack abilities; can fight in Combat if Engaged again.

### Reserve Deployment

- **All armies:** Beginning in Round 1, deploy up to **Round number** Reserve Units per player at the end of Movement; **no banking** unused arrivals. All arrivals share this limit unless expressly additional.
- **Normal arrival:** Deploy wholly within friendly Deployment Zones, legally coherent and outside Engagement. Ordinary Reserves cannot enter via battlefield edges without an explicit special rule.
- **Order:** Alternate Reserve placements, starting with the side that held Movement First Activation; Assault Priority was already determined.
- **Arrival limits:** Arriving Units count as having Moved and as having completed their Movement activation. No Movement phase actions, Combat Skill Actions, or Charges that Round; no Heavy unless explicitly waived.
- **After arrival:** Eligible Assault Move, ranged/Spell Attack, Combat activation, and Reactions remain possible.
- **Deep Strike:** From Round 2, designate during Movement, then insert **after Scoring in the End Step**; counts toward the normal arrival allowance, maximum **one insertion per Round**. Normal insertion more than 8" from enemies (flank/beacon more than 6"); act from the following Round.
- **Alpha Strike — Rapid Deployment:** Once per battle in Round 1, one **additional** Reserve Unit may deploy through a Command during its normal Movement activation opportunity, then Move and Charge normally. This is outside the regular allowance, but does not waive Heavy or grant an extra activation.
- Units still in Reserve cannot act or control objectives; undeployed Units are not automatically Defeated at battle end.

### Assault

Each Unit chooses **one** ordinary Assault action: **Assault Move (up to Move)**, **ranged Weapon/Spell Attack**, or **Charge**. No free Assault Move alongside an Attack. Ordinary Reserve and Deep Strike arrivals cannot Charge that Round; Alpha Strike Rapid Deployment is an explicit exception. Personal CP Commands may be used during that selected Unit's own activation, independently of its chosen action unless an ability specifies otherwise. Faction/Doctrine Commands use the controlling side's priority; Reactions interrupt eligible triggers.

### Charge

- Declare a legal visible target wholly within 12"; the charging Unit cannot already be Engaged or Withdrawn.
- **Ordinary Units:** roll 2D6, sum both for distance and use the highest for Impact. **PCs:** roll Body + Athletics (Rogues may use Agility + Athletics), use the two highest for distance and highest for Impact, then allocate each Success to +1" distance or +1 Impact. No minimum Success requirement. Charge X still applies; Move is not added.
- Success: move into Engagement; Impact Hits = **highest of the two dice + printed Impact X + other applicable Impact bonuses − Brace X**, minimum 0.
- Failure: stay in place; Assault activation ends.
- Resolve Impact once per charging Unit, not per Model: **Armor → Ward → Damage**, ignoring Evade. Alpha Strike Shock Assault increases Impact Hits, not distance.
- Two natural sixes/ones can Surge/Blunder; successful Charge with Blunder causes Off-Balance. Eligible Chargers can fight in Combat.

### Range Bands

- Some Commands, Talents, and Bestiary entries use **Short / Medium / Long** instead of an exact distance: Short = up to 6", Medium = beyond 6" up to 12", Long = beyond 12". Measure normally.

### Combat

- Alternate eligible Unit activations.
- Each eligible Unit may:
- Make one Melee Attack.
- Use eligible Combat abilities.
- Units must be Engaged or have a special rule permitting their activation.

### Terrain and Keywords

| Situation | Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| Physical Cover | Cover 1 |
| Higher Elevation | Cover 1 against ranged Attacks from below |
| Cover + Elevation | Cover 2 |
| Tough X | Reroll X natural 1s on Armor |
| Cover X | Reroll X natural 1s on Evade |

- Cover X permits Evade against eligible ranged Attacks.
- Applicable Cover X sources stack. Multiple ordinary physical obstacles count as one source.
- Each die may only be rerolled once.
- For multi-model Units, ordinary Cover and elevation apply when at least half the targeted Models qualify.

### Attack Resolution

- Target → Attack Dice → Accurate/other rerolls → Surge/Blunder → Critical conversions → Armor → Evade (if eligible) → Ward → Damage

| Attack | Defenses |
| --- | --- |
| Ranged | Armor → Ward |
| Ranged vs Cover | Armor → Evade (Cover 1) → Ward |
| Ranged vs Higher Elevation | Armor → Evade (Cover 1) → Ward |
| Ranged vs Cover + Elevation | Armor → Evade (Cover 2) → Ward |
| Engaged | Armor → Evade → Ward |
| Impact and delayed automatic Hits | Armor → Ward; no Evade unless expressly allowed |

For radius-based effects that apply to whole Units, a Unit qualifies when **at least half of its current Models** are within the area (round up). Resolve the printed Hits **once per qualifying Unit**, using that effect's normal Saves. See [Area Effects Against Units](10_keywords.md#area-effects-against-units).

- Surge: Two natural 6s.
- Blunder: Two natural 1s.
- Each die may only be rerolled once.
- Printed/passive Critical X requires a Surge; Critical X explicitly granted by a CP-paid Command does not.
- Long Range X subtracts 2 dice from the combined Attack pool at extended range (minimum 1), without worsening Hit.
- Multiple Evade permissions do not provide additional Evade rolls.

### Command Points

- Standard RPG: 2 CP per Player; GM receives **max(2, 2 × PCs − 1) CP**.
- Refresh every Round. No carryover.
- One Command per Unit activation from the acting side (personal, Faction, or Doctrine); one eligible opposing Reaction. An Attack, Charge, or Save sequence may receive at most one friendly Command effect, including earlier prepared effects. Instant cannot be responded to with another Command.

### Scoring and End Step

- Score objectives according to the scenario.
- Default objective control: Eligible Unit within 3".
- More eligible Units controls; equal numbers Contest.
- Defeated, Withdrawn, and undeployed Reserve Units cannot control.
- Resolve End-of-Round effects and expire conditions.
- Begin the next Priority Step.
