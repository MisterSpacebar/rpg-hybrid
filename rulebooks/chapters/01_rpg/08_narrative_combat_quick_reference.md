# Narrative Combat — Quick Reference

- Side-Based Turns • Abstract Range Bands • Theatre of the Mind

### Round Sequence

- Player Turn: Movement → Assault → Combat
- GM Turn: Movement → Assault → Combat
- End of Round: Resolve effects and expire conditions.
- Players normally act first. The GM may act first during the opening Round of an ambush.
- Each Unit acts once per eligible Phase. The acting side chooses the order.

### Deployment and Reserves

- Up to 50% of an army's Point Value may begin in Reserves.
- Reserves enter at the end of their side's Movement Phase through established friendly entry points.
- Deployed Reserves forfeit their Movement activation.
- No Movement abilities or Combat Skill Actions on arrival.
- May act during Assault and Combat.
- Deployment prevents Heavy during that Turn.
- Units still in Reserves cannot act or control objectives.

### Range Bands

| Range | Distance |
| --- | --- |
| Engaged | Melee |
| Short | Up to 6" |
| Medium | 6–12" |
| Long | Beyond 12" |

- Positions are relative to each target.

### Movement

- Move one Range Band or reposition, subject to Move and Slow X.
- Short → Engaged: Normal Engage, no Impact.
- Engaged → Short: Disengage and become Withdrawn.
- Terrain may restrict movement or require Skill Checks.
- Combat Skill Action (1 CP): Attempt one ordinary Skill Check during Movement, such as Stealth, Athletics, Awareness, or Medicine. May accompany normal Movement when appropriate. Once per Character per Movement Phase.
- Withdrawn: Cannot Charge, Assault Attack, voluntarily Engage, or control objectives. May move and use non-Attack abilities; can fight in Combat if Engaged again.

### Assault

Select **one** ordinary action per eligible Unit: **Assault Move** (one Range Band), **Assault Attack** (one ranged Weapon/Spell Attack), or **Charge**. They are mutually exclusive. Eligible **CP Commands** may be used during that Unit's activation without replacing its action unless explicitly required. Heavy requires no voluntary Movement action in the preceding Movement Phase.

### Charge

- Character: Body + Athletics; Simple NPC Unit: 2D6 (4+ Successes).
- **Short → Engaged:** 1 Success. **Medium → Engaged:** 2 Successes and fiction/mobility must permit; Long not normally possible.
- CHARGE X aids narrative reach, but does not automatically add Successes.
- Successful Charge Impact = **highest die rolled + printed Impact X − defender's Brace X** (minimum 0), once per Unit.
- Impact: automatic Physical Hits, Damage 1; **Armor → Ward; no Evade**.
- Failure: No movement; Assault activation ends. Successful Charge Blunder: Off-Balance.
- Charging Unit can attack during its Combat activation if eligible.

### Combat

- Engaged Units make one Melee Attack and may use eligible Combat abilities.
- Non-Engaged Units may act only if a legal Combat ability or special rule permits it.

### Terrain and Keywords

| Situation | Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| Physical Cover | Cover 1 |
| Higher Elevation | Cover 1 against ranged Attacks from below |
| Cover + Elevation | Cover 2 |
| Tough X | Reroll X natural 1s on Armor |
| Cover X | Reroll X natural 1s on Evade |

- Cover X permits Evade against eligible ranged Attacks.
- Applicable Cover X sources stack. Multiple ordinary physical obstacles count as one source. Each die may only be rerolled once.

### Attack Resolution

- Target → Hit dice → Accurate/other rerolls → Surge/Blunder → Critical conversions → Armor → Evade (if eligible) → Ward → Damage

| Attack | Defenses |
| --- | --- |
| Ranged | Armor → Ward |
| Ranged vs Cover | Armor → Evade (Cover 1) → Ward |
| Ranged vs Higher Elevation | Armor → Evade (Cover 1) → Ward |
| Ranged vs Cover + Elevation | Armor → Evade (Cover 2) → Ward |
| Engaged | Armor → Evade → Ward |
| Impact and delayed automatic Hits | Armor → Ward; no Evade unless expressly allowed |

For radius-based effects that apply to whole Units, a Unit qualifies when **at least half of its current Models** are within the area (round up). Resolve the printed Hits **once per qualifying Unit**, using that effect's normal Saves. See [Area Effects Against Units](10_keywords.md#area-effects-against-units).

- Shooting while Engaged: Defender may Evade.
- Shooting into friendly Engagement: Hit worsens one step.
- Pinpoint ignores that penalty.
- Multiple Evade permissions do not provide additional Evade rolls.

### Command Points

- Each Player: 2 CP.
- GM: **max(2, 2 × number of PCs − 1) CP**.
- Refresh every Round. No carryover.
- Personal Commands are used during **that Unit's own activation**; Doctrine/Faction Commands while the controlling side has priority, Reactions on triggers.
- Reactions may interrupt either Turn.

### End of Round

- Resolve End-of-Round effects, remove Withdrawn and Off-Balance, expire temporary conditions, and advance the Round.
- Encounters end when the situation or objectives are resolved.
