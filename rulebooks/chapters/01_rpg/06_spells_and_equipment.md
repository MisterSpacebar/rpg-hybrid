# Starting Spell Attacks

This chapter gives the **authoritative PC profiles for Spell Attacks granted by a Base Class or Subclass**. These identical profiles are repeated in the [Classes](05_classes.md) chapter for ease of character creation. The `A` value is the **final starting number of Attack dice for a PC** before specific Talent, Command, or equipment modifications. The provisional growth rebalance reduced prior A 3 → **2** and A 4 → **2**. The **v5 Damage-output rebalance** (see [Equipment and Crafting](11_equipment_and_crafting.md)) then gave every Damage 1 Starting Spell **+1 Attack**. Starting spells are included in the PC’s base battlefield value unless expressly identified as optional with a separate Point cost.

Spell Attacks do not occupy the four optional prepared Command slots; learnable additional spells are in [Spell Catalogue](12_spell_catalogue.md). Offensive spell abilities still obey their phase and Keyword rules.

## Mage: Base Spells

| Spell | Range | A | Hit | Dmg | Keywords | Cost |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Magic Missile | 6" | 3 | 5+ | 1 | Magic, Pinpoint | Included |
| Force Bolt (optional) | 8" | 3 | 5+ | 1 | Magic, Surge: Push 1" | +10 Points |

All Mages begin with **Magic Missile only**. **Force Bolt** is an optional permanently learned Spell Attack for +10 Points; on Surge, push the target 1" away from the caster, respecting legal placement. Subclass starting spells remain included as printed.

## Mage: Subclass Spells

| Subclass | Spell | Range | A | Hit | Dmg | Keywords | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Elementalist | Fireball | 6" | 3 | 5+ | 1 | Magic, Fire, Blast 1" | Included (choose one) |
| Elementalist | Spark | 6" | 3 | 6+ | 1 | Magic, Lightning, Surge: Instant | Included (choose one) |
| Elementalist | Ice Shard | 6" | 3 | 5+ | 1 | Magic, Frost, Surge: Slow 2 | Included (choose one) |
| Necromancer | Life Drain | 4" | 3 | 5+ | 1 | Magic, On Damage: Heal 1 | Included |
| Illusionist | Mind Spike | 6" | 3 | 5+ | 1 | Magic, Surge: Weaken Hit | Included |
| Illusionist | Psychic Blade | Engaged | 3 | 5+ | 1 | Magic | **10 Points (optional)** |

- **Elementalist:** Choose one subclass spell at creation. Fireball follows Blast; Spark gains Instant only on Surge; Ice Shard applies Slow 2 on Surge.
- **Necromancer:** Life Drain Heals 1 Damage to the caster if it deals Damage, once per Attack.
- **Illusionist:** Mind Spike worsens the target's next Hit target by one step, if the Attack occurs this Round. Psychic Blade may be acquired separately and is used during the Combat Phase.

## Priest: Base Spells

| Spell | Range | A | Hit | Dmg | Keywords | Cost |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Radiant Bolt | 6" | 3 | 5+ | 1 | Magic, Radiant | Included |
| Sacred Brand | 3" | 3 | 6+ | 1 | Magic, Surge: Accurate 1 | Included |

All Priests know both. **Sacred Brand:** If the initial post-reroll Hit dice show a Surge, the current Attack gains Accurate 1 and may immediately reroll one natural 1 that has not already been rerolled. This is an exception to the usual order because its Accurate bonus is granted by the Surge itself. Apply any resulting Critical conversions afterward.

## Priest: Subclass Spells

| Subclass | Spell | Range | A | Hit | Dmg | Keywords | Cost |

| Cleric | Smite | 6" | 3 | 6+ | 1 | Magic, Radiant, Anti-Undead 1 | Included |

- **Smite:** Anti-Undead 1 adds one Attack die against Undead.
- **Thorn Lash:** on Surge, applies Slow 2 to the target's next Movement activation.
- **Omen Bolt:** on Surge, enemies cannot react to that Attack, as for Instant.

## Elemental Attributes and Assault Timing

The extra Fire, Frost, Lightning, Radiant and Nature descriptors refer to the shared [Attack Types and Elemental Attributes](10_keywords.md#attack-types-and-elemental-attributes) rules; they do not change Saves on their own. A Fire spell suppresses an applicable creature’s regeneration only if **unsaved Damage** is actually inflicted. Casting a ranged Spell Attack is the Unit's **one ordinary Assault action**, rather than a free Attack accompanying an Assault Move. Eligible Assault Commands cost CP separately.


## Artificer Formulas Are Not Starting Spell Attacks

Artificer Subclasses receive **Formulas**, not the Mage/Priest starting Spell Attacks listed here. Formulas are CP-powered Commands found in [Artificer Formulas](18_artificer_formulas.md). Their printed Formula Attack profiles, when applicable, are also repeated in the [Artificer Class](05_classes.md#artificer) for ease of selection.
