# MATCHED PLAY MISSIONS

### Standard Wargame Scenarios — Revised Draft v2 (pre-layout reconciliation)

## 1. Overview

Matched Play uses standardized battlefield sizes, army-building rules, and victory conditions for competitive battles.

A Matched Play game consists of:

1. Two armies built to the agreed Points limit.
2. One Battlefield Layout.
3. One Matched Play Mission.
4. Alternating terrain placement.
5. Five Rounds of Structured Combat.

The Battlefield Layout determines deployment zones and objective-marker positions.

The Mission determines how Victory Points (VP) are scored.

### 1.1 Standard Game Size

| **Format** | **Army Points per Side** | **Battlefield** | **Objectives** |
| ---------- | ------------------------ | --------------- | -------------- |
| Small      | 1,000                    | 36" × 36"       | 3              |
| Standard   | 2,000                    | 36" × 60"       | 5              |
| Grand      | 3,000                    | 48" × 72"       | 7              |

The standard format is **2,000 Points, five objectives, and five Rounds**.

The eight Missions below are written for this format. Smaller and larger battles may use the scaling guidelines at the end of the chapter.

## 2. Setting Up a Battle

Resolve setup in the following order:

1. Agree on the army Points limit.
2. Select a Battlefield Layout.
3. Select a Matched Play Mission.
4. Place objective markers at the layout's designated positions.
5. Establish the terrain pool and alternate terrain placement.
6. Determine which player uses each deployment zone.
7. Declare Reserves and deploy armies.
8. Begin Round 1.

### Layout Selection

Standard competitive games may use any of the nine numbered M-series layouts, including an agreed alternate version.

If selecting a layout randomly, roll D10 and reroll 10.

If the players use an alternative variant, such as M2 Staggered Front v2, agree on that variant before rolling or drawing the layout.

### Mission Selection

Roll D8 to determine the Mission, or agree on a Mission before army deployment.

The selected Mission applies equally to both players.

### Objective Affiliation for Missions

Where a Mission distinguishes friendly-affiliated, enemy-affiliated, and Neutral objectives, determine their affiliation **before deployment** using the chosen layout. Each side receives the same number of affiliated objectives; for the standard five-objective game, designate two for Side A, two for Side B, and one Neutral. Three-objective games designate one per side and one Neutral; seven-objective games designate three per side and one Neutral. Where the layout lacks explicit affiliation labels, assign each objective to the side with the nearer deployment zone, then resolve ambiguous placements by mutual agreement or a roll-off while retaining equal numbers. Record these assignments at setup; they do not change when control changes. Layouts with numbered objectives may use their printed affiliations when supplied.

## 3. Objective Control

Unless a Mission states otherwise, an objective is controlled according to the Structured Combat rules.

A Unit contributes to control if at least one eligible Model is within 3" of the objective.

Each eligible Unit contributes one to its side's control total, regardless of its number of Models.

The side with more eligible Units in range controls the objective.

If both sides have equal eligible Units in range, the objective is **Contested** and neither side controls it.

Downed, Withdrawn, and Reserve Units cannot control objectives.

A Unit may count toward only one objective during the same Scoring Phase. If it is within range of multiple objectives, its controlling player chooses which one it contributes to.

## 4. Victory Point Scoring

### Primary VP

Unless a Mission explicitly replaces Primary scoring, each player scores:

**1 Primary VP for each objective controlled at the end of the Scoring Phase.**

A player may score up to 5 **ordinary objective-control Primary VP** per Round in the standard format. Mission-specific one-time bonuses (such as Relic deliveries) are additional only when explicitly stated.

### Tactical VP

Each Mission also provides one Tactical Objective.

A player fulfilling that condition during the Round gains **1 Tactical VP**.

A player may earn no more than 1 Tactical VP per Round, even if the condition is satisfied multiple times.

### Scoring Schedule

Rounds proceed normally through:

Priority → Movement → Assault → Combat → Scoring → End Step.

Objective control and Mission conditions are evaluated during the Scoring Phase.

**VP scoring begins in Round 2.**

Round 1 establishes battlefield control but awards no VP.

Conditions referring to the previous Round may use the objective-control state recorded during Round 1.

### Maximum Ordinary Scoring

| **Source**         | **Maximum VP per Round** |
| ------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Primary Objectives | 5                        |
| Tactical Objective | 1                        |
| **Total**          | **6**                    |

## 5. Victory Conditions

A normal battle lasts five Rounds.

At the end of Round 5, the player with more VP wins.

If both players have equal VP, the result is a Draw.

### Decisive Victory

Beginning in Round 2, a player immediately wins if they finish a Scoring Phase with a VP lead of **10 or more**.

This is checked after all Primary and Tactical VP for that Round have been awarded.

A Decisive Victory ends the battle regardless of the remaining Rounds.

### Army Destruction

Destroying the opposing army does not automatically award additional VP.

If one army has no remaining eligible Units, continue resolving any remaining scoring necessary to determine victory, or end the battle immediately if no further VP can be earned by that army and the winner is mathematically certain.

# 6. Matched Play Mission List

| **D8** | **Mission**        | **Tactical Focus**                          |
| ------ | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| 1      | Domination         | Capture and recapture territory             |
| 2      | Frontlines         | Advance into enemy positions                |
| 3      | Breakthrough       | Push Units through contested objectives     |
| 4      | Recover the Relics | Collect and transport objectives            |
| 5      | Supply Requisition | Gather supplies while controlling territory |
| 6      | Sabotage           | Disable enemy objectives                    |
| 7      | Seek and Destroy   | Defeat opposing formations                  |
| 8      | Arcane Supremacy   | Attune and defend magical sites             |

## MP1 — DOMINATION

*Capture and Recapture*

### Primary Objective

Score 1 VP for each controlled objective during every scoring Round.

### Tactical Objective — Seize the Initiative

Gain 1 Tactical VP if you control at least one objective that your opponent controlled at the end of the previous Round.

The objective must have changed from enemy control to your control.

Taking a previously Neutral or Contested objective does not satisfy this condition unless the opponent controlled it during the preceding Round.

### Special Rules

Record objective control at the end of Round 1, even though no VP is awarded.

From Round 2 onward, compare each objective's current control with its recorded control from the previous Round.

### Design Notes

Domination rewards aggressive territorial play and counterattacks.

It is the simplest Matched Play Mission and serves as the baseline for evaluating army balance across different layouts.



---



## MP2 — FRONTLINES

*Advance and Occupy*

### Primary Objective

Score 1 VP for each controlled objective during every scoring Round.

### Tactical Objective — Push Forward

Gain 1 Tactical VP if you control at least one enemy-affiliated objective.

For this Mission, an objective is enemy-affiliated to the opposing side as assigned during setup. Each side has two enemy-affiliated objectives at 2,000 Points and one Neutral objective.

### Special Rules

Objective affiliation is fixed during setup and recorded on the layout. Once assigned, it is independent of which side currently controls the objective or which force is physically nearer.

### Design Notes

Frontlines rewards offensive movement and forces players to commit Units beyond their safest positions.

It is especially useful with diagonal, split, or staggered layouts.

## MP3 — BREAKTHROUGH

*Escape Through Enemy Positions*

### Primary Objective — Breach the Line

This Mission replaces ordinary objective-control scoring.

Each side must move its Units through the opponent's designated breakthrough points.

Each side treats its **enemy-affiliated objectives** as its Breakthrough Points. At 2,000 Points there are two such points for each side; the central Neutral objective cannot be a Breakthrough Point.

An eligible Unit may **Break Through** if:

- It is within 3" of one of its assigned Breakthrough Points.
- Its side controls that objective during the Scoring Phase.
- The Unit is not Engaged, Downed, or Withdrawn.
- The Unit has not previously scored through Breakthrough.

When a Unit Breaks Through, remove it from the battlefield.

It counts as having successfully escaped, not as being defeated.

Each successfully escaped Unit awards **2 Primary VP**.

A player may earn up to the format’s normal **Primary VP cap per Scoring Phase** from Breakthrough (5 VP in a standard 2,000-point game). If multiple Units escape simultaneously, they all leave the battlefield, but only up to that format’s Primary VP cap is awarded that Phase. The escaped Units are not Defeated and cannot return to play.

Units that have escaped no longer participate in the battle.

### Tactical Objective — Successful Penetration

Gain 1 Tactical VP if at least one friendly Unit Breaks Through during the current Scoring Phase.

### Special Rules

The same Unit cannot score more than once.

A Unit that Breaks Through cannot return through Reserves or other ordinary redeployment rules.

A player may Break Through with multiple eligible Units at the same controlled objective, subject to the per-Round VP limit.

### Design Notes

Breakthrough rewards maneuvering and reaching enemy positions rather than holding territory indefinitely.

Using objective markers as Breakthrough Points makes it compatible with layouts where the armies do not begin along conventional opposing board edges.

## MP4 — RECOVER THE RELICS

*Collect and Deliver*

### Primary Objective

Score 1 VP for each controlled objective during every scoring Round.

### Setup

Place one Relic marker at each of the five objective markers.

Each Relic is a separate Scenario Object.

The objective marker remains in place if its Relic is removed.

### Special Rules — Recover Relic

An eligible unengaged Unit within 1" of an unclaimed Relic may perform a Recover Mission Action in **Movement or Assault**, replacing its normal action in that phase.

The Unit becomes the Relic's carrier.

A carrier:

- May carry only one Relic.
- May move normally.
- Cannot Charge.
- Drops the Relic if defeated.
- May voluntarily drop the Relic during its activation.

A Relic may be recovered from the ground by either side using the same Mission Action.

### Special Rules — Deliver Relic

A carrier that ends its Movement or Assault Move wholly within its friendly Deployment Zone may deliver its Relic.

Remove the Relic from play.

Each Relic can be delivered only once.

**Relic Delivery Points:** Each successfully delivered Relic awards **2 bonus Primary VP** once. A player may score at most **4 delivery VP per Round**. These finite delivery VP are tracked separately from the ordinary objective-control Primary VP cap, and are awarded when delivery is resolved (normally during Movement or Assault). They count toward the 10-VP Decisive Victory check at the end of that Round's Scoring Phase; do not check the lead mid-Phase. A delivery made in Round 1 is recorded but does not award VP until the Round 2 Scoring Phase; for Tactical scoring only, it counts as delivered during Round 2. This preserves the universal no-scoring Round 1 rule without penalizing early delivery.

### Tactical Objective — Secure an Artifact

Gain 1 Tactical VP if you deliver at least one Relic during the current Round.

### Design Notes

Recover the Relics creates a tradeoff between holding objectives and carrying valuable objects away from the front.

The Relics do not replace the normal capture objectives, so players must balance territorial scoring against extraction.

## MP5 — SUPPLY REQUISITION

*Gather and Hold*

### Primary Objective

Score 1 VP for each controlled objective during every scoring Round.

### Special Rules — Gather Supplies

Each objective also represents a Supply Location.

An eligible unengaged Unit within 1" of an objective may perform a Gather Supplies Mission Action in **Movement or Assault**, replacing its normal action in that phase.

Place a Supply token belonging to that side at the objective.

Supply tokens last until the end of the current Round.

Each side may place at most one Supply token at each objective per Round.

### Tactical Objective — Secure the Supplies

Gain 1 Tactical VP if, during Scoring, you control at least one objective containing your Supply token.

Remove all Supply tokens during the End Step.

### Design Notes

Supply Requisition requires a Unit to devote its Assault activation to an objective interaction.

It rewards controlling ground while performing logistical tasks, rather than merely standing within control range.



---



## MP6 — SABOTAGE

*Disrupt Enemy Infrastructure*

### Primary Objective

Score 1 VP for each controlled objective during every scoring Round, except objectives disabled by Sabotage during that Round.

### Special Rules — Sabotage Objective

An eligible unengaged Unit within 1" of an enemy-affiliated objective may perform a Sabotage Mission Action in **Movement or Assault**, replacing its normal action in that phase.

Place a Disabled token on that objective.

A side may Sabotage an **enemy-affiliated objective** as assigned at setup. The Neutral objective cannot be Sabotaged.

### Disabled Objectives

A Disabled objective awards no Primary VP to either player during the current Scoring Phase.

The objective may still be controlled, contested, or interacted with for other applicable effects.

Remove all Disabled tokens during the End Step.

### Tactical Objective — Successful Sabotage

Gain 1 Tactical VP if you successfully disabled at least one objective during the current Round.

### Design Notes

Sabotage rewards infiltrating enemy positions and temporarily denying territorial scoring.

Because disabling an objective denies scoring to both sides, a successful Sabotage action must be timed carefully.



---



## MP7 — SEEK AND DESTROY

*Target Enemy Formations*

### Primary Objective

Score 1 VP for each controlled objective during every scoring Round.

### Tactical Objective — Eliminate Opposition

Gain 1 Tactical VP if at least one enemy Unit was defeated during the current Round.

A multi-model Unit counts as defeated when its last Model is removed or defeated.

A Player Character or other single-model Character counts as defeated for this condition when it becomes Downed.

### Special Rules

Defeating additional Units during the same Round does not award additional Tactical VP.

Units escaping the battlefield or returning to Reserves are not considered defeated.

### Design Notes

Seek and Destroy encourages active combat while preserving the importance of controlling objectives.

It is especially suitable for armies designed around decisive engagements and aggressive assaults.



---



## MP8 — ARCANE SUPREMACY

*Attune and Control*

### Primary Objective — Magical Control

This Mission modifies ordinary objective scoring.

Each objective begins Unattuned.

A player scores 1 Primary VP for each objective that is both:

- Controlled by their side.
- Attuned to their side.

An Unattuned objective awards no VP, even if controlled.

### Special Rules — Attune Objective

An eligible unengaged Unit within 1" of an objective may perform an Attune Mission Action in **Movement or Assault**, replacing its normal action in that phase.

Place that side's Attunement token on the objective.

Attunement persists until an opposing Unit successfully Attunes the same objective.

Each objective can be Attuned to only one side at a time.

Attuning an objective does not automatically grant control over it.

### Tactical Objective — Establish Attunement

Gain 1 Tactical VP if you successfully Attuned at least one objective during the current Round and control that objective during the Scoring Phase.

### Design Notes

Arcane Supremacy requires players to commit Units to establishing control before collecting VP.

A side may hold an Attuned objective without controlling it temporarily, but only the side matching the Attunement can score from it.

The objective's magical state creates a persistent strategic consideration beyond ordinary occupation.

# 7. Mission Actions

Certain Missions permit interactions called **Mission Actions**. A Unit may make **at most one Mission Action per Round**, in **either its Movement activation or its Assault activation**. A Mission Action completely replaces the Unit's **ordinary action in that selected phase**; it does not replace Commands and does not consume CP unless a particular mission says otherwise.

- **Movement Mission Action:** Select an eligible Unit for Movement activation; perform the legal interaction instead of moving, Disengaging, or taking another ordinary Movement action during that activation. It may still perform a legal ordinary Assault action later in the Round.
- **Assault Mission Action:** Select an eligible Unit for Assault activation; perform the interaction instead of Assault Moving, attacking, or charging. It cannot combine the interaction with any other ordinary Assault action in the same activation.
- **Combat:** No ordinary Mission Actions. Combat activations belong to eligible Engaged Units, and Mission Actions do not occur in Combat.

Unless a Mission states otherwise, the Unit must be **unengaged**, have at least one eligible Model within **1"** of the object or objective, and satisfy any interaction restriction. **Withdrawn Units cannot perform Assault Mission Actions**. Mission Actions are not Attacks and do not automatically require Skill checks. A Unit may perform ordinary eligible Commands with normal timing and CP cost during its activation unless a specific ability says otherwise. A Command never grants an additional ordinary action implicitly.

**Character Skill Alternatives:** An explicit mission may allow an eligible Character to attempt an interaction through a **Movement-phase Skill Action costing 1 CP**; this replaces that Character's ordinary Movement action and uses the mission's stated Difficulty (Normal / 2 successes unless specified). The Character must still be in legal interaction range unless the mission expressly grants a remote action. A Character cannot count an interaction twice by using both its Skill Alternative and an ordinary Mission Action in the same Round.
## 8. Scoring and Timing Clarifications

### Previous-Round Control

When a Mission compares objective control with the previous Round, record objective control at the end of every Scoring Phase, including Round 1.

### Simultaneous Scoring

Both players determine Primary and Tactical VP for the same Scoring Phase before checking for Decisive Victory.

### Multiple Objectives

A Unit may contribute to only one objective's control during the same Scoring Phase.

A single Unit may nevertheless carry a Relic or possess another scenario token while controlling an eligible objective, provided it is not Withdrawn or otherwise ineligible.

### Tokens and Scenario Objects

Scenario tokens and markers do not block Model movement unless their specific rules say otherwise.

A dropped object should be represented by a flat marker where practical.

## 9. Optional Format Scaling

For nonstandard army sizes, the recommended adjustments are:

| **Format** | **Objectives** | **Maximum Primary VP/Round** | **Maximum Tactical VP/Round** | **Decisive Victory Lead** |
| ---------- | -------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| 1,000      | 3              | 3                            | 1                             | 6 VP                      |
| 2,000      | 5              | 5                            | 1                             | 10 VP                     |
| 3,000      | 7              | 7                            | 1                             | 14 VP                     |

For Missions using objective affiliation, assign an equal number of objectives to the two sides before deployment and designate the remaining central or otherwise agreed objective Neutral. Do not assume fixed numbering across different layouts.

For Missions requiring physical markers, use the same number of objects as objectives unless otherwise stated.

## 10. Matched Play Design Principles

Matched Play Missions are intended to:

1. Use the same Structured Combat rules as Campaign battles.
2. Function across different Standard Battlefield Layouts.
3. Reward objective control and meaningful tactical decisions.
4. Avoid excessive bookkeeping or additional turn sequences.
5. Provide ways for disadvantaged armies to recover through aggressive play.
6. Keep mission-specific bonuses small relative to Primary scoring.
7. Preserve meaningful differences between army mobility, durability, and offensive power.
8. Produce a decisive result within approximately five Rounds.

The eight Missions provide distinct scoring incentives without requiring separate army-building or combat systems.
