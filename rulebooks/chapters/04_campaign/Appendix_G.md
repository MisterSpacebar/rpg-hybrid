# APPENDIX G — EXPLORATION AND DISCOVERIES

## Campaign Mode v4.3 — revised

Exploration Sites represent ruins, lost settlements, forgotten military installations, unusual resources, and other locations worth investigating.

A successfully resolved Site normally awards one Discovery reward.

The Discovery deck contains twelve cards, with each providing a distinct possible result.



---



# G.1 Exploration Procedure

## G.1.1 Selecting a Site

The company may investigate an eligible unresolved Site through Explore or another explicitly qualifying mission.

The site must be reachable or accessible through appropriate narrative circumstances.

Political ownership of a hex does not automatically resolve a Site within it.

An expedition may involve skill checks, combat, investigation, negotiation, or other RPG challenges.

## G.1.2 Successful Exploration

When a Site is successfully resolved:

1. Complete the Site's narrative objective.
2. Draw one eligible Discovery card.
3. Apply its reward to the company.
4. Record any permanent resource, military, political, or map effect.
5. Mark the Site as resolved.

Each Site normally awards its Discovery only once.

If a Site specifies a unique reward instead of a generic Discovery, follow its stated instructions.

## G.1.3 Explore Supremacy

When an Explore Order with Supremacy awards a Discovery:

- Reveal two eligible Discovery cards.
- Choose one.
- Return the unchosen card to the available pool and randomize the remaining pool.

If only one card remains available, take that card normally.

If a Site has a fixed named reward rather than a random Discovery, Supremacy does not duplicate that unique reward. The mission may instead provide additional information appropriate to the Site.

## G.1.4 Discovery Cards and Depletion

Draw Discovery cards without replacement.

Resolved unique Discoveries should not be duplicated while unused Discoveries remain.

If every eligible Discovery has already been claimed, a further Site may award an appropriate site-specific substitute, normally 2 Gold, unless the GM established another reward before the expedition began.

This is particularly relevant when exceptional missions resolve more Sites than the normal annual Explore limit permits.

## G.1.5 Discovery Rewards and Ownership

Gold and permanently acquired equipment become part of the company's assets.

Military formations enter the Owned Roster, including reserves.

Resource discoveries are recorded on the strategic map.

Agreements and political contacts must identify their location or institution.

No Discovery automatically changes political control unless it explicitly establishes and legally resolves a territorial claim.

# G.2 Discovery Cards

## G-D1 — Buried Arsenal

**Discovery Type:** Military Cache

The expedition uncovers a buried weapons store, forgotten armory, or military equipment cache.

**Reward:** Choose one:

- Receive a one-time **3-Gold recruitment credit** toward one eligible Core or Regular formation; or
- Receive one cache of ordinary military equipment worth up to **75 equipment Points**, distributed among eligible Company characters or formations.

For the formation option, immediately select one legal formation and apply the credit to its ordinary recruitment Gold cost (1 Gold per 25 formation Points, rounded up). Pay any remaining Gold when claiming the Discovery; unused credit is lost. This is a qualifying one-time acquisition and does **not** consume a Recruit Order. Its full Points value still counts against the deploying PC's detachment ceiling.

The specific formation may come from a normally unavailable regular faction when the arsenal's established contents justify a **one-time, formation-specific access exception**. The reward neither grants an extra slot nor unlocks other formations in that faction's roster.

Equipment uses existing legal profiles, loadout limits and Point accounting. It grants no Talents, XP, bespoke crafting properties or unrestricted exchanges for Gold. The Company gains no additional recruit or Develop action through either option.

## G-D2 — Crystal Vein

**Discovery Type:** Regional Resource

The company discovers a valuable source of construction material.

**Reward:**

- Gain **1 Gold**.
- Reveal one additional **Stone resource source** associated with the explored Site.

Place a discovered Stone source marker on the Site's hex.

The company receives an appropriate extraction right to that source, but must maintain a legal supply connection to use it.

The resource counts toward the normal maximum accessible Stone rating of 3.

It does not transfer political control of the hex.

The discovered marker is additional to the map's original printed resource totals. It remains on the map even if the Company later loses its extraction right or supply connection.

## G-D3 — Hidden Bazaar

**Discovery Type:** Trade and Political Contact

The expedition discovers an unusual trading community, concealed marketplace, or neglected diplomatic network.

**Reward:**

- Reveal one additional **Influence resource source** associated with the Site.
- Establish one appropriate commercial or diplomatic contact there.

Place a discovered Influence resource marker.

The company may access it while the agreement and legal connection remain valid.

The contact may support later Commerce or Politics operations. The discovered resource marker remains on the map if Company access changes or the contact expires.

If the company's affiliated Grand Faction has legal grounds to maintain a representative at the location, it may also place or relocate one of its existing Political Influence markers there, respecting the two-marker limit.

The printed Influence resource and Political Influence marker remain separate mechanics.

## G-D4 — Forgotten Granary

**Discovery Type:** Supply Resource

The expedition recovers an old provisioning facility or discovers a usable food and military supply source.

**Reward:**

- Gain **1 Gold**.
- Reveal one additional **Supply resource source** in the Site's hex.

Place a discovered Supply source marker.

The company gains the right to use it while it maintains a legal supply connection.

The source counts toward the normal maximum accessible Supply rating of 3. The resource marker persists even if Company access is later lost.

The discovery does not automatically construct a Supply Camp or transfer territorial ownership.

## G-D5 — Ancient Observatory

**Discovery Type:** Strategic Intelligence

The party discovers an old observatory, surveying instrument, or repository of distant observations.

**Reward:** Obtain two distinct pieces of useful strategic intelligence.

Each may reveal one of the following:

- The nature of an unresolved Site.
- A concealed military preparation.
- An enemy's named support source.
- An important route or strategic hazard.
- A relevant political relationship.
- A hidden prerequisite or threat associated with a future objective.

The two revelations must provide meaningful information rather than repeating what the players already know.

No Site is resolved merely by being observed, and no enemy Asset is disabled automatically.

## G-D6 — Lost Forge

**Discovery Type:** Industrial Site

The expedition locates a dormant forge, abandoned smithy, or forgotten technical installation.

**Reward:**

- Establish lawful operating rights to the recovered Site, subject to its actual political circumstances.
- Receive a **2-Gold discount** toward restoring or constructing one Workshop associated with the discovery.

The Workshop must be constructed through a legal Construct project.

Its ordinary terrain, location, and resource requirements still apply.

The discount is consumed when used and expires at the end of the following Act.

The Site does not become a functioning Workshop merely because its existence is discovered.

## G-D7 — Sealed Treasury

**Discovery Type:** Treasure

The expedition recovers a hidden treasury, abandoned strongbox, or collection of valuable goods.

**Reward:** Gain **4 Gold**.

This Gold is added to the company treasury.

The discovery does not automatically establish a commercial agreement or award another political Stake.

## G-D8 — Fallen Banner

**Discovery Type:** Military Legacy

The company discovers the insignia, surviving records, or remaining veterans of a forgotten military formation.

**Reward:**

- Gain one-time recruitment access to a specific eligible **non-Core formation** associated with the discovery.
- Reduce the cost of that one recruitment by **1 Gold**.

The formation may originate from a normally unavailable regular faction.

It must be purchased through a legal acquisition opportunity, such as Recruit or an applicable military agreement.

All appropriate slot, tag, and deployment restrictions remain in effect.

The one-time recruitment right expires at the end of the following Act if unused.

The discovery does not provide the formation for free.

## G-D9 — Shipwrecked Expedition

**Discovery Type:** Equipment Cache

The expedition recovers valuable equipment from a lost voyage or ruined expedition.

**Reward:** Gain up to **75 points of eligible equipment** in total, divided among company characters or formations.

The recovered equipment does not require a separate Develop Order for initial distribution. Select existing legal equipment profiles and observe all normal equipment/loadout slots; this reward creates no free XP, Talents, or custom crafted item effects.

It must follow all ordinary equipment restrictions and counts toward the appropriate Hero or formation point values.

Unused equipment value is lost.

It cannot be exchanged directly for Gold or converted into unrestricted unit recruitment rights.

## G-D10 — The Old Cartographer

**Discovery Type:** Geographic Knowledge

The party recovers a mapmaker's records, route surveys, or knowledge of neglected passages.

**Reward:**

- Reveal one hidden Site, significant geographic hazard, or previously unknown legal connection.
- Obtain one useful route or access lead associated with that discovery.

The route lead may permit the company to approach an otherwise difficult location through a legal passage, or provide the basis for a later passage agreement.

It does not create a permanent Road, Bridge, or Port automatically.

Hostile control and impassable geography remain relevant.

## G-D11 — Guardian's Rest

**Discovery Type:** Unusual Ally

The expedition encounters a dormant guardian, powerful creature, or other unusual entity capable of protecting the company.

**Reward:** Gain one eligible guardian or creature formation with a **strict maximum purchased value of 75 Points**, without paying its initial recruitment cost. This is not a Gold credit: the Company may not choose a more expensive guardian by paying the difference.

The guardian uses an appropriate legal formation profile.

The reward provides a one-time acquisition exception if the creature would not normally belong to an accessible regular faction.

It must occupy a suitable formation or Asset slot when deployed.

If a PC wishes to use the creature as a personal Animal Companion instead, the character must still satisfy the relevant Animal Companion Talent requirements.

The guardian is not automatically an additional free activation outside the normal deployment rules. It remains one owned formation subject to normal slots, Points, and campaign casualty rules.

## G-D12 — The Hidden Waystone

**Discovery Type:** Ancient Route

The expedition recovers a forgotten waystone, passage marker, or remnant of a regional travel network.

**Reward:**

- Identify one additional legally traversable connection or route associated with the Site.
- Obtain one single-use assisted passage through that connection for the company Party or Force, subject to the discovered route's genuine geographic limitations.

The passage may be used during an otherwise eligible movement operation and does not consume an additional Company Order.

It does not increase the maximum movement distance of that operation; it establishes a legal connection that can be traversed within the ordinary movement allowance.

After that passage is used, continued access requires a permanent legal route, treaty, or other ongoing arrangement.

# G.3 Resource Discoveries and the Map

Crystal Vein, Hidden Bazaar, and Forgotten Granary introduce discovered resource symbols beyond the initial resource totals printed on the tiles.

They use the following procedure:

1. Place the appropriate resource marker on the resolved Site's hex.
2. Record the company's discovery-based extraction, commercial, or operating right.
3. Verify a legal supply connection.
4. Add the symbol to the company's accessible rating, up to the normal maximum of 3.
5. Recalculate access if the connection or agreement is later lost.

A discovered source remains part of the map.

Other powers may attempt to negotiate for it or contest its use through the ordinary campaign rules.

The original starting resource totals in Appendix O remain unchanged.

## G.4 Military Discovery Limits

Free formations and military Assets from Discoveries are added to the Owned Roster.

They still require legal deployment slots and count toward the normal point ceiling.

A Discovery giving access to a specific outside-faction unit does not unlock the entire outside roster.

A reward that permits a unit to be acquired later must still identify an appropriate legal acquisition opportunity and Gold cost.
