# APPENDIX H — DEVELOPMENT REWARDS

## Campaign Mode v4.3 — revised

Development cards represent political patronage, useful contacts, financial support, military preparations, and other advantages earned during the campaign.

Development rewards are intentionally smaller than the major rewards from successful adventures, exploration Sites, and Special Opportunities.

They support ordinary company progression without replacing the normal Company Orders.



---



# H.1 Earning Development Rewards

A participating Grand Faction receives one Development reward when it wins a normal colored political Stake.

The reward belongs to the faction whose colored marker was placed.

When the PCs earn a marker for their affiliated Grand Faction, the company receives the reward.

When an independent Grand Faction wins, that NPC power receives the reward even if the PCs directed its Maneuver.

If the Principal Opposition wins, the GM resolves the reward for the Principal Opposition.

A gray Neutral result does not award an automatic Development draw.

A Neutral Opportunity instead provides its own stated reward.

Development rewards may also be granted through an Agenda, Contested Act, or other explicit campaign effect.

## H.1.1 Drawing Cards

Shuffle the eighteen Development cards together.

Draw without replacement.

When the draw pile is exhausted, reshuffle its available discard pile to form a new draw pile.

Unspent cards retained by a faction are not returned to the draw pile until they are used, discarded, or expired.

## H.1.2 Immediate and Temporary Rewards

Gold rewards resolve immediately for the company and are placed in its treasury.

Other Development rewards may be held until their stated conditions are met.

The company may hold at most **three unused temporary Development rewards**.

If a fourth temporary reward would be held, the players must use an immediately applicable reward or discard one of their unused rewards.

Temporary Development rewards expire at the end of the following Act unless otherwise stated.

If earned during Act IV, they expire when the campaign ends.

## H.1.3 Using Development Rewards

A Development reward does not grant another Company Order unless expressly stated.

A discount normally applies to one eligible transaction or project and cannot reduce the applicable total below 1 Gold.

Discounts may combine with Supremacy, but every discount retains its own eligibility and expiration requirements.

A reward granting military access or a political contact does not automatically award territorial control or an Act marker.

# H.3 Development Card Rules

## H-D1 — War Chest

**Copies:** 5\
**Timing:** Immediate for PCs.

**Company Effect:** Gain **1 Gold**.

Add the Gold to the company treasury.

**NPC Equivalent:** The faction receives one limited 1-Gold support credit, usable toward an already legal named military recovery, contract obligation, or construction expense.

This credit is tracked by the Development card itself, not an NPC Gold treasury.

If the faction has no eligible expenditure before the card expires, it receives no substitute unit or construction.

A support credit cannot create a new strategic action.

## H-D2 — Windfall

**Copies:** 3\
**Timing:** Immediate for PCs.

**Company Effect:** Gain **2 Gold**.

Add the Gold to the company treasury.

**NPC Equivalent:** The faction receives one limited 2-Gold support credit for an already legal named military recovery, contract obligation, or construction expense.

Unused credit has no cash-out value.

The credit does not waive Military Access requirements or produce a free formation.

## H-D3 — Recruitment Contacts

**Copies:** 3\
**Timing:** Held until used.

**Company Effect:** Reduce the cost of one eligible formation acquisition or military replenishment by **1 Gold**.

The formation must already have a legal recruitment source.

This card may support an eligible Recruit operation, Commerce contract, or qualifying recovery transaction.

It does not provide a separate recruitment opportunity.

**NPC Equivalent:** Establish one named recruitment or replenishment contact associated with an existing legal source.

The contact provides the same limited 1-Gold benefit toward one qualifying military acquisition or recovery.

It does not grant the NPC an additional Maneuver or an unrestricted outside-faction roster.

## H-D4 — Work Crews

**Copies:** 2\
**Timing:** Held until used.

**Company Effect:** Reduce the Gold cost of one eligible Develop or Construct operation by **1 Gold**.

The benefit applies to the total expenditure for that operation, not separately to each PC or project.

The selected project must still satisfy all normal resource, location, crafting, and equipment requirements.

**NPC Equivalent:** Reduce the cost of one already legal construction, repair, or industrial project by 1 Gold.

This does not bypass the limit of one free Minor Improvement per Act from an NPC's routine Industry Maneuver.

It does not authorize a new Frontier Outpost annexation.

## H-D5 — Informants

**Copies:** 2\
**Timing:** Held until used.

**Company Effect:** Obtain one meaningful piece of strategic intelligence.

Choose one appropriate inquiry:

- Reveal the exact committed Maneuver card of one non-PC faction during the current Preparation Council.
- Identify one known opponent's named military support source.
- Reveal the nature of an unresolved Site or hidden military preparation.
- Identify one significant political agreement or prepared objective that can reasonably be discovered.

The information must be genuine.

When used during a Preparation Council, reveal the information before the PCs finalize their Company Order.

**NPC Equivalent:** The NPC gains one additional legal Scout or intelligence revelation, without consuming its normal Maneuver effect.

The information becomes part of that faction's established campaign knowledge.

This does not change the Strength of its current political commitment.

## H-D6 — Diplomatic Favor

**Copies:** 2\
**Timing:** Held until used.

**Company Effect:** Obtain one minor diplomatic concession from a willing or otherwise legally obligated authority with whom the company already has a legitimate political connection.

Examples include:

- An introduction to a political institution.
- Narrow passage permission.
- An appropriate audience or meeting.
- Authorization to establish a political representative.
- A limited extension of an existing nonmilitary agreement.

The Favor may permit placing or relocating one Political Influence marker if the location and the normal two-marker limit allow it.

**Restrictions:** A Diplomatic Favor cannot independently grant political control, a Political Mandate, an entire new military treaty, or a free formation.

**NPC Equivalent:** Establish one comparable minor political contact, permission, or eligible Political Influence placement.

The normal marker limit and geographic requirements still apply.

## H-D7 — Field Preparations

**Copies:** 1\
**Timing:** Held until a qualifying battle.

**Company Effect:** Gain **one Battlefield Edge** for a qualifying structured battle.

**Before deployment**, publicly commit the Edge, naming **one exact Round** and **one opposing participant**. When that Round begins and CP refreshes, that participant has 1 fewer CP for the Round, to a minimum of 1 CP. Targeting the GM affects their shared pool; targeting the PCs names one specific PC.

The Edge is spent as soon as it is committed before deployment, even if the battle ends before its chosen Round. A side may commit at most two Edges in the same battle; multiple effects respect the minimum-1-CP floor. The Edge is effective even in early campaigns without physical Command cards, and **does not pre-exhaust or disable a particular Command**.

**NPC Equivalent:** The same one-use Battlefield Edge, committed for an eligible battle involving that faction. NPC use follows the same timing, declaration and minimum-CP requirements.

The reward does not provide extra army Points, extra CP or an additional activation.

# H.4 NPC Development Without a Separate Treasury

Participating NPC powers receive Development cards normally.

However, the GM does not maintain a full Gold income and expenditure system for each power.

Instead, financial rewards are represented by **single-use support credits attached to the actual Development cards**.

These can only reduce the cost of a military or infrastructure development that is already legal and has an authorized means of being completed.

The card is spent when its benefit is used.

This system does not permit an NPC faction to turn several Windfalls directly into unexplained new armies or territorial claims.

Other rewards use their stated NPC equivalents.

NPC powers may hold up to three unused temporary Development cards, using the same expiration rules as the company.

## H.4.1 Named Support and Military Recovery

A support credit may help restore an eligible Mauled named formation, maintain a qualifying contract, or repair a damaged facility when that recovery is otherwise available.

The credit cannot recreate a permanently Destroyed unique Asset without a valid replacement source.

It cannot bypass the underlying political agreement.

## H.4.2 Construction Credits

A support credit can contribute toward an authorized project, but does not grant a separate construction action.

If the faction has already used its routine free Minor Industry construction for the Act, it must have another legitimate construction opportunity before using the credit on a new Improvement.

## H.4.3 Unusable Rewards

A reward that cannot legally be used before expiration is discarded.

This is intentional: not every political reward will be equally useful in every strategic situation.

The GM should not create an arbitrary additional unit or treaty merely to prevent an NPC card from expiring.



---



# H.5 Development Cards and Other Discounts

For the PC Company, multiple eligible discounts may apply to the same purchase.

Resolve them against the combined applicable expenditure.

**Example:** Four PCs select Recruit with Supremacy and purchase formations costing 12 Gold in total.

Recruit Supremacy reduces the total by 2 Gold.

A previously earned Recruitment Contacts reward reduces the total by another 1 Gold.

The company pays 9 Gold.

Both discounts are expended.

No formation is obtained for free solely because several discounts were combined.

For NPC factions, the same principle applies to support credits, but each qualifying project must already be authorized.



---



# H.6 Development Rewards and the Act Track

A normal colored Stake awards:

1. One colored Act marker.
2. One Development draw.
3. Any separately satisfied material consequence specified by the completed Task.

A Neutral Opportunity awards:

1. One gray Act marker.
2. The Opportunity's specified reward.
3. No additional automatic Development draw.

A Neutral result caused by failed or ineligible political commitments does not award a Development draw.

Contested Act and Agenda benefits are resolved separately according to their own rules.
