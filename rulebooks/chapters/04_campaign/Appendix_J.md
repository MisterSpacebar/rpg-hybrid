## Campaign Mode v4.3

This appendix lists the components required for Campaign Mode and establishes the preparation and transition procedures for each campaign format.

## J.1.1 Company Orders

Prepare one shared seven-card Company Order hand.

Each card may be selected once per Campaign Year and refreshes at the beginning of Spring.

## J.1.2 Faction Maneuver Decks

Prepare one twelve-card deck for each participating non-PC Grand Faction.

The PCs' affiliated Grand Faction does not use an additional independent Maneuver deck.

Each non-PC deck refreshes at the beginning of every Act.

## J.1.3 Act Agendas

Prepare three eligible Agendas per Act, twelve in total.

Randomly select one appropriate Agenda at the beginning of each Act.

## J.1.4 Political Task Catalog

The forty-eight Task cards comprise:

- Eighteen General Tasks.
- Six Tasks associated with each of the five Grand Factions.

Only include faction-specific Tasks belonging to participating Grand Factions.

Tasks are organized into Open, Sponsored, and Exclusive categories.

## J.1.5 Neutral Opportunities

Prepare four Treasury Opportunities and eight Special Opportunities.

Every Act receives one scheduled Treasury Opportunity.

Each campaign format additionally receives its specified number of Specials.

Only one Neutral Opportunity is scheduled during any given Turn.

## J.1.6 Discoveries and Development Rewards

Prepare the twelve-card Discovery deck and eighteen-card Development deck.

Discovery cards are drawn without replacement while available. Explore Supremacy permits revealing two eligible cards and selecting one.

Development cards are awarded to the winning Grand Faction of a normal colored political Stake.

The same card cannot be drawn twice from the available deck before it is returned or the draw pile is legitimately refreshed.

## J.1.7 Charters

Prepare five Grand Faction Charters.

The Company receives the Charter associated with its affiliated Grand Faction upon entering Act II.

Prepare its classifications according to the actual Act length.

| 10           | 4    | 3         | 3         | 10    |

The Task Display begins with up to three selected Tasks.

After resolving the Active Task, remove it and replenish the Display from that Act's reserve pool.

If a Task has no available legal target, replace it with another eligible Task of the same classification.

Tasks should not be exhausted permanently merely because they were used in an earlier Act. Shuffle the eligible Task catalog when preparing a new Act and avoid repeats where practical.

# J.6 Pre-Campaign Setup

Complete the following steps before Act I.

1. Select the campaign format.
2. Choose the PCs' affiliated Grand Faction.
3. Select the Principal Opposition. Under the **standard public-identity procedure**, name its Grand Faction now; its military faction and preparations may remain concealed until their normal reveal deadline.
4. Select the remaining independent Grand Factions.
5. Place all participating Capital tiles.
6. Place the appropriate Developed and Wild Frontier tiles.
7. Verify that the map is connected and important geographic routes remain usable.
8. Place Political Control markers and Local Authorities.
9. Place starting Roads and printed infrastructure.
10. Place unresolved exploration Sites, including Founder's Legacy.
11. Place strategic NPC Force markers and the PC Party marker.
12. Record initial legal resource access.
13. Create the PCs and calculate their initial Hero Points using `max(100, XP spent on Talents)`, plus equipped equipment and explicit additional Point costs.
14. Prepare the Company's seven Order cards.
15. Prepare participating NPC Maneuver decks.
16. Shuffle available Development and Discovery rewards.
17. Schedule Neutral Opportunities privately.
18. Select the Act I Agenda and Task pool.
19. Reveal the initial three-card Task Display.
20. Assign Council Influence, normally beginning with the PCs.

The PCs do not receive their Charter and Patron formations before Act II unless a scenario expressly provides an exception. At Act II, each PC gains two Patron slots, but the **two free Patron formations are shared by the entire Company**, awarded at their ordinary starting sizes.

**Opposition identity:** The recommended standard is to make the **Opposition Grand Faction public from setup**, because its Capital, participation, Maneuver card, and independent-versus-opposition status are otherwise visible through the Council. The GM may keep its *home military faction* unselected or concealed during Act I, but must select and reveal it no later than the end of Act II. Any military Units deployed before selection must already have a lawful source; selecting the home military faction cannot retroactively change earlier battles or agreements. This satisfies the Campaign Core's minimum disclosure deadlines (one identity by the end of Act I, both by the end of Act II).

A secret-Grand-Faction variant requires a procedure agreed at setup for Council direction of independent powers and hidden commitments; it is **not** the default and must not retroactively deny players a legal Council choice.

# J.8 Neutral Opportunity Scheduling

At the beginning of each Act, privately assign:

- One Treasury Opportunity.
- The format's required number of Special Opportunities.

Assign each Opportunity to a different Turn.

Reveal each scheduled Opportunity during its Preparation Council.

The Company may choose whether to pursue it.

Completing it awards one gray Act marker and its stated reward, replacing the normal political result for that Turn.

A failed or ignored Opportunity leaves the ordinary Stake available for resolution.



---



# J.9 Beginning Each New Act

At the beginning of Acts II, III, and IV:

1. Resolve the previous Act's Agenda or Contested result.
2. Award 5 Act Advancement XP per PC.
3. Award the Provincial Dividend: 1 Gold per PC for each majority-controlled Province.
4. Apply the Company's appropriate Charter, Patron, Doctrine, and deployment progression.
5. Apply the Principal Opposition's appropriate recruitment-slot progression and military reveal deadlines. **At Act III, the GM selects and reveals its first Doctrine and Commands before the PCs choose their Doctrine; at Act IV, the GM chooses its second, distinct Doctrine.**
6. Select the new Agenda.
7. Prepare the new Task pool and public Display.
8. Refresh the NPC Maneuver decks.
9. Expire Mandates and other effects that ended with the previous Act.
10. Establish the new political and military situation.
11. Retain the existing Council Influence holder.

In Acts II and III, also establish a Pivotal Objective.

Pivotal Objectives are consequential challenges involving the central campaign conflict. They may be resolved through combat, investigation, diplomacy, sabotage, defense, or another substantial undertaking.

Each Act offers one Pivotal Objective XP award of 5 XP per PC.

These objectives do not require additional cards.



---



# J.10 Beginning a Campaign Year

At the beginning of Spring:

1. Refresh all seven Company Orders.
2. Reset the year's Supremacy record.
3. Continue tracking territorial control, agreements, resources, and military assets normally.

Do not clear the Act Track unless an Act has ended.

Do not refresh NPC Maneuver decks unless an Act has begun.



---



# J.11 Principal Opposition Preparation

The GM builds one shared Opposition army, rather than one detachment per PC. Use the campaign-specific slot progression below (the normal matched-play 15-slot layout does not override these totals).

| Format | Act II slots | Act III slots | Final Act IV slots | Doctrine Commands, Act III + IV |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Local | 8 | 11 | **13** | 2 + 1 |
| Standard | 8 | 12 | **15** | 2 + 2 |
| Long | 8 | 13 | **17** | 3 + 2 |
| Grand | 8 | 13 | **19** | 3 + 3 |

Act II begins with H/C/C, two home-faction slots, two faction-Command slots, and one campaign expansion slot. Further Doctrine foundations, selected Command slots, and campaign expansion slots yield the table totals. Campaign expansion slots may be C, E, R, V, S, or A, subject to the normal source and Points restrictions; they are not free Units.

The GM selects and reveals the **first Doctrine at the start of Act III before PC Doctrine selection**, and chooses the second, distinct Doctrine at the start of Act IV after seeing PC development. Reveal the Opposition Grand Faction at setup under the normal procedure; reveal the home military faction no later than the end of Act II.

**Campaign CP:** Each PC ordinarily has 2 CP per Round; for Local, Standard and Long the GM has `max(2, 2 × PCs − 1)` shared CP; for Grand the GM has `max(2, 2 × PCs + 1)` shared CP. With four PCs, the GM has 7 CP in Standard or 9 CP in Grand. This is separate from 2,000-point matched play's eight-Command-card CP economy.

Before Act II, identify the Opposition's first substantial military objective.

Before Act III, identify an escalation involving a major Force, military institution, specialist formation, or other important strategic resource.

During Act III, make the Opposition's major final-battle support sources discoverable through appropriate intelligence and campaign events.

During Act IV, establish legitimate opportunities for the PCs to interfere with these preparations.

An Opposition Operation must use legal strategic positioning and applicable campaign mechanisms. It does not grant free extra Maneuver cards or automatic territorial conquest.

The PCs may resolve an intended confrontation through combat, diplomacy, investigation, or another legitimate alternative.

# J.13 Completion Checklist

At the conclusion of the final Turn:

1. Resolve the last Company operation.
2. Resolve the final Stake and NPC Maneuvers.
3. Award applicable Turn, exploration, and objective XP.
4. Determine the final Act outcome.
5. Resolve the concluding battle, settlement, or other final scenario as established.
6. Record territorial and military consequences.
7. Apply the selected Act IV Agenda's epilogue effect.
8. Record surviving formations, agreements, and Assets.
9. Conclude the campaign or establish the state of its continuation.
