# APPENDIX A — COMPANY ORDER CARDS

## Campaign Mode — Revised for Current Campaign Rules

Company Orders represent the principal strategic undertaking selected collectively by the Player Character Company during each Campaign Turn.

The Company has seven numbered Order cards.

Each Order may be selected once per Campaign Year. Used Orders refresh at the beginning of Spring, independently of Act boundaries.

Every Campaign Turn permits one Company Order, regardless of the number of PCs.

## A.1 Supremacy

An Order gains **Supremacy** when its number is higher than every Order previously played during that Campaign Year.

The first Order played each Year always gains Supremacy.

Supremacy awards only the benefit printed on the selected card. It does not duplicate the Order, provide another Turn, or create another political Stake.

Gold reductions apply once to the combined eligible Company expenditure, not separately to each PC. They cannot reduce a positive expenditure below 1 Gold.



---



## A.2 Order 1 — Explore

**Role:** Expeditions, discoveries, reconnaissance, and exploration Sites.

### Normal Action — Expedition

Undertake one eligible expedition, investigate one unresolved Site, or conduct a meaningful reconnaissance operation.

An expedition may require travel, investigation, skill checks, combat, negotiation, or another appropriate adventure.

When a unique Site is successfully resolved, the Company receives its ordinary Discovery or fixed reward and every PC receives **5 Exploration XP**.

A Site awards this XP only once.

Reconnaissance, scouting, or revisiting a resolved Site does not award another Discovery or Exploration XP.

### Supremacy — Insight

When the Order awards a random Discovery, reveal two eligible Discovery cards and choose one.

Return the unchosen card to the available pool.

This does not duplicate a Site's fixed reward or XP.

### Restrictions

The target must be reachable through a legal route or other valid means.

Exploration does not automatically transfer political ownership or grant military access beyond the actual reward's terms.



---



## A.3 Order 2 — Commerce

**Role:** Reliable income, trade agreements, mercenaries, and contracted services.

Choose one of two modes.

### Option A — Earn Income

Gain **1 Gold per participating PC**.

Add the full amount to the shared Company treasury.

The Company requires access to a legitimate commercial settlement or institution. Its affiliated Capital normally qualifies.

The baseline payment does not require a separate Skill Check.

### Option B — Secure Contract

Pursue one significant commercial or contractual outcome, such as:

- Hire one eligible mercenary formation.
- Establish a trade agreement.
- Secure access to a regional resource.
- Obtain specialist services.
- Establish logistical support or a specific commercial recruitment right.

A hired formation requires its normal Gold cost unless the negotiated agreement specifies otherwise.

The contract must indicate whether the formation is permanent or temporary, its obligations, and when it expires.

This option does **not** also award the ordinary Commerce income.

### Supremacy — Favorable Terms

- **Earn Income:** Gain 1 additional Gold.
- **Secure Contract:** Reduce one eligible contract expense by 1 Gold.

Commerce income does not scale with Provinces. Provincial Dividends are a separate source of income.

## A.4 Order 3 — Develop

**Role:** Crafting, equipment, troop improvements, Talent retraining, Flex slots, and advanced Asset projects.

### Normal Action — Improve the Warband

The Company receives a shared pool of:

**1 + the number of participating PCs Minor Project opportunities.**

A Minor Project costs one opportunity.

The Company may exchange two Minor opportunities for one Major Project.

Minor Projects may be repeated.

Each **Major Project category** may be selected at most once per Develop Order.

Projects may be allocated to any eligible PC, owned formation, or Company Asset.

The Company must satisfy all Gold costs, point costs, prerequisites, and required checks.

### Minor Projects

| **Project**         | **Effect**                                                                  |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Craft Equipment     | Produce one eligible piece of equipment                                     |
| Upgrade Equipment   | Apply one eligible ordinary equipment improvement                           |
| Upgrade Formation   | Purchase or install one normal printed formation upgrade                    |
| Refit Formation     | Change one eligible loadout or equipment choice, paying required costs      |
| Train or Specialize | Complete one eligible standard training project defined by an existing rule |

Minor Projects may be duplicated and may target the same PC or formation repeatedly, provided the resulting configuration remains legal.

**Upgrading an owned formation:** When a Develop project adds purchased Points to an existing formation, pay the normal upgrade Points in the detachment roster and **1 Gold per 25 Points added, rounded up**. Calculate this Gold amount from the added Points, not from the full Unit value. Printed additional Models, weapons, Veterans, and other standard upgrades follow this rule. Their normal per-Model surcharges still apply. Replacing equipment with a cheaper option does not automatically refund previously spent Gold. Special mission rewards may explicitly waive the Gold cost but do not waive the added Points.

Equipment cost or crafting difficulty does not automatically determine whether a project is Major. Ordinary crafting and printed formation upgrades remain Minor unless their own rules explicitly specify otherwise.

### Major Projects

| **Category**      | **Effect**                                          |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Expand Detachment | Purchase one Flex slot                              |
| Talent Retraining | Replace one Talent with another of the same Tier    |
| Asset Research    | Unlock one specific Asset or advanced modification  |
| Asset Development | Substantially improve or reconstruct an owned Asset |

Each Major Project consumes two Minor opportunities.

A four-PC Company therefore has five opportunities and may undertake, for example, two different Major Projects and one Minor Project.

#### Expand Detachment

**Cost:** 2 Gold.

Purchase one Flex slot for an eligible PC.

Choose one legal slot category: **C, E, R, V, S, or A**. An ordinary Flex slot cannot be H. A Flex slot grants eligibility for that recruitment category only; it does not automatically grant access to faction units that the Company has not unlocked.

A Flex slot provides slot-type flexibility, not additional deployment points or a free formation.

Each PC may purchase at most two Flex slots over the campaign.

Total detachment slots remain capped at eight per PC in Local, Standard, and Long, or nine in Grand.

Only one Expand Detachment project may be undertaken during a Develop Order.

#### Talent Retraining

**Cost:** 2 Gold.

One PC replaces one acquired Talent with another Talent of the same Tier.

The entire Talent structure must remain legal afterward, including tier-pyramid requirements, ranked Talent requirements, class restrictions, and all other prerequisites.

The replacement cannot produce an XP refund. Any additional XP cost must be paid.

The replacement takes effect after the current Campaign Turn is resolved.

Only one Talent Retraining project may be undertaken during a Develop Order.

Ordinary character advancement through earned XP does not require Develop.

#### Asset Research

**Normal Cost:** 2 Gold plus any required materials or access.

Research one specified Asset, prototype, or advanced modification.

The Company must possess a legitimate research lead, such as a blueprint, Workshop, suitable industrial contact, discovered military Asset, or another established source.

The project may require an appropriate Skill Check.

Research grants access to the specified option; it does not automatically create a free Asset.

Only one Asset Research project may be undertaken per Develop Order.

#### Asset Development

Undertake one substantial approved modification, reconstruction, or upgrade to an existing Company Asset.

The Company must possess the relevant Asset and satisfy its facility, resource, Gold, point, and technical requirements.

Ordinary printed formation upgrades remain Minor Projects.

Only one Asset Development project may be undertaken per Develop Order.

### Supremacy — Efficient Development

Reduce the Company's combined eligible Development Gold expenditure by **2 Gold**.

This applies once to the entire Order.

It does not provide additional project opportunities or permit duplicate Major Project categories.

## A.5 Order 4 — Construct

**Role:** Infrastructure, supply connections, territorial development, and defensive positions.

### Normal Action — Construction

Complete either:

- One Major Improvement; or
- Up to two Minor Improvements.

This allowance belongs to the Company collectively, not to every PC.

| **Improvement**  | **Category** | **Gold** | **Required Resource Access** |
| ---------------- | ------------ | -------- | ---------------------------- |
| Road             | Minor        | 1        | Stone 1                      |
| Bridge           | Minor        | 2        | Stone 1, Supply 1            |
| Watchtower       | Minor        | 2        | Stone 1, Influence 1         |
| Supply Camp      | Minor        | 2        | Supply 2                     |
| Frontier Outpost | Minor        | 2        | Stone 1, Supply 1            |
| Fortress         | Major        | 4        | Stone 2, Supply 1            |
| Workshop         | Major        | 3        | Stone 2, Supply 1            |
| Trade Hub        | Major        | 3        | Supply 1, Influence 2        |
| Port             | Major        | 3        | Stone 1, Supply 1            |

Construction requires lawful location access and the necessary connections.

A Frontier Outpost claims one eligible unclaimed hex, not an entire Province.

### Supremacy — Coordinated Construction

Reduce combined eligible construction expenditure by **1 Gold**.

## A.6 Order 5 — Recruit

**Role:** Acquiring permanent formations and replenishing military strength.

### Normal Action — Muster

Each participating PC may acquire or replenish one eligible formation.

The normal acquisition cost is:

**1 Gold per 25 formation points, rounded up.**

This uses the Unit's final purchased Points, including weapon packages, Veterans, purchased Models, and other upgrades. A reward described as a **Gold recruitment credit** reduces only the Gold paid for its designated acquisition; the Unit still counts at full Points. A credit cannot generate spare Gold or remove recruitment eligibility restrictions unless its reward explicitly says otherwise.

Each formation must have a legal recruitment source and an appropriate slot before deployment. **During Act I**, when the Company has only the PCs' H slots, Recruit may secure future contacts, prospective contracts, or another otherwise legal recruitment benefit, but cannot bypass locked Patron or Doctrine slots, create an early full Company Force, or deploy additional formations without specific permission.

New formations enter the Owned Roster and need not be deployed immediately.

Recruit does not grant additional slots or deployment points.

Mauled and Scattered formations use their ordinary Upkeep recovery rules and do not require Recruit when otherwise eligible for that recovery.

### Supremacy — Efficient Muster

Reduce the Company's combined eligible recruitment expenditures by **2 Gold**.

Unused recruitment opportunities do not carry forward.

## A.7 Order 6 — Politics

**Role:** Alliances, military access, territorial agreements, diplomatic influence, and institutional changes.

### Normal Action — Political Initiative

Pursue one substantive political objective or agreement.

Possible outcomes include:

- A military alliance.
- A local recruitment charter.
- Passage or resource-access rights.
- A Political Mandate.
- A negotiated territorial claim.
- Military manufacturing access.
- A diplomatic settlement.
- Modification or termination of an existing agreement.

The Company must satisfy the actual conditions of the requested outcome.

A political Stake victory does not automatically fulfill the substantive agreement.

### Supremacy — Additional Concession

Upon successfully completing the principal objective, obtain one related minor concession.

The concession may provide a contact, narrow passage permission, favorable clause, or similar secondary benefit.

It cannot independently provide another major agreement, formation, territorial annexation, or Act marker.



---



## A.8 Order 7 — Mobilize

**Role:** Force movement, military operations, interception, defense, and offensive action.

### Normal Action — Military Operation

Move the Company Force up to **two legal hexes** and undertake one eligible military operation.

A movement that uses a qualifying Road may increase its maximum distance by one hex.

Operations may include an attack, defense, escort, relief expedition, military occupation, interception, or disruption of enemy infrastructure.

The battle, when required, uses the normal structured combat rules.

Movement or an unopposed military presence alone does not automatically change political control.

Before Act II, an eligible Mobilize operation may be undertaken by the PC Party without a full Company Force.

### Supremacy — Battlefield Edge

Gain one Battlefield Edge for a qualifying battle arising from the operation. Before deployment, declare the specific Round and opposing participant affected by that Edge. That participant loses 1 available CP (minimum 1) in the designated Round when CP refreshes. The Edge is expended once committed even if its chosen Round never occurs.

The normal maximum of two Edges per side applies.
