# APPENDIX L — QUICK REFERENCE SHEETS

## Campaign Mode v4.3

# L.1 Campaign Lengths

| Format   | Act I | Act II | Act III | Act IV | Total |
| -------- | ----- | ------ | ------- | ------ | ----- |
| Local    | 3     | 4      | 5       | 6      | 18    |
| Standard | 4     | 5      | 7       | 9      | 25    |
| Long     | 5     | 6      | 8       | 10     | 29    |
| Grand    | 6     | 7      | 9       | 11     | 33    |

A Turn represents one Season.

Spring → Summer → Autumn → Winter

Company Orders refresh every Spring.

NPC Maneuver decks refresh at each new Act.

# L.2 Campaign Turn

1. Reveal Task Display and scheduled Neutral Opportunity.
2. Council Influence holder chooses the Active Stake, target, suit, and Lead.
3. Each participating non-PC Grand Faction commits one Maneuver.
4. PCs choose one available Company Order and determine Supremacy.
5. Resolve the Company's operation.
6. Determine any successful Neutral Opportunity or PC political claim.
7. Resolve eligible NPC commitments if necessary.
8. Award exactly one colored or gray Act marker.
9. Award one Development draw to the winner of a colored Stake.
10. Resolve legal NPC basic map actions.
11. Update campaign records and Upkeep.
12. Award applicable XP.
13. Advance Season and pass Council Influence.

# L.3 Company Order Reference

| # | Order     | Normal Effect                                 | Supremacy                       |
| - | --------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| 1 | Explore   | One expedition/Site/reconnaissance            | Two Discovery choices, keep one |
| 2 | Commerce  | 1G/PC income or one contract                  | +1G or −1G contract             |
| 3 | Develop   | 1 + PCs project capacity                      | −2G                             |
| 4 | Construct | One Major or two Minor Improvements           | −1G                             |
| 5 | Recruit   | One eligible formation per PC                 | −2G                             |
| 6 | Politics  | One significant political agreement/objective | One minor concession            |
| 7 | Mobilize  | Move Force and resolve military operation     | One Battlefield Edge            |

Supremacy applies when the Order number exceeds every previously played Order number that Year.

The first Order each Year automatically qualifies.

# L.4 Develop Projects

Capacity = 1 + participating PCs.

One Minor Project consumes one opportunity.

One Major Project consumes two opportunities.

Minor Projects may repeat.

Each Major Project category may be used once per Develop Order.

| Minor Projects             | Major Projects    |
| -------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Craft Equipment            | Expand Detachment |
| Upgrade Equipment          | Talent Retraining |
| Upgrade Formation          | Asset Research    |
| Refit Formation            | Asset Development |
| Eligible standard training | —              |

A Flex slot costs 2 Gold, grants one fixed **C, E, R, V, S, or A** category, and cannot be H. Maximum two per PC. Existing formation upgrades normally cost **1 Gold per 25 Points added, rounded up**; their Points also increase the formation value.

Talent Retraining costs 2 Gold and requires a fully legal resulting Talent structure.

Asset Research normally costs 2 Gold plus any special requirements.

Normal character XP spending does not consume Develop capacity.

# L.5 Hero Point Value

**Base Hero Points = max(100, XP spent on Talents)**

**Final Hero Points = Base Hero Points + Equipped Equipment Points + other explicit Point costs**

| XP earned | XP spent on Talents | Base Hero Points |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 50 | 25 | 100 |
| 110 | 85 | 100 |
| 110 | 110 | 110 |
| 150 | 125 | 125 |
| 175 | 150 | 150 |
| 200 | 200 | 200 |
| 250 | 225 | 225 |

Earned-but-unspent XP does not raise battlefield value. Ordinary Talent effects are already reflected in spent Talent XP; don't add their XP cost again. Equipment and explicitly additional Point costs still apply. Spend and record XP, then finalize the Hero's Points before each deployment.

# L.9 Formation Costs and Recovery

**New recruitment:** `Gold = ceil(Formation Points / 25)`.

**Purchased upgrades to an owned formation:** `Gold = ceil(Added Points / 25)`; any printed equipment or per-Model surcharges add their Points normally. Upgrades do not refund already spent Gold.

| Points | Recruitment Gold |
| ---: | ---: |
| 50 | 2 |
| 75 | 3 |
| 100 | 4 |
| 125 | 5 |
| 150 | 6 |
| 200 | 8 |

**Surviving formation:** If its purchased Models or Wounds need restoration, pay **half its normal full recruitment Gold cost, rounded up**, as one flat replenishment payment per formation at eligible Upkeep. Until replenished, it may deploy with reduced Model count; ordinary Wounds of surviving Models recover under campaign rules.

**Completely Defeated formation:** Roll one D6 after battle.

| D6 | Result | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Destroyed** | Remove from Owned Roster; legal full-cost replacement or explicit Reconstitution exception |
| 2–4 | **Mauled** | Unavailable until paid recovery at half recruitment Gold, rounded up |
| 5–6 | **Scattered** | Unavailable; returns after one completed Turn or eligible early recovery for 1 Gold |

Double Formations pay recovery as **one** formation using their full doubled purchased Points; they roll one casualty die, not one per half. PCs use Critical Injuries, not formation casualty rolls.

# L.10 Territory and Resources

A Grand Faction controls a Province if it controls at least two of its three hexes.

Individual hex ownership remains distinct.

The resources are Stone, Supply, and Influence.

Accessible resource ratings range from 0 to 3.

Political Influence markers are not printed Influence resources and do not grant territorial ownership.

# L.12 Political Stakes

Open: Any eligible claimant.

Sponsored: Requires an appropriate sponsor.

Exclusive: Political recognition belongs to its specified eligible beneficiary.

Every Turn awards exactly one colored or gray Act marker.

A colored winner receives one Development draw.

Completing a Neutral Opportunity instead awards its stated reward and a gray marker.

A political victory does not automatically grant territorial or material outcomes.

## Local Influence

A qualifying faction receives +1 nonstacking Strength for controlling or maintaining Political Influence at or adjacent to the target.

Matching-suit eligible NPC commitments take priority.

Ties favor the qualifying incumbent, then the eligible Lead, then Neutral.

# L.14 Principal Opposition

**Identity:** Under the standard setup, the Principal Opposition Grand Faction is public. Its home military faction may remain concealed, but must be revealed by the end of Act II. The Campaign Core permits an agreed concealed-identity variant as long as at least one identity is revealed by the end of Act I and both by the end of Act II, with Council participation handled fairly.

**Doctrine order:** The GM reveals its first Doctrine and chosen Commands at the start of Act III **before** PCs select theirs. The GM selects its second, distinct Doctrine at the start of Act IV, after seeing PC strategy.

| Campaign | Act II slots | Act III slots | Act IV slots | Doctrine Commands III + IV |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Local | 8 | 11 | **13** | 2 + 1 |
| Standard | 8 | 12 | **15** | 2 + 2 |
| Long | 8 | 13 | **17** | 3 + 2 |
| Grand | 8 | 13 | **19** | 3 + 3 |

The Opposition uses **one shared army**, not one detachment per PC. Faction, Doctrine and campaign expansion slots never provide free Units or bypass access, Point, Hero or Asset limits. Outside-faction allied or contracted formations normally remain limited to two deployed Units. Important military Assets persist across encounters.

**Campaign tactical CP:** Each PC normally receives **2 CP per Round**, refreshing at the start of the Round whether or not physical Command cards exist.

| PCs | Total PC CP | GM CP Local/Standard/Long | GM CP Grand |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2 | 4 | 3 | 5 |
| 3 | 6 | 5 | 7 |
| 4 | 8 | 7 | 9 |
| 5 | 10 | 9 | 11 |

GM formula: Local/Standard/Long `max(2, 2 × PCs − 1)`; Grand `max(2, 2 × PCs + 1)`. This is **not** matched play's eight-card CP pool. The Grand exception is provisional for balance testing.

# L.15 GM Campaign Checklist

### Beginning an Act

- Resolve previous Act.
- Award Act Advancement XP, if entering Acts II–IV.
- Award Provincial Dividend, if eligible.
- Update Charter, Patron, Doctrine, and deployment progression, plus Opposition slots and identity disclosure.
- At Act III, choose the GM first Doctrine before PC Doctrines; at Act IV, choose GM second Doctrine.
- Prepare Agenda and Task pool.
- Refresh NPC Maneuver decks.
- Establish Pivotal Objective in Acts II and III.
- Review Opposition Operation and agreements.

### Preparation Council

- Reveal scheduled Neutral Opportunity.
- Choose Stake and legal target.
- Record Local Influence.
- Commit NPC Maneuvers.
- Choose Company Order and Supremacy.
- Establish the operation.

### Resolution Council

- Resolve Company objective.
- Determine Neutral or political winner.
- Award one Act marker.
- Award Development draw when colored.
- Resolve NPC basic actions.
- Update map, roster, and agreements.
- Resolve Gold, surviving-formation flat replenishment, and defeated-formation casualty rolls.
- Award Turn, Site, and Pivotal XP.
- Advance calendar and pass Council Influence.

# L.16 Battlefield Edge Quick Reference

1. **Before deployment**, publicly commit each Edge, name the **exact Round** and **one opposing participant**.
2. When that Round's CP refreshes, that participant receives **1 fewer CP**, to a minimum of 1. Against the GM, target the shared pool; against PCs, target one named PC.
3. A maximum of **two Edges per side** may be committed to one battle. Both may affect one target subject to the CP floor.
4. An Edge is spent upon commitment **even if its Round is never reached**.
5. The Edge works without physical cards, does **not** pre-exhaust a Command, and does not award extra CP.

# L.17 PC Detachment and Charter Reference

| Stage | Typical PC detachment entitlement |
| --- | --- |
| Act I | Personal H for the PC Hero only (1 slot) |
| Act II | Two Patron slots; two standard-size free Patron formations shared by the entire Company (3 slots per PC) |
| Act III | One Doctrine foundation and first selected Command slot (5 slots per PC) |
| Act IV | Another selected Doctrine Command slot (6 slots per PC) |
| Grand special progression | Third Doctrine Command slot (7 slots per PC) |
| Develop | Up to two additional C/E/R/V/S/A Flex slots (8 ordinary maximum; 9 Grand) |

A legal Double Formation consumes **one** compatible slot, pays its full doubled Points and Gold, and remains one Unit for activations, Unit-wide keywords and objective control.

The Company's single shared Grand Faction Charter Command costs **1 CP from the using PC**, can be activated at its stated timing, and may be used **once per Round across the whole Company**. A Charter Command doesn't add another CP or grant a free ordinary action.
