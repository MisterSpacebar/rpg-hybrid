# APPENDIX E — GRAND FACTION POLITICAL TASKS

## Campaign Mode — Revised for Current Campaign Rules

This appendix contains thirty faction-specific political Task cards.

Each of the five Grand Factions contributes:

- Two Open Tasks.
- Two Sponsored Tasks.
- Two Exclusive Tasks.

These Tasks follow the standard requirements established in Appendix D.

A faction-specific Task enters the eligible catalog only when its associated Grand Faction participates in the campaign. When drawing or displaying an invalid Task, substitute another legal Task of the **same Open, Sponsored, or Exclusive category**, as described in Appendix D. The associated faction's narrative identity does not bypass the normal target, sponsor, or beneficiary requirements.

## E.1 Faction Task Rules

**Open:** Any eligible participating Grand Faction may claim the Task, provided it has a legitimate connection to the chosen target.

**Sponsored:** The Task requires the backing of the specified institution, community, or political sponsor. The Task's associated Grand Faction does not automatically satisfy the sponsoring requirement merely because its name appears on the card.

**Exclusive:** The associated Grand Faction is the designated beneficiary unless a successful mission establishes another explicitly permitted outcome. A rival may obstruct an Exclusive Task, potentially causing it to resolve as Neutral.

When the associated Grand Faction is the PCs' own political sponsor, the PCs may complete its Exclusive Tasks on that power's behalf.

Because the PC-affiliated Grand Faction does not play a separate NPC Maneuver, the PCs must obtain the result through an eligible Company mission or another expressly permitted action.

All faction Tasks use flexible map targets. Named Capital landmarks are thematic references, not required physical locations. **Specify any actual military recruitment right or equipment/Asset access obtained through a material consequence**; a generic statement about recognition never unlocks an entire foreign roster or waives ordinary Points, Gold, or recruitment slots.

# E.2 Unbound — The Free Peoples

The Unbound favor independent communities, exploration, mutual assistance, and protection against centralized control.

Their Tasks often reward diplomatic legitimacy and access to frontier support rather than large permanent military institutions.

## U-O1 — Assembly of Freeholders

**Type:** Open\
**Suit:** Authority\
**Eligible Target:** An independent settlement, frontier council, or gathering of regional representatives.\
**Company Orders:** Politics, Commerce.

**Stake Objective:** Gain public recognition for a cooperative arrangement among free communities.

**Claimant Requirement:** Maintain a genuine relationship with at least one represented community.

**Material Consequence:** A completed agreement may establish a Political Influence contact, passage rights, or another specified local political concession.

The assembly does not automatically transfer settlement ownership.

## U-O2 — Paths of the Untamed

**Type:** Open\
**Suit:** Discovery\
**Eligible Target:** A wilderness route, unresolved Site, old frontier trail, or remote passage.\
**Company Orders:** Explore, Construct.

**Stake Objective:** Earn recognition for identifying, recovering, or securing useful knowledge of a frontier route.

**Claimant Requirement:** Possess legal exploratory access to the selected region.

**Material Consequence:** A successful expedition may reveal safe passage, a hidden Site, or a previously unknown resource connection.

Constructing a permanent Road still requires an eligible construction project.

## U-S1 — Defense of the Homesteads

**Type:** Sponsored\
**Suit:** Warfare\
**Eligible Target:** An independent settlement facing a credible military or monstrous threat.\
**Company Orders:** Mobilize, Politics, Explore.

**Required Sponsor:** The threatened settlement or its recognized representatives.

**Stake Objective:** Establish recognition as an appropriate protector of the community.

**Material Consequence:** Completing the required defense or agreement may provide a local militia commitment or continuing protective treaty.

The local defenders do not automatically become permanent company property.

## U-S2 — The Common Harvest

**Type:** Sponsored\
**Suit:** Industry\
**Eligible Target:** A frontier cooperative, productive settlement, or Supply-bearing community.\
**Company Orders:** Commerce, Construct, Politics.

**Required Sponsor:** A recognized local producer or community association.

**Stake Objective:** Support a cooperative provisioning arrangement without forcing direct annexation.

**Material Consequence:** A completed contract may provide temporary Supply access or authorization for an appropriate logistical Improvement.

A successful Industry trick does not create an additional printed resource symbol.

## U-X1 — Charter of the Free Peoples

**Type:** Exclusive — Unbound\
**Suit:** Authority\
**Eligible Target:** A settlement seeking recognition of independent government or a free-company charter.\
**Company Orders:** Politics.

**Stake Objective:** Secure recognition of the Unbound's proposed charter and its protection of local autonomy.

Rival factions may challenge or obstruct the proposed settlement.

**Material Consequence:** A legitimate agreement may establish recognized free-company recruitment rights, a defensive obligation, or political cooperation.

The settlement remains independent unless a separate agreement explicitly changes its sovereignty.

## U-X2 — Guardians of the Greenward

**Type:** Exclusive — Unbound\
**Suit:** Discovery\
**Eligible Target:** A wilderness Site, protected frontier territory, or community possessing important local knowledge.\
**Company Orders:** Explore, Mobilize.

**Stake Objective:** Establish the Unbound's recognized responsibility for protecting or investigating the location.

**Material Consequence:** Rescuing local scouts, completing a Site, or negotiating an agreement may unlock one specified frontier specialist formation or reconnaissance Asset.

No unit is granted solely for winning the Stake.

# E.3 Conclave — The Learned Orders

The Conclave prioritizes research, industry, institutional authority, and specialist military development.

Its Tasks provide opportunities for scientific discoveries, production agreements, and access to unusual military equipment.

## C-O1 — The Arcane Survey

**Type:** Open\
**Suit:** Discovery\
**Eligible Target:** A magical anomaly, abandoned research location, unusual natural feature, or unresolved Site.\
**Company Orders:** Explore, Develop.

**Stake Objective:** Gain recognition for investigating the phenomenon or establishing its scholarly significance.

**Claimant Requirement:** Possess credible investigative access or sponsorship from an appropriate research institution.

**Material Consequence:** A completed study may reveal an equipment improvement, specialist research opportunity, or unusual Site information.

Research recognition does not automatically create a completed military Asset.

## C-O2 — The Artificers' Exchange

**Type:** Open\
**Suit:** Industry\
**Eligible Target:** A guild, Workshop, equipment market, or comparable production institution.\
**Company Orders:** Commerce, Develop.

**Stake Objective:** Secure recognition for a cooperative technical or industrial exchange.

**Claimant Requirement:** Possess a legal connection to the participating producers.

**Material Consequence:** A completed arrangement may unlock crafting access, specialist repair services, or one specified military equipment option.

Purchases and Develop projects retain their ordinary costs unless an explicit reward changes them.

## C-S1 — The Academy Commission

**Type:** Sponsored\
**Suit:** Industry\
**Eligible Target:** A laboratory, local industrial partner, recognized guild, or military equipment institution.\
**Company Orders:** Commerce, Develop, Construct.

**Required Sponsor:** A recognized academic, civic, or guild institution supporting the commission.

**Stake Objective:** Gain recognition as the appropriate executor or sponsor of a specialized research or manufacturing project.

**Material Consequence:** A fulfilled commission may provide access to one specified Elite Support formation, Artillery formation, or exceptional equipment category.

The commission must identify the actual military option granted.

It does not unlock the Conclave's entire roster.

## C-S2 — The Keeping of the Seals

**Type:** Sponsored\
**Suit:** Discovery\
**Eligible Target:** A dangerous archive, forbidden Site, research repository, or contained magical threat.\
**Company Orders:** Explore, Politics.

**Required Sponsor:** The recognized custodian or institution responsible for the location.

**Stake Objective:** Earn recognition for safeguarding the institution or investigating its dangers.

**Material Consequence:** Successful containment may deny an adversary access to a named dangerous Asset, protect an existing facility, or grant specific research information.

Such restrictions require the actual containment objective to be completed.

A Discovery trick alone cannot seal a hostile facility permanently.

## C-X1 — Patent of the Archmagisters

**Type:** Exclusive — Conclave\
**Suit:** Authority\
**Eligible Target:** A research institution, valuable invention, or disputed specialist production right.\
**Company Orders:** Politics, Commerce.

**Stake Objective:** Establish recognition of the Conclave's proposed custodianship or exclusive production patent.

**Material Consequence:** A completed agreement may restrict a named specialist equipment source to approved partners.

Existing permanently owned units are not automatically erased.

An invalidated contract affects future recruitment and support according to its terms.

## C-X2 — The Luminarium Prototype

**Type:** Exclusive — Conclave\
**Suit:** Industry\
**Eligible Target:** A research facility, Workshop, experimental equipment project, or recoverable prototype.\
**Company Orders:** Develop, Explore, Construct.

**Stake Objective:** Establish the Conclave as the recognized sponsor or custodian of a significant experimental project.

**Material Consequence:** Completing the project may provide one named specialist Asset, exceptional equipment item, or experimental formation.

Its final acquisition must be supported by the actual project outcome and any required facility.

Winning the Industry trick does not automatically place a completed prototype into the army.

# E.4 Wayfarers — The Oathbound Clans

The Wayfarers prioritize clan relationships, ancestral responsibilities, secure travel, and mutual defense.

Their Tasks emphasize traditional obligations and the military support available from regional alliances.

## W-O1 — Paths of the Pilgrims

**Type:** Open\
**Suit:** Discovery\
**Eligible Target:** An ancestral route, frontier Site, mountain passage, or culturally important landmark.\
**Company Orders:** Explore, Commerce.

**Stake Objective:** Earn recognition for restoring or maintaining access to an important regional journey.

**Claimant Requirement:** Possess a genuine connection to the route, travelers, or associated institution.

**Material Consequence:** A completed expedition may reveal an accessible path, hidden Site, or useful local guides.

Permanent Roads and Bridges require their normal construction procedures.

## W-O2 — The Roadward Patrol

**Type:** Open\
**Suit:** Warfare\
**Eligible Target:** A trade route, caravan path, settlement approach, or threatened regional passage.\
**Company Orders:** Mobilize, Politics.

**Stake Objective:** Gain recognition for defending travelers and maintaining security on the selected route.

**Claimant Requirement:** Maintain an eligible military presence or a recognized protection agreement.

**Material Consequence:** An actual successful patrol, escort, or defensive mission may restore safe passage or create a specified military protection obligation.

A Warfare trick does not automatically defeat raiders or establish political control.

## W-S1 — The Council of Clans

**Type:** Sponsored\
**Suit:** Authority\
**Eligible Target:** A gathering of clan representatives, an oathbound settlement, or an established regional assembly.\
**Company Orders:** Politics.

**Required Sponsor:** At least one recognized clan or community representative independent of the claimant.

**Stake Objective:** Secure recognition of a proposed arrangement among the participating clans.

**Material Consequence:** A completed agreement may grant a political contact, passage right, or temporary allied warband.

The warband's duration, ownership, and recruitment requirements must be stated in the agreement.

## W-S2 — The Oathbound Caravan

**Type:** Sponsored\
**Suit:** Industry\
**Eligible Target:** A protected caravan, regional trade expedition, or isolated supply community.\
**Company Orders:** Commerce, Mobilize.

**Required Sponsor:** The caravan owner, merchant association, or community arranging the journey.

**Stake Objective:** Establish the claimant as a recognized escort, trading partner, or supplier.

**Material Consequence:** A completed escort or contract may establish Supply access, commercial rights, or an appropriate military transport arrangement.

The convoy does not automatically provide free formations or permanent resource ownership.

## W-X1 — Succession at the Oathstone

**Type:** Exclusive — Wayfarers\
**Suit:** Authority\
**Eligible Target:** A disputed clan leadership position, ancestral oath, or recognized regional succession.\
**Company Orders:** Politics.

**Stake Objective:** Establish recognition for the Wayfarers' proposed leadership settlement.

**Material Consequence:** A successfully resolved dispute may establish access to one named commander, allied clan formation, or political agreement.

The arrangement does not provide additional Command Points or unrestricted military recruitment.

The PCs may support or contest the succession through the narrative mission.

## W-X2 — Banner of the Wandering Host

**Type:** Exclusive — Wayfarers\
**Suit:** Warfare\
**Eligible Target:** An oathbound warband, ancestral standard, military rallying point, or threatened allied settlement.\
**Company Orders:** Mobilize, Politics.

**Stake Objective:** Recognize the Wayfarers' authority to assemble a regional defensive host.

**Material Consequence:** A completed muster or military agreement may unlock one specified allied warband, cavalry contingent, or other appropriate formation.

The unit must be acquired according to its agreement and deployed legally.

Winning the Warfare trick does not automatically assemble a free army.

# E.5 Legions — The Organized Hosts

The Legions prioritize organized military power, strategic infrastructure, territorial security, and recognized command authority.

Their Tasks support professional army development and the political mechanisms sustaining large military operations.

## L-O1 — The Military Road

**Type:** Open\
**Suit:** Industry\
**Eligible Target:** A suitable military passage, supply route, or strategically important connection between regions.\
**Company Orders:** Construct, Commerce, Mobilize.

**Stake Objective:** Gain recognition for securing or developing an important military transport corridor.

**Claimant Requirement:** Maintain a plausible military or logistical connection to the selected route.

**Material Consequence:** A legal construction operation may establish a Road, Bridge, or appropriate supply Improvement.

Recognition does not construct the infrastructure without the relevant project.

## L-O2 — Frontier Interception

**Type:** Open\
**Suit:** Warfare\
**Eligible Target:** A hostile patrol, raiding force, disputed border approach, or threatened settlement.\
**Company Orders:** Mobilize, Explore.

**Stake Objective:** Earn recognition for responding to a credible military threat.

**Claimant Requirement:** Maintain an eligible Force, defensive obligation, or appropriate mission access.

**Material Consequence:** A successful interception may preserve a settlement, secure a route, or remove the identified threat.

The enemy is not destroyed merely because the claimant wins a Warfare card contest.

## L-S1 — The Provincial Garrison

**Type:** Sponsored\
**Suit:** Authority\
**Eligible Target:** A settlement requesting protection, military administration, or garrison support.\
**Company Orders:** Politics, Recruit.

**Required Sponsor:** The relevant Local Authority or settlement government.

**Stake Objective:** Obtain recognition for an authorized garrison arrangement.

**Material Consequence:** A completed agreement may provide a regional recruitment charter, authorized garrison position, or temporary local militia contingent.

The sponsoring settlement retains political control unless the agreement explicitly transfers it.

## L-S2 — The Quartermaster's Route

**Type:** Sponsored\
**Suit:** Industry\
**Eligible Target:** An important supply junction, military storehouse, caravan source, or logistical route.\
**Company Orders:** Commerce, Mobilize, Construct.

**Required Sponsor:** A relevant quartermaster institution, military organization, or commercial supplier.

**Stake Objective:** Gain recognition for maintaining the sponsor's supply arrangements.

**Material Consequence:** A completed operation may establish a legal Supply connection, access to repairs, or an approved military supply agreement.

It does not generate automatic Gold or unrestricted replacement formations.

## L-X1 — Appointment of the Marshal

**Type:** Exclusive — Legions\
**Suit:** Authority\
**Eligible Target:** A disputed military command, army headquarters, or recognized appointment of a senior officer.\
**Company Orders:** Politics.

**Stake Objective:** Establish recognition of the Legions' proposed military appointment.

**Material Consequence:** A completed appointment may make one named commander or eligible signature formation available through the ordinary roster and military access rules.

It does not grant additional Doctrine commands or Command Points.

The appointed officer must still be legally included in an army.

## L-X2 — The Iron Offensive

**Type:** Exclusive — Legions\
**Suit:** Warfare\
**Eligible Target:** A strategically important military position reachable by a Legion Force or an established allied army.\
**Company Orders:** Mobilize, Politics, Explore.

**Stake Objective:** Establish recognition for the Legions' declared military campaign or regional security objective.

The PCs may undermine the operation through reconnaissance, diplomacy, or a suitable battle.

**Material Consequence:** A successful military operation may capture an eligible position, deny an opponent access to a military facility, or complete another stated military objective.

Winning the Warfare trick alone does not constitute a conquest.

During Act I, the Stake may concern preparation and public recognition of the planned offensive; actual territorial conquest must await a legal military operation.

# E.6 Concord — The Allied Compact

Concord prioritizes institutional cooperation, diplomatic recognition, mutually beneficial commerce, and military alliances.

Its Tasks provide ways to build coalition support without requiring direct territorial annexation.

## CO-O1 — The Shared Market

**Type:** Open\
**Suit:** Industry\
**Eligible Target:** A regional market, merchant coalition, trade junction, or recognized economic institution.\
**Company Orders:** Commerce, Construct.

**Stake Objective:** Gain recognition for organizing or supporting a cooperative trading arrangement.

**Claimant Requirement:** Maintain a legitimate commercial relationship with at least one participating institution.

**Material Consequence:** A completed agreement may provide trade access, a mercenary hiring contact, or authorization for a suitable commercial Improvement.

The Stake does not automatically generate passive Gold income.

## CO-O2 — Civic Mediation

**Type:** Open\
**Suit:** Authority\
**Eligible Target:** A dispute between settlements, guilds, Grand Factions, or other recognized political authorities.\
**Company Orders:** Politics.

**Stake Objective:** Gain recognition as an appropriate mediator of the dispute.

**Claimant Requirement:** Possess a legitimate diplomatic connection or invitation to the negotiation.

**Material Consequence:** A successful settlement may establish a treaty, Political Influence contact, concession, or recognized resolution of a local grievance.

Mediation does not automatically force either party to surrender territory or military assets.

## CO-S1 — The Union Charter

**Type:** Sponsored\
**Suit:** Authority\
**Eligible Target:** Two or more communities, civic institutions, or recognized political organizations considering formal cooperation.\
**Company Orders:** Politics, Commerce.

**Required Sponsor:** At least one participating institution authorizing negotiation of the proposed Charter.

**Stake Objective:** Earn recognition for organizing the proposed union or institutional agreement.

**Material Consequence:** A completed Charter may establish commercial access, defensive obligations, political cooperation, or a specified recruitment right.

The participating institutions retain their existing sovereignty unless the agreement explicitly provides otherwise.

## CO-S2 — The Mutual Defense Accord

**Type:** Sponsored\
**Suit:** Warfare\
**Eligible Target:** A settlement, important route, allied institution, or region facing a credible military threat.\
**Company Orders:** Politics, Mobilize.

**Required Sponsor:** A community or political authority seeking a formal defensive arrangement.

**Stake Objective:** Gain recognition as a legitimate defensive partner.

**Material Consequence:** A completed agreement may provide one temporary allied contingent or specified defensive recruitment right.

Actual deployment depends on the agreement, available formations, and normal point and slot restrictions.

The Accord does not create a free formation merely from its political marker.

## CO-X1 — The Hall of Delegates

**Type:** Exclusive — Concord\
**Suit:** Authority\
**Eligible Target:** An assembly of political representatives, disputed diplomatic institution, or regional council.\
**Company Orders:** Politics.

**Stake Objective:** Establish recognition of Concord's proposed role as convenor or coordinating authority.

**Material Consequence:** A completed diplomatic arrangement may provide an authorized Political Influence contact, a qualified Political Mandate for the company, or one specified institutional privilege.

A Political Mandate requires the relevant independent power to agree; recognition of the council does not automatically compel an unrelated faction to obey.

## CO-X2 — Accord of the Five Roads

**Type:** Exclusive — Concord\
**Suit:** Discovery\
**Eligible Target:** A network of important routes, trade connections, and frontier approaches.\
**Company Orders:** Explore, Commerce, Politics.

**Stake Objective:** Establish recognition of Concord's proposed regional travel and access agreement.

The title refers to the Compact's traditional ideal of connecting distant communities. It does not require five literal Roads to exist on the current board.

**Material Consequence:** A completed survey or treaty may establish specified passage rights, identify missing regional connections, or secure legal access to a trade route.

The agreement does not construct free Roads or automatically connect disconnected supply networks.
