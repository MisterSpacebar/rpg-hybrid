# APPENDIX Q — CAMPAIGN RECORDS AND TRACKING SHEETS

## Campaign Mode v4.4

Campaign Mode uses persistent records to track the development of Player Characters, military formations, territorial control, political relationships, and the wider strategic conflict.

These records allow decisions made during narrative adventures, structured battles, and Campaign Councils to carry forward throughout the campaign.

Not every table requires a separate physical sheet. Records may be combined into a campaign journal, shared spreadsheet, or other appropriate format.

The Company should maintain one collective record of its resources and military organization, while each player maintains their own Character and Detachment records.

The Game Master (GM) maintains records of participating Grand Factions, political Tasks, and the Principal Opposition.



---



# Q.1 Recordkeeping Principles

The following principles govern Campaign recordkeeping.

- Shared Company Resources: Gold, political agreements, infrastructure, and collectively owned military resources are recorded once for the Company.
- Individual Characters: Each PC tracks their own XP, Talents, equipment, and Critical Injuries.
- Individual Detachments: Each PC maintains their own deployment allowance, legal formation slots, and assigned units.
- Owned Roster: The Company may own more formations than it can deploy in a particular battle.
- Persistent Map: Political ownership, resource discoveries, and Improvements remain in place until legally changed.
- Separate Calendars: Company Orders refresh each Spring; NPC Maneuver decks refresh at the beginning of each Act.
- One Political Result: Every Campaign Turn produces exactly one colored or gray Act marker.
- Limited NPC Administration: Independent Grand Factions do not require complete Gold treasuries, equipment inventories, or individually tracked ordinary soldiers.

Permanent Character loss, replacement Characters, and Legacy Captains use the additional records in Q.7.1 and Q.9.1.

These records are used only when relevant and do not require ongoing maintenance during ordinary play.

Each Campaign Turn represents one Season.

Seasons advance in the following order:

Spring → Summer → Autumn → Winter → Spring

## Q.7.3 Hero Point Calculation

**Base Hero Points = max(100, XP Spent on Talents)**

**Final Hero Points = Base Hero Points + Equipped Equipment Points + Other Explicit Point Costs**

Keep the Character's **Total Earned XP** and **Total Spent XP** in the advancement record. Enter a separate **XP Spent on Talents** total for the Hero Points calculation; do not automatically substitute a spending total that includes other expenditures unless the RPG rules explicitly price that purchase as Talent XP.

Unspent XP does not increase battlefield Points. XP-funded Talents that have already contributed to the base value are **not** added a second time. Equipment and specifically listed additional costs are counted normally.

**Example:** A PC earned 155 XP, spent 125 on Talents, holds 30 unspent XP, and equips 20 Points of items. Its Hero value is 125 + 20 = **145 Points**, excluding any separately stated additional costs.

## Q.7.5 — Permanent Character Loss and Replacement

Supplementary record. Use only when a PC permanently dies or retires.

Each Player Character has three Critical Injury slots.

Their first and second Critical Injuries are survivable under the normal General Adventuring rules.

Upon receiving a third Critical Injury, the Character permanently dies or retires, and the Character Sheet is retired.

A replacement Character may be introduced according to Campaign Mode's Character Replacement rules.

The new Character normally begins with XP equal to the Company's current shared XP Benchmark and with equipment appropriate to the current campaign stage.

They begin with no Critical Injuries.

## Q.9.2 Formation Casualties and Replenishment

When a Unit survives a battle but has lost Models or accumulated Wounds, it is **Depleted**. Restoring it to its purchased full strength costs **half its normal full recruitment Gold cost, rounded up**, and does not require a Recruit Order. The Company must have an appropriate recovery source. If no losses were suffered, no replenishment payment is required.

When a formation is completely **Defeated**, roll once for the Unit (a Double Formation also rolls only once):

| D6 | Status | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Destroyed | Remove from the Owned Roster; legal new acquisition or specific recovery required |
| 2–4 | Mauled | Retained but unavailable until paid recovery at half normal full recruitment Gold, rounded up |
| 5–6 | Scattered | Retained but unavailable; returns after one completed Turn or eligible early recovery for 1 Gold |

A surviving formation has no risk of becoming Destroyed solely from its normal replenishment. Replenishment and paid recovery require access to a suitable source and eligible Upkeep. A formation does not receive the normal free full-strength reset simply because a battle ends. Named Assets and Legacy Captains follow their own explicit additional restrictions.

## Q.9.4 — Legacy Captain Register

Supplementary record. Use when a surviving former PC returns as a named military Hero.

A Legacy Captain is a **one-Model Hero** converted using one of the five **Class Templates** in Appendix P, not a generic Captain automatically granted unrelated abilities. The conversion retains its actual Ancestry, original Body/Agility/Mind/Spirit values, Base Class, Subclass Signature, and selected previously learned Commands, passives, Spells, or Formulas. The full cost, including equipment and retained purchased abilities, may not exceed **225 Points**.

The Class Template already includes Hero status and the appropriate Class/Subclass abilities. **Do not add the generic Captain's Heroic Commission, Legacy Distinction, or complimentary Captain Commands.** The previous simplified four-step method is superseded by Appendix P's six-step conversion.

# Q.20 GM and Company Closeout Checklist

- Confirm the Act marker and any Development draw belong to the appropriate political Grand Faction.
- Apply only legal NPC Maneuver effects to targets committed before the Company's operation.
- Update the Company treasury, unit status, agreements, territorial controllers, and accessible resources.
- Record surviving-formation half-cost replenishment and defeated-formation D6 casualty outcomes separately.
- Record XP earned, XP spent on Talents, unspent XP, and equipment before computing deployed Hero Points.
- Record newly revealed opposing factions/Doctrines, available military sources, army slots, and applicable GM CP.
- Advance the Season; refresh Company Orders only on Spring and NPC Maneuver decks only when entering a new Act.
- At the end of an Act, resolve Agenda or Contested rewards and the beginning-of-next-Act advancement and Dividend exactly once.
