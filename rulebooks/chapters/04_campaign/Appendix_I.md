# APPENDIX I — GRAND FACTION CHARTERS

## Campaign Mode v4.3 — revised

Grand Faction Charters represent the political and military privileges granted to the PC Company by its sponsoring Grand Faction.

A Charter becomes active at the beginning of Act II, when the company is formally established.

Each Charter provides:

1. One persistent passive company benefit.
2. One shared Charter command for structured combat.
3. Access to the normal Patron recruitment benefits established by the core campaign rules.

There are five Charters, one for each Grand Faction.



---



# I.1 General Charter Rules

## I.1.1 Receiving a Charter

At the beginning of Act II, the company receives the Charter associated with its affiliated Grand Faction.

The company also receives:

- Two appropriate Patron recruitment slots per PC.
- Two free eligible Patron formations in total.
- The right to purchase additional eligible Patron Core formations under the normal recruitment rules.

The Charter does not grant unrestricted ownership of the sponsoring faction's other armies, institutions, or territory.

## I.1.2 Charter Passives

Charter passive effects are available beginning in Act II.

Unless stated otherwise, a passive effect that operates once per Act refreshes at the beginning of each new Act.

Unused uses do not carry forward.

A passive benefit does not create another Company Order or Faction Maneuver.

## I.1.3 Shared Charter Commands

Each Charter grants one shared command option.

Represent the command with one physical card or a dedicated command section on the Charter card.

The command is available to the company collectively, not once per PC.

Unless the command specifies otherwise:

- It costs **1 CP**.
- It may be used by a participating PC during the appropriate combat timing window.
- It becomes exhausted after use.
- It readies at the beginning of the next Round.
- It may be used at most once per Round.
- It does not grant additional CP or unit activations.

A shared Charter Command is separate from each PC's personal Doctrine Commands and does not add to their CP. The activating PC spends CP from their **own normal 2-CP allocation** (unless an explicit campaign effect changes it). It is not duplicated by multiple PCs and readies once at the start of each Round.

Battlefield Edges now reduce the selected opposing participant's CP for one Round; **they do not pre-exhaust the shared Charter Command**. Having a physical Charter card is optional; Command availability and the CP resource are separate.

The Chartered faction's regular military tags and unit profiles remain relevant where a command specifies them.

# I.2 Unbound — Charter of the Free Peoples

**Company Identity:** Frontier independence, exploration, irregular warfare, and cooperation with local communities.

## Passive — Frontier Contacts

Once per Act, after the company successfully resolves an unresolved exploration Site, it may Scout one different legally accessible unresolved Site or nearby strategic location.

The additional Scout reveals appropriate information concerning the location, such as its principal danger, political significance, or available route.

The additional Site remains unresolved.

The passive does not award another Discovery or grant an additional Explore Order.

If no appropriate unresolved Site remains, the company may instead identify one relevant frontier threat or regional military preparation.

## Shared Command — Cover of the Wilds

**Cost:** 1 CP\
**Timing:** Reaction, when a friendly Infantry Unit is targeted by an eligible ranged Attack, before the Attack roll.

**Effect:** That Unit gains **Cover +1** against this Attack. The granted Cover stacks with existing Cover ranks, including eligible physical Cover and elevation. It permits an otherwise unavailable Evade Save against eligible ranged Attacks as defined by the combat rules, but never grants a second Evade Save or permits rerolling any die twice.

The command becomes exhausted until the next Round.

**Strategic Character:** The Unbound favor exploration, concealment, and defensive use of terrain rather than direct military concentration.



---



# I.3 Conclave — Charter of the Learned Orders

**Company Identity:** Research, technical specialization, advanced equipment, and disciplined industrial development.

## Passive — Research Commission

Once per Act, when the company selects Develop, reduce its eligible equipment-crafting or formation-upgrade expenditure by **1 Gold**.

The reduction applies once to the company's combined eligible expenditure, not once per PC.

It may combine with Develop Supremacy, subject to the normal minimum expenditure of 1 Gold.

The passive does not reduce the cost of purchasing Flex slots.

It does not permit additional Major or Minor Development projects.

## Shared Command — Calculated Volley

**Cost:** 1 CP\
**Timing:** Before a friendly unit makes a ranged attack or an offensive spell attack that uses an attack roll.

**Effect:** The selected Attack gains **Accurate +1**, adding to any Accurate rank it already possesses. Accurate rerolls are limited to natural 1s, and no individual die may be rerolled more than once. The bonus applies once to the combined Attack pool, not once per attacking Model.

The command becomes exhausted until the next Round.

**Strategic Character:** The Conclave emphasizes reliable ranged attacks, technical development, and specialist military equipment.



---



# I.4 Wayfarers — Charter of the Oathbound Roads

**Company Identity:** Mobility, traditional alliances, clan protection, and the defense of allied communities.

## Passive — Oathbound Passage

Once per Act, during an eligible company movement operation, the PC Party or Company Force may move **one additional legal hex** if the movement passes through an unclaimed region or territory where the company has recognized allied passage rights.

This bonus does not stack with the additional movement provided by a Road.

The operation gains at most one additional hex from these two sources.

The passive cannot cross impassable terrain, bypass a hostile blockade, or provide passage through territory where the company has no legitimate access.

## Shared Command — Oathguard

**Cost:** 1 CP\
**Timing:** Reaction, when a friendly Unit within 6" of **another friendly Unit** (including an allied Hero) is about to make Armor Saves against an Attack, before Armor dice are rolled.

**Effect:** The defender gains **Tough +1** for those Armor Saves. Add this rank to other eligible Tough ranks, applying the result once to the relevant save pool. Tough rerolls natural 1s only; no die may be rerolled more than once.

The command becomes exhausted until the next Round.

**Strategic Character:** The Wayfarers excel at mutual protection, coordinated movement, and fighting beside trusted allies.



---



# I.5 Legions — Charter of the Military Standard

**Company Identity:** Organized recruitment, disciplined formations, strategic movement, and professional military organization.

## Passive — Muster Discipline

Once per Act, when the company selects Recruit, reduce its combined eligible recruitment expenditure by **1 Gold**.

The reduction is company-wide.

It may combine with Recruit Supremacy, subject to the normal minimum expenditure of 1 Gold.

The passive does not create an additional formation or recruitment opportunity.

## Shared Command — Drilled Advance

**Cost:** 1 CP\
**Timing:** During the Movement Phase of a friendly unit's activation, before completing its normal Move.

**Effect:** Increase that unit's permitted normal movement distance by **2 inches** for the current Move.

This bonus does not apply to a Charge.

The command does not permit the unit to enter engagement through an action that would not ordinarily allow it.

It does not change the unit's Charge, Impact, or other attack characteristics.

The command becomes exhausted until the next Round.

**Strategic Character:** The Legions emphasize predictable force development, organized battlefield movement, and maintaining coherent formations.



---



# I.6 Concord — Charter of the Allied Compact

**Company Identity:** Diplomatic cooperation, shared defense, commercial relationships, and coordinated military support.

## Passive — Recognized Envoys

Once per Act, after the company successfully completes a Commerce or Politics operation involving an external Local Authority or participating Grand Faction, it may place or relocate one Political Influence marker belonging to its affiliated Grand Faction.

The marker must be placed at a location connected to that successful agreement and where the company has legitimate political access.

The normal maximum of two Political Influence markers remains in effect.

This passive does not grant another full agreement, Political Mandate, territorial claim, or allied formation.

## Shared Command — Coordinated Response

**Cost:** 1 CP\
**Timing:** Immediately after a friendly unit completes a normal Move.

**Effect:** Choose another friendly Unit within 6" of the Unit that moved. **The chosen Unit must not be Engaged.** It may immediately reposition up to **2"**, using a legal path and maintaining required Coherency.

This movement is not a Charge, cannot enter Engagement, cannot disengage an Engaged Unit, and cannot override Withdrawn or other movement restrictions. It does not count as the second Unit's normal activation, allow an Attack or Mission Action, or trigger this Command again.

The command becomes exhausted until the next Round.

**Strategic Character:** Concord emphasizes the cooperation of several friendly formations rather than relying on one unit's individual excellence.

# I.8 Charters and Political Control

A Charter represents sponsorship and established privileges, not unrestricted control of the Grand Faction's government.

The PC Company may gain access to the sponsor's controlled regional resources through its Charter and legal supply connections.

The sponsor's Capital and other territory remain politically controlled by the Grand Faction itself.

Territorial changes negotiated or achieved by the company are recorded under the appropriate political controller.

A Charter does not automatically give the PCs a separate Faction Maneuver deck.

The company continues using Company Orders, while the Grand Faction's political standing is represented by the company's eligible Act markers and its persistent regional position.

## I.8.1 Changing Political Relationships

A campaign may include political disputes between the company and its sponsor.

Such events should be resolved through actual narrative or campaign objectives.

The GM should not remove a Charter's existing mechanical benefits arbitrarily merely because a political Stake was lost.

A fundamental break in sponsorship requires a significant campaign event and should establish the resulting rights and obligations explicitly.

# I.9 Chartered Company Example

A four-PC company affiliated with Concord enters Act II.

It receives the Charter of the Allied Compact.

Each PC gains two appropriate Patron recruitment slots.

The company collectively receives two free Patron formations.

The company also gains the passive Recognized Envoys benefit and the shared Coordinated Response command.

During a later Politics operation, the company negotiates a legitimate defensive agreement with a Local Authority.

The operation succeeds.

The company obtains the agreement's principal political result and may use Recognized Envoys to place one legal Political Influence marker at the settlement.

If the company also obtained Politics Supremacy, that additional minor concession remains separate.

During a structured battle, one PC spends 1 CP to activate Coordinated Response.

A friendly infantry unit completes its normal movement.

A second friendly formation within 6 inches repositions up to 2 inches without charging or engaging.

The shared command becomes exhausted.

No other PC can use Coordinated Response again during that same Round.

The command readies at the beginning of the next Round.
