# APPENDIX N — CAMPAIGN COUNCIL AND TURN PROCEDURE

## Campaign Mode v4.4

The Campaign Council manages the transition between narrative adventures, structured battles, and the strategic campaign.

Each Campaign Turn represents one Season.

The company receives one Company Order, and each participating non-PC Grand Faction commits one Faction Maneuver card.

Only one political Stake is awarded during the Turn.

The Council ensures every strategic action has a declared objective, every faction's involvement is determined before the session's outcome, and the persistent map changes accordingly.



---



# N.1 Structure of a Campaign Turn

A Campaign Turn consists of three stages.

| **Stage**                   | **Purpose**                                                                                          |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **I. Preparation Council**  | Select the Stake, commit faction cards, choose the Company Order, and establish the upcoming mission |
| **II. Campaign Play**       | Resolve the company's expedition, political objective, military engagement, or other operation       |
| **III. Resolution Council** | Settle the Stake, apply faction map actions, update the campaign, award XP, and advance the calendar |

The Preparation Council for the next Turn normally occurs at the conclusion of the previous gaming session.

The Resolution Council normally occurs when the current Turn's mission has been completed.

A Campaign Turn may span multiple gaming sessions when a major adventure or battle remains unresolved.

No additional Turn passes merely because a session ends.



---



# N.2 Information Available to the Players

At the beginning of each Turn, the following information is normally public:

- The current Act and Season.
- The Act Agenda.
- The current Task Display.
- The Council Influence holder.
- The selected Active Stake and its primary target.
- The faction designated as Lead.
- Known territorial ownership and political Influence markers.
- Known faction treaties and military obligations.
- General declared NPC strategic intentions.

The following may remain concealed:

- The exact Strength of unrevealed faction cards.
- The Principal Opposition's longer-term plans.
- Undiscovered exploration Sites or hidden military assets.
- Unrevealed narrative complications.
- Information specifically concealed by a mission or political agreement.

A player who directs an independent NPC through Council Influence or a Political Mandate may inspect that faction's remaining available Maneuver cards for the purpose of choosing its commitment.

This does not reveal the hands of unrelated factions.

Once a card has been resolved, it enters that faction's visible exhausted-card area.



---



# N.3 Preparation Council

## Step 1 — Review the Campaign State

Confirm:

1. The current Act and Season.
2. The active Agenda.
3. The company treasury and applicable restrictions.
4. The availability of Company Orders.
5. The remaining Maneuver cards of participating NPC factions.
6. The locations of strategic Forces.
7. Relevant Political Control and Influence markers.
8. Outstanding treaties, Mandates, prepared objectives, and military contracts.

Resolve any beginning-of-Act procedures before preparing the Turn.

## Step 2 — Review the Task Display

Reveal up to three available eligible Task cards.

Each Task should identify:

- Its classification.
- Its leading suit.
- Its political objective.
- Its eligible target type.
- Its requirements for claiming the Stake.
- Any additional material or military consequence.

A Task is not required to target a specific named tile.

Choose targets based on the current strategic map.

If a Task cannot legally apply anywhere in the current campaign, replace it with an eligible Task from the remaining pool.

If no replacement is available, remove the invalid Task and continue with the remaining display.

An eligible Task may involve more than one region or political actor.

## Step 3 — Reveal Any Neutral Opportunity

If the Turn contains a scheduled Neutral Opportunity, reveal it before the company chooses its Order.

The Opportunity should state:

- The objective.
- Its eligibility and success requirements.
- Its reward.
- Any relevant map location.
- Whether it replaces the normal Stake if completed.

Neutral Opportunities do not create additional Campaign Turns.

The PCs may attempt the Neutral Opportunity instead of contesting the regular political Stake.

## Step 4 — Council Influence Chooses the Stake

The Council Influence token determines which side chooses the Active Stake.

The holder selects one displayed eligible Task.

They declare:

1. The selected Task.
2. The specific legal primary target.
3. Any secondary locations relevant to the Task.
4. The Stake's leading suit.
5. One plausible participating non-PC Grand Faction as the Lead.

The Lead must have an appropriate political connection or plausible means of pursuing the objective.

The primary target is locked before the company chooses its Order.

A target may not be relocated afterward simply to produce a more favorable result.

If the Stake involves an edge, army, or institution rather than an ordinary hex, identify its primary relevant map position or declare that it has no geographic target.

## Step 5 — Determine Local Influence

Identify which participating factions qualify for Local Influence.

A faction receives +1 to its committed card Strength if it has Political Control or a Political Influence marker on the primary target or an adjacent hex.

Only one such bonus may apply.

Record qualification now.

The bonus does not change if an army moves or a Political Influence marker is placed later in the Turn.

For Stakes without a meaningful geographic target, Local Influence normally does not apply.

## Step 6 — Commit the Principal Opposition

The GM selects one available Faction Maneuver card for the Principal Opposition.

The commitment is recorded face down.

The GM declares its general strategic intent, including the intended basic map action or target where applicable.

If the Principal Opposition is the Lead, it may commit a matching-suit or off-suit card according to the ordinary rules.

Otherwise, it may Follow with an eligible matching-suit card or Pivot.

It must respect the same geographic and political eligibility rules as any other faction.

## Step 7 — Direct Independent Factions

Resolve Political Mandates first.

If the PCs possess a valid Mandate, they select the named faction's eligible Maneuver commitment.

The Mandate is then spent.

Next, the Council Influence holder may direct one eligible independent Grand Faction not already directed through the same commitment.

The controlling side selects its card and declares whether it Follows or Pivots.

Its choice must respect the faction's standing obligations and interests.

The GM resolves the remaining independent powers using the public faction priority procedure.

Each participating independent faction commits exactly one available card.

The selected Lead must be given its legal commitment during this process; being designated Lead does not permit a second card.

If no independent power remains eligible for direction, the Council Influence holder receives no replacement action.

## Step 8 — Lock NPC Strategic Intentions

Record the intended target of each faction's basic Maneuver action.

For example:

- A Discovery card names a Site or intelligence objective.
- An Industry card identifies a legal Improvement location or industrial contact.
- An Authority card identifies a political contact or Influence marker location.
- A Warfare card identifies the Force marker and intended one-hex movement.

These intentions are established before the PCs select their Company Order.

If a declared action becomes illegal because of an actual campaign event before resolution, the action fails unless a specific rule permits a legal adjustment.

The card remains spent.

The GM should not retroactively change its destination merely to avoid the consequences of the PCs' successful mission.

## Step 9 — The PCs Select a Company Order

The PCs choose one unused Company Order.

They declare:

1. The selected Order.
2. Whether it qualifies for Supremacy.
3. Their intended principal strategic action.
4. The objective or location being targeted.
5. Whether they intend to contest the Active Stake, pursue a Neutral Opportunity, or undertake an unrelated action.

For Recruit and Develop, individual PC choices may be finalized when the Order is actually resolved.

The company does not have to contest the Active Stake.

However, an unrelated Company Order cannot automatically claim it.

## Step 10 — Establish the Scenario

Determine whether the company's action requires:

- Narrative roleplaying.
- A skill challenge or investigation.
- Structured combat.
- Strategic construction or recruitment.
- Ordinary campaign adjudication.

The GM uses the declared situation, faction commitments, map position, and relevant Task requirements to prepare the next session.

The Preparation Council then ends.

# N.4 Campaign Play

Resolve the company's chosen objective using the appropriate normal rules.

An Explore Order may generate an adventure through an unresolved Site.

A Politics Order may involve negotiations with competing political representatives.

A Mobilize Order may initiate structured combat.

A Develop or Recruit Order may be resolved without a major encounter if it involves ordinary purchases and permitted projects.

The consequences of player decisions should remain meaningful even when the chosen operation is not associated with the current Stake.

## N.4.1 Completing a Political Stake

The company may claim a political Stake when:

- Its selected operation is eligible for the Task.
- It satisfies the Task's political requirements.
- It completes any required mission or challenge.
- It meets applicable sponsorship requirements.

When the PCs act for their own affiliated Grand Faction, an eligible success awards that faction's colored Act marker.

When they act on behalf of an eligible independent faction, the marker belongs to that faction.

The PCs do not automatically claim a Stake merely because they hold Council Influence or direct the faction that wins the trick.

## N.4.2 Mission Failure

If the PCs fail or abandon their mission, apply the actual narrative consequences.

They do not automatically invalidate NPC card commitments.

If the political Stake remains available, the eligible NPC factions resolve it using their committed cards.

If the PCs destroyed or otherwise conclusively invalidated the objective, no NPC may claim it merely by playing a high-Strength card.

In that case, the Stake is Neutral unless a specific Task consequence identifies another eligible outcome.

## N.4.3 Missions Spanning Multiple Sessions

A major mission may require several sessions.

Until it is completed:

- The same Campaign Turn remains active.
- The same Company Order remains committed.
- The existing NPC commitments remain in effect.
- No additional Campaign Turn XP is awarded.
- No additional provincial dividends occur.
- The Season does not advance.

Normal RPG rewards and character consequences may still occur during the adventure according to the core RPG rules.

# N.5 Resolution Council

## Step 1 — Record the Company Result

Determine whether the company successfully completed its declared operation.

Apply appropriate immediate narrative or strategic consequences.

Examples include:

- Obtaining a Discovery.
- Crafting equipment.
- Recruiting a formation.
- Establishing a treaty.
- Winning a structured battle.
- Constructing an Improvement.
- Acquiring a mercenary contract.

Record the result even if the company did not contest the Stake.

## Step 2 — Resolve a Declared Neutral Opportunity

If the PCs declared an eligible scheduled Neutral Opportunity as their primary objective and completed it:

1. Record a **gray Neutral result**, to be awarded once during Resolution Step 6 with the Opportunity's stated reward.
2. Do not resolve the normal political Stake for a second colored marker.
3. Do not award an automatic Development draw.
4. Preserve all independently legal basic NPC Maneuver actions.

If the Opportunity fails, is ignored, or is abandoned, the normal political Stake remains eligible for a PC claim or NPC contest. A Company mission cannot score both the Neutral Opportunity and the normal colored Stake in one Turn.

## Step 3 — Determine PC Political Stake Eligibility

If no Neutral Opportunity was completed, determine whether the Company completed the Active Stake's actual political requirements through its declared operation. If successful:

1. Identify the eligible political recipient: usually the PCs' affiliated Grand Faction, but possibly an independent faction for whom they acted.
2. Record the **colored political winner**, to receive its Act marker and **one Development reward draw** in Resolution Step 6.
3. Resolve any separate material outcome only if its printed requirements and the mission outcome were satisfied.
4. Do not award another marker from the NPC trick; the NPC basic Maneuver actions still resolve.

Directing an independent Maneuver or holding Council Influence is not itself a PC political victory. If the PCs have not qualified, compare eligible NPC commitments in the usual manner.

## Step 4 — Reveal Faction Maneuver Cards

Reveal all committed NPC Maneuver cards.

Identify:

- The designated Lead.
- The Stake's leading suit.
- Which factions Followed.
- Which factions Pivoted.
- Any eligible off-suit Lead.
- Which factions remain politically eligible after the session's events.

Apply each faction's previously recorded Local Influence qualification.

## Step 5 — Resolve the Trick

If the PCs or Neutral Opportunity have not already determined the marker, compare eligible NPC commitments.

Matching-suit cards take precedence over off-suit commitments.

The highest eligible modified Strength wins.

Apply the established tie-breakers:

1. Eligible incumbent defender.
2. Eligible designated Lead.
3. Neutral result if unresolved.

If no eligible matching-suit claimant exists, an eligible off-suit Lead may claim the Stake.

If no eligible claimant succeeds, record a gray Neutral result for Resolution Step 6.

## Step 6 — Award One Act Marker

Resolve the previously determined result **once**: place exactly one colored or gray marker on the Act Track and record its recipient. A colored winner receives exactly one Development draw; a completed Neutral Opportunity receives its own stated reward and a gray marker but no automatic Development draw. These rewards are not awarded earlier when the result is merely identified.

Apply any separately satisfied material Task consequences; do not duplicate a Neutral Opportunity reward already awarded in this step.

A normal colored political Stake grants the Development reward established in Appendix H, not unrelated territory, formations, infrastructure, or automatic material Task consequences.

## Step 7 — Resolve NPC Maneuver Effects

Every participating NPC faction resolves the basic effect of its committed card, if still legal.

| **Suit**  | **Basic Effect**                                     |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Discovery | Scout or gather intelligence                         |
| Industry  | Eligible Minor Improvement or industrial alternative |
| Authority | Political Influence or minor contact                 |
| Warfare   | Move one legal hex with a Force marker               |

Resolve actions using their previously declared targets.

Apply relevant construction limits, marker limits, access restrictions, and geography.

A faction that lost the Stake still receives its legal basic Maneuver effect.

A faction that won the Stake does not receive an extra Maneuver effect.

## Step 8 — Update the Strategic Map

Apply all legitimate changes to:

- Individual hex control.
- Provincial Control.
- Force positions.
- Roads and Bridges.
- Improvements.
- Political Influence.
- Exploration Sites.
- Resource access.
- Agreements and recruitment rights.
- Named military assets and casualties.

Provincial Control is recalculated whenever relevant hex ownership changes.

Resource access is updated using the current controlling powers and valid connections.

## Step 9 — Resolve Economy and Recovery

Update:

- Company Gold.
- Military purchases.
- Equipment and crafting expenditure.
- Contract payments.
- Recovery costs.
- Available and unavailable formations.
- Temporary rewards and agreements.

Resolve eligible paid recovery of Mauled and Scattered formations under the normal casualty rules.

Temporary contracts expire when their specified duration ends.

## Step 10 — Award Experience

Each participating PC receives **5 XP** for the completed Campaign Turn.

Record any additional milestone rewards expressly granted by the scenario.

A Campaign Turn does not award additional standard campaign XP solely because it required multiple gaming sessions.

## Step 11 — Refresh the Task Display

Remove the resolved Active Task from the current Act's Task pool.

Replenish the public display up to three Tasks from the remaining eligible pool.

A Task consumed during a Turn in which a Neutral Opportunity prevailed is still removed. The political contest was displaced by the Neutral result rather than repeated in another Turn.

Do not automatically replace the Task with a second scoring opportunity during the same Turn.

## Step 12 — Advance the Calendar

Advance to the next Season.

If the new Season is Spring, refresh all seven Company Orders.

Faction Maneuver cards do not refresh at Spring unless a new Act also begins.

If the completed Turn was the last Turn of the Act, resolve the Act before preparing the next Turn.

## Step 13 — Pass Council Influence

Pass the Council Influence token to the other side.

If the PCs held it during the completed Turn, the GM receives it for the next Turn.

If the GM held it, the PCs receive it.

Do not reset Council Influence at an Act boundary.

The next Preparation Council may now begin.

# N.6 Beginning a New Act

When an Act begins, resolve the following in order.

1. Complete the previous Act's political results and rewards.
2. Award 5 Act Advancement XP to each participating PC when entering Acts II, III, or IV.
3. Apply Provincial Dividends where appropriate.
4. Activate any newly available Company progression.
5. Apply the Principal Opposition's recruitment-slot progression, military identity deadlines, and Doctrine selection (the GM selects the Act III Doctrine **before** the PCs select theirs).
6. Reveal the new Act Agenda.
7. Prepare the new Act Task pool and public Display.
8. Refresh all participating NPC Maneuver decks.
9. Resolve expired Act-duration Political Mandates and temporary agreements.
10. Continue with the existing Council Influence holder.

### Provincial Dividends

At the beginning of Acts II, III, and IV, the Company receives **1 Gold per participating PC for every Province** controlled by its affiliated Grand Faction. There is **no cap**. A Province is majority-controlled when the Grand Faction owns at least two of its three hexes.

Non-PC Grand Factions with eligible Province control receive the current abstract military repair or reinforcement benefit from Appendix O rather than a separate Gold treasury. These benefits do not create additional Company Orders, Maneuvers, or Act markers.

### Company Progression

**Act II:** The company receives its Charter and Patron benefits.

**Act III:** PCs gain their initial Doctrines, and the Principal Opposition activates its first Doctrine.

**Act IV:** PCs gain their next Doctrine benefits, and the Principal Opposition activates its second Doctrine.

Apply the Grand Campaign's additional progression where specified.

### Order Refresh

Beginning a new Act does not automatically refresh Company Orders.

If the Act begins during Summer, Autumn, or Winter, the company continues using its current Campaign Year's remaining Orders.



---



# N.7 Ending an Act

At the conclusion of an Act:

1. Count the colored Act markers belonging to each participating Grand Faction.
2. Ignore gray Neutral markers when determining the faction totals.
3. Identify the faction with the highest colored-marker count.
4. Apply the Agenda victory reward if there is a unique winner.
5. If the highest count is tied, resolve a Contested Act.
6. Record persistent territorial, political, and military consequences.
7. Clear the completed Act Track.
8. Begin the next Act using the normal procedure.

### Contested Act

When the highest colored-marker total is tied, no faction receives the full Agenda victory reward.

Every participating Grand Faction receives **one** appropriate minor Development consolation.

The Principal Opposition receives no additional consolation merely because it is controlled by the GM.

These are Act-resolution benefits, not additional seasonal Maneuvers.

# N.8 Worked Example — Political Council

The PCs are affiliated with Concord.

The Principal Opposition is the Iron Regency of the Legions.

The Conclave and Wayfarers are independent powers.

The campaign is in Act III of a Standard Campaign.

The PCs hold Council Influence.

## Preparation Council

Three Tasks are available:

- A Discovery Task concerning missing surveyors.
- An Authority Task concerning a regional council.
- A Warfare Task concerning a disputed crossing.

The PCs choose the Warfare Task.

They designate a legal disputed crossing near Stonebridge and nominate the Wayfarers as Lead.

The Wayfarers maintain an eligible protection agreement involving the crossing.

The PCs use Council Influence to commit the Wayfarers' Warfare 5 card.

The Wayfarers qualify for +1 Local Influence because they maintain nearby political control.

The GM commits the Iron Regency's Warfare 6 card.

The Regency does not qualify for Local Influence.

The Conclave's interests do not require it to defend the crossing. It commits an Industry card and declares a legal industrial project elsewhere.

The PCs then select Develop (3).

They do not intend to complete the Warfare Task themselves.

## Campaign Play

Each PC resolves their appropriate Development projects.

Two PCs upgrade existing formations, one purchases a Flex slot, and one crafts equipment.

Apply the normal Gold costs and checks.

## Resolution Council

The Wayfarers reveal Warfare 5, gaining +1 Local Influence for a final Strength of 6.

The Iron Regency reveals Warfare 6 for a final Strength of 6.

Both commitments are eligible.

The Wayfarers are the recognized incumbent defenders of the crossing, so they win the tie.

Place one Wayfarer-colored Act marker and draw one Development reward for the Wayfarers.

Now resolve the basic faction actions:

- The Wayfarers move one Force marker one legal hex.
- The Regency moves one Force marker one legal hex.
- The Conclave completes its legal Industry action.

No political ownership changes automatically.

Record the PCs' Development results.

Award 5 XP per PC.

Advance the Season and pass Council Influence to the GM.

# N.9 Worked Example — PCs Claim the Stake

During a later Turn, the GM holds Council Influence.

The GM selects an Authority Stake involving a regional military charter.

The Iron Regency is designated Lead and commits Authority 5.

The Conclave Follows using Authority 4.

The Wayfarers Pivot using Discovery 3.

The PCs select Politics (6).

They approach the Local Authority and negotiate an agreement that satisfies the Task's eligible Open requirements.

The negotiation succeeds.

The PCs receive the Concord-colored Act marker and one Development draw for completing the political objective.

The Regency's Authority 5 does not override the completed RPG mission.

However, each NPC still receives its legal basic Maneuver action.

The Regency may establish its declared minor political contact, the Conclave may perform its Authority effect, and the Wayfarers may scout.

The agreement may also grant a specified regional military recruitment right, depending on the actual terms.

The company receives its political success and any eligible negotiated benefit, but does not automatically receive additional military formations beyond those provided by the agreement.

# N.10 Worked Example — Neutral Opportunity

During Act II, the PCs discover a Neutral Treasury Opportunity.

It offers a reward of 2 Gold per PC for recovering valuable supplies without delivering them to any participating Grand Faction.

The GM holds Council Influence and chooses an unrelated Industry Stake.

The normal NPC commitments are made.

The PCs select Explore and pursue the Treasury Opportunity.

The adventure succeeds.

For four PCs, the company receives **8 Gold**.

Place one gray Neutral marker on the Act Track.

No NPC receives a colored marker that Turn.

The committed NPC cards still resolve their legal basic map actions.

The selected Industry Task is removed at the end of the Turn.

The company receives its normal completed-Turn XP.



---



# N.11 Worked Example — Political Consequences for the Principal Opposition

At the beginning of Act III, the Iron Regency holds a military manufacturing agreement with the Conclave.

That agreement allows the Regency to field one specified Conclave Elite Support formation as part of a legal army.

Later, the PCs undertake a successful Politics operation with the Conclave.

They negotiate an exclusive manufacturing agreement that supersedes the Regency's previous military contract when its current obligations expire.

Record the new agreement and its expiration conditions.

During the next eligible army-building step, the GM must remove the Conclave contingent from the Regency's available outside recruitment options.

The Regency still retains its normal legal Legion units, its complete scenario point allowance, and its Doctrine commands.

The GM may substitute another legal formation, but cannot continue including the borrowed Conclave unit without a valid source.

This represents a meaningful political consequence without reducing the enemy's baseline deployment ceiling.

The change persists into Act IV unless another campaign event reverses it.

# N.12 Worked Example — Act and Year Boundaries

A **Local Campaign** has Acts of 3, 4, 5, and 6 Turns and begins in Spring on Turn 1. It is therefore a good example of why the Campaign Year and Act clocks are independent.

| Turn | Act | Season | Council Influence (PCs begin) | Company Orders refresh? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | I | Spring | PCs | Yes |
| 2 | I | Summer | GM | No |
| 3 | I | Autumn | PCs | No |
| 4 | II | Winter | GM | No |
| 5 | II | Spring | PCs | Yes |
| 6 | II | Summer | GM | No |
| 7 | II | Autumn | PCs | No |
| 8 | III | Winter | GM | No |
| 9 | III | Spring | PCs | Yes |

When Turn 3 ends, Act I is resolved and Act II begins on Turn 4. Apply the Act II Charter and Patron benefits, 5 Advancement XP per PC, any Provincial Dividend, a new Agenda, a new Task pool, and the NPC Maneuver-deck refresh. **Do not refresh Company Orders**: Turn 4 is Winter, not Spring.

Company Orders refresh at the beginning of Turn 5. When Act II ends after Turn 7, Act III begins on Turn 8, again without refreshing Orders. The Opposition chooses its first Doctrine before PCs choose their own. NPC decks refresh on the Act boundary, but Company Orders refresh only when Turn 9 begins in Spring.

# N.13 Fast Strategic Turns

Not every Campaign Turn requires a full gaming session.

A Turn may be resolved quickly when:

- The Company Order involves a straightforward purchase or project.
- No major RPG encounter is required.
- The factions' commitments have clear legal outcomes.
- No consequential battle or negotiation must be played.

Resolve the entire Turn using the normal Council steps.

Do not skip the NPC card commitments, political Stake, map updates, or upkeep simply because the company chose an uncomplicated Order.

Multiple fast Turns may be resolved during one Campaign Council.

Each remains a separate Campaign Turn with its own Order, Maneuvers, Stake, Act marker, XP award, and Season advancement.

# N.14 Campaign Council Checklist

## Preparation

- Confirm Act, Season, Agenda, and Council Influence.
- Review the three-card Task Display.
- Reveal any Neutral Opportunity.
- Select the Active Stake and legal target.
- Designate the Lead.
- Record Local Influence eligibility.
- Commit the Principal Opposition card.
- Resolve Political Mandates and directed independent factions.
- Commit remaining independent faction cards.
- Declare NPC map-action targets.
- Choose the Company Order.
- Determine Supremacy.
- Establish the upcoming mission.

## Resolution

- Resolve the company mission.
- Apply completed player objectives.
- Determine PC Stake or Neutral Opportunity success.
- Reveal NPC Maneuver cards.
- Resolve the political trick if necessary.
- Place exactly one Act marker and award one Development draw if colored.
- Resolve legal NPC map actions.
- Update territory, provinces, infrastructure, and resources.
- Update political agreements and military access.
- Apply Gold expenditures and rewards.
- Resolve casualties and recovery.
- Award 5 XP per PC.
- Remove the selected Task and refill the display.
- Resolve any Act-ending procedure.
- Advance the Season and refresh Orders if entering Spring.
- Pass Council Influence.
- Prepare the next Turn.

# N.15 Essential Reminders

**One Campaign Turn equals one Season.**

**One Company Order is selected per Turn.**

**Each non-PC Grand Faction commits one Maneuver per Turn.**

**Council Influence changes who selects the Stake and directs an independent power; it never creates an additional card play.**

**A Political Mandate replaces control of an existing faction commitment rather than adding another commitment.**

**NPC card Strength determines political competition, not the magnitude of map development.**

**Winning a political trick does not automatically transfer territory or grant an army.**

**Every NPC receives its legal basic Maneuver effect, even when it loses the trick.**

**Only one colored or gray Act marker is awarded each Turn; a colored winner receives one Development draw.**

**Company Orders refresh in Spring; Faction Maneuvers refresh at the beginning of Acts. Act milestones also grant their stated XP and progression.**

**Political agreements and military access continue to influence future armies until changed or expired.**
