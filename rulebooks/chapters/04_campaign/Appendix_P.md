# APPENDIX P — LEGACY CAPTAINS AND HERO CONVERSION

*Campaign Mode — Revised Legacy Character Rules*

A **Legacy Captain** is a named Hero drawn from a surviving Player Character in an earlier campaign. They may return as a commander, adventurer, spellcaster, specialist, ally, or rival. They retain the defining qualities of the original Character—**Ancestry, Class, Subclass, Characteristics, signature abilities, and selected learned Talents**—but participate in structured battles as a **single military Unit**, not as a second fully advancing Player Character.

The aim is recognition without duplication: a former Dwarf Knight should still protect allies with *Hold the Line* and *Bodyguard*; an Elf Elementalist should still use *Infusion* and familiar spells; an Alchemist should still use the Formulas they actually learned.

**A Legacy Captain is always a named Hero and may cost no more than 225 Points.** A Class Template includes its Hero status, defining Class Ability, one Subclass Signature, starting Class Spell Attacks or Artificer Formulas where applicable, and the Character's original Ancestry Trait. These included benefits are **not purchased again**. Purchased Talents and equipment are added separately as described below.

The term *Legacy Captain* is used for the campaign rule even when the returning Hero is a Rogue, Mage, Priest, Artificer, or a military figure with another title.

---

## P.1 Eligibility and Ground Rules

1. The returning Character must have survived the previous campaign, retired legitimately, or have an explicitly established setting-appropriate means of returning. Previously established deaths, bargains, and allegiances still matter.
2. Use the former Character Sheet as the **selection record**. Copy actual Ancestry, Class, Subclass, Characteristics, relevant Skill thresholds, and learned abilities. Do not freely choose abilities the Character never possessed.
3. The converted Character is **one named Model** with the `Hero` and `Character` designations, and its Class-appropriate Unit Types, Roles, and Traits. Its current allegiance determines its Faction tag.
4. The finished Unit uses normal structured or narrative combat phases, saves, engagements, CP, Command timing, Unit activation, and Unit-wide keyword rules. Its previous personal XP total is **not** its new military Points value.
5. A Legacy Captain does not gain XP, develop a new Talent Pyramid, receive the active PC's personal CP, or use the three-Critical-Injury survival allowance. It is a persistent campaign military formation.
6. Its **entire military value**, including selected equipment, additional learned abilities, and purchased profile improvements, must be **225 Points or less**. There is no additional automatic Heroic Commission charge on the Class Templates below.

### P.1.1 Relationship to Army Construction

A Legacy Captain occupies **one compatible recruitment slot** and counts toward applicable Hero limits. It never replaces the current PC's required personal H slot. A Legacy Captain has no entitlement to its former campaign's equipment stockpile, Gold, military formations, faction recruitment privileges, or command structure.

The Company must obtain the Character through a legitimate source: Recruit, a Patron or faction roster, Commerce, Politics, a named agreement, a mission reward, or another explicit campaign exception. Recruitment eligibility should be identified when the Legacy Captain is approved. The Class Templates are **Hero profiles**; they are not a new unrestricted recruitment category. The GM assigns legal eligible slots based on the comparable military role (normally H for martial leaders, E or eligible H for spellcasting Heroes, and S/E or eligible H for support Heroes). **Hero status itself does not automatically grant H-slot eligibility.**

---

## P.2 Conversion Procedure

Complete the following six steps. The original Character Sheet is only needed during conversion and when referencing a retained Skill-based ability.

1. **Choose the original Class Template.** Copy its military characteristics, tags, and base Points from P.3. Hero status is already included.
2. **Preserve the original Ancestry.** Apply the original Ancestry Trait and record the Character's original Characteristics and Size. Apply the conversion notes in P.4 where a session-duration or narrative rule needs battlefield wording.
3. **Preserve Class and Subclass identity.** Keep the template's Class Ability and the Character's original Subclass Signature. Starting Spell Attacks and Artificer Field Formulae are included when noted in P.5. Do not buy these features again.
4. **Select retained purchased Talents.** Choose only abilities the former Character actually learned. Preserve their recognizable effects, consolidating ranked progress and limiting exceptional effects as described in P.6. Pay the listed Legacy conversion Points.
5. **Select equipped weapons, spells, and equipment.** Choose legal loadout options the former Character actually used or could credibly retain. Pay printed equipment/learned-spell Points, or the approved equivalent military upgrade price. The old inventory is not automatically free.
6. **Validate and record.** Ensure the total is at most 225 Points, the Model has a legal recruitment source and slot, and every Command identifies its CP cost, phase, targets, frequency, and any retained Skill rating. Transfer the result to a one-page Legacy Datasheet (P.11).

**No additional class-conversion surcharge:** Do not add the generic Captain's 125 Points, the +35 Heroic Commission, or a full former-PC XP cost to any of the Class Template prices. The following templates replace that starting arithmetic.

## P.3 Class Templates

Each Class Template represents **one Model**. The characteristics below are provisional military balance values, **not a recalculation from Characteristic dice**. The Character's old Body, Agility, Mind, and Spirit are retained separately for abilities that refer to them.

| Original Class | Base Points | Move | Wounds | Armor | Evade | Ward | Starting identity |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Warrior** | **150** | 5" | 5 | 5+ | 5+ | — | Hero, Character, Infantry, Armored; *Overpower* |
| **Rogue** | **150** | 5" | 4 | 6+ | 4+ | — | Hero, Character, Infantry, Mobile; *Exploit Opening* |
| **Mage** | **160** | 3" | 4 | 6+ | 6+ | 6+ vs Magic | Hero, Character, Infantry, Wizard; base Mage spells |
| **Priest** | **160** | 3" | 5 | 6+ | 6+ | — | Hero, Character, Infantry, Support; *Divine Ward*, base Priest spells |
| **Artificer** | **160** | 4" | 5 | 6+ | 6+ | — | Hero, Character, Infantry, Support; *Field Formulae* |

Add the Subclass's explicitly granted tags: Knight adds Infantry (already present, no duplicate effect); Fighter adds Mobile; Bard and Illusionist add Support; Druid adds Wizard. Other appropriate Traits or Roles must be explicitly granted by an ability, Ancestry, equipment, or the resulting datasheet. A base Armor value does **not** automatically grant the `Armored` tag; the Warrior template expressly grants it.

### P.3.1 Attacks and equipment

A Legacy Hero uses the eligible **PC weapon, starting spell, and Formula Attack profiles** from the current Equipment, Classes, Spell Catalogue, and Artificer Formulas chapters, rather than inheriting a generic Captain's free 4-die Officer's Weapon or a generic Wizard's Arcane Bolt in addition to its own spells.

- Starting Class Spell Attacks and the starting Subclass Spell Attack(s) granted to the original Character are **included in the template**; the Mage Elementalist retains their one chosen starting elemental spell, not all three.
- An Artificer retains **two originally learned starting Formulas** from its Subclass, including the one designated as its Field Specialty. The other Formula occupies one optional prepared Command slot. Formulas learned later are additional purchased options.
- Physical weapons, armor, shields, foci, and other gear add their legal **printed PC equipment Points** (or an approved equivalent military package where the PC item cannot be represented directly). A listed zero-Point Dagger or Staff remains zero Points.
- Additional permanently learned Spell Attacks retain their Spell Catalogue Point cost. They are alternative Attack profiles, **not extra Assault or Combat actions**.
- A Class Template does not gain the generic Captain's two complimentary officer Commands or the generic Wizard's complimentary Arcana; its included class features take their place.

**Equipment conversion:** If a former weapon or artifact cannot reasonably use its original profile in military combat, use the closest existing paid equipment/formation upgrade and write that substitution on the datasheet. Do not combine two profiles' favorable parts or import purchased bonuses for free.

## P.4 Ancestry and Original Characteristics

A Legacy Captain keeps its original **Body, Agility, Mind, and Spirit** values (including legitimate improvements from the earlier campaign), its Size, and the actual Ancestry Trait or chosen Adaptation. Those values are not directly converted into additional Wounds, Attacks, or Saves beyond the specific Trait or retained ability that references them.

Use the original ancestry benefits, with these battlefield-duration and targeting clarifications:

| Ancestry | Retained Legacy benefit |
| --- | --- |
| **Human — Adaptable** | Retain the Characteristic originally increased at creation; do not choose a second +1. |
| **Dwarf — Stout** | +1 Wound. |
| **Elf — Graceful** | +1" Move. |
| **Orc — Ferocious** | On a melee Attack Surge, gain Critical 1 for that Attack. |
| **Halfling — Fortunate** | Reroll one natural 1 **once per battle** instead of once per session. The die cannot be rerolled again. |
| **Gnome — Ingenious** | Gain +1D6 before one Mind-based Check **once per battle** instead of once per session. |
| **Goblin — Scurry** | After completely Evading an Attack, reposition up to 1" along a legal path. This cannot bypass Engagement/Disengage restrictions. |
| **Tiefling — Otherworldly** | Ward 6+ against Magic Attacks; use the better applicable Ward rather than taking two Ward Saves. |
| **Beastfolk** | Retain the **Adaptation actually chosen**. Improvements to Awareness, Stealth, Survival, Athletics, Medicine, or Mysticism matter for retained eligible Skill Checks; physical Adaptations retain their normal rules otherwise. |
| **Kobold — Pack Fighter** | Weapon Attacks gain Accurate +1 against an enemy Engaged with another friendly Unit. |
| **Undead — Deathless** | Retain Deathless, the `Undead` Trait, and the Character's original Skeleton, Zombie, Spirit, or Vampire Archetype. See below. |

**Undead Archetypes:** Skeleton retains Tough 1 against ranged Weapon Attacks. Spirit retains Ward 6+ against non-Magic Weapon Attacks. Vampire retains its once-per-Round Heal 1 on destroying an enemy Unit with an Melee Attack. Zombie retains its first-defeat **Hard Body + Survival** survival Check, now **once per battle**; on success it remains at **up to 2 Wounds** (no more than its current maximum). This is a deliberately limited version of the original Body-based recovery to prevent a one-Model Hero from receiving a full additional Wound bar.

A Beastfolk's chosen Adaptation is not rerolled or exchanged for new options during conversion. An Ancestry does not grant a different Faction tag; the Legacy Captain's current army allegiance determines its Faction.

### P.4.1 Skill checks on the compact datasheet

Record only the **original Skill thresholds that retained abilities actually call for** (for example Athletics for *Bodyguard*, Craft for selected Artificer Formulas, or Mysticism for a retained magical Talent).

When a retained ability calls for `Characteristic + Skill`, roll the Characteristic number of D6 and count successes at that Skill's original threshold. Observe the ability's printed cap, CP cost, and timing. There is **no additional universal difficulty** for a Talent or Formula Check unless its text specifies one.

Legacy Captains may make scenario-required Checks when the mission explicitly permits them. They do not gain an unrestricted extra PC Skill Action merely because their Characteristics are recorded.

### P.4.2 Caps and changed values

Normal caps apply: Characteristics cannot exceed 6, Skill thresholds cannot become better than 2+, and each die can be rerolled at most once. A retained ability with a large X retains its specific printed cap. Where an effect could create an unlimited number of Units, reactivations, permanent objects, or unavoidable damage, apply its explicit conversion in P.5 or P.7 rather than importing the unrestricted interaction.

## P.5 Class and Subclass Signatures

The Legacy Hero **always retains its original Base Class Ability and one original Subclass Signature** as part of the template's cost. Those abilities keep their names, ordinary CP costs, timing, and identity unless an explicit conversion below changes them. Class-granted Spell Attacks and starting Formulas are also included, as specified in P.3.1.

| Class | Subclass | Included Signature | Legacy application |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Warrior** | Berserker | **Frenzy** | 1 CP Movement; suffer 2 unsavable Damage; until end of Round gain +X" Move and +X Attacks on the next attack, where X = half Body rounded up. |
| | Knight | **Hold the Line** | 1 CP Reaction against incoming Attack/Charge; Tough X = half Body rounded up; ignore applicable forced movement. |
| | Fighter | **Finesse** | 1 CP with one Weapon Attack, once per Round; gain Accurate X, where X = Agility; if the rerolled dice Surges, the attack gains Critical 1. |
| **Rogue** | Assassin | **Ambush** | 1 CP Assault; a Weapon Attack or Charge made from Concealed this Phase gains Attacks +2 or Impact +2, whichever applies. |
| | Ranger | **Hunter's Eye** | Passive; when the Ranger Surges on a ranged attack, reduce the target's Cover by X, where X = the number of Surges. |
| | Bard | **Inspire** | 1 CP Movement; one other friendly Unit within Short gains +1D6 on its next Skill Check, or Anti-[chosen enemy Tag] X on its next Weapon or Spell Attack, where X = half Spirit rounded up. |
| **Mage** | Elementalist | **Infusion** | 1 CP Assault; one of Empower, Extend, or Unleash modifies one Spell Attack as originally printed. |
| | Necromancer | **Raise Thrall** | 1 CP Movement; summon or reinforce one temporary Thrall Unit, maximum **3 Models**, with the restrictions below. |
| | Illusionist | **Phantasm** | 1 CP Movement; summon one Phantasm token within Short and at least 3" from enemies; enemies charging a friendly Unit within 3" of it reduce their Charge distance by half Mind rounded up. |

| | Oracle | **Foretell** | 1 CP Reaction; turn one failed die into a normal success on an eligible friendly roll; no Surge generated. |
| **Artificer** | Alchemist | **Field Specialty** | One originally known Alchemist Formula is always prepared; the other starting known Formula uses an optional Command slot. |
| | Machinist | **Field Specialty** | Apply the same rule to the Character's originally known Machinist Formulas. |
| | Runesmith | **Field Specialty** | Apply the same rule to the Character's originally known Runesmith Formulas. |

**Base Class Abilities, included:** Warrior — *Overpower* (each Surge on an attack or charge reduces the target's Tough by 1); Rogue — *Exploit Opening* (1 additional normal Hit per Surge on a Weapon Attack); Mage — its two starting Spell Attacks; Priest — *Divine Ward* and its two starting Spell Attacks; Artificer — *Field Formulae* and the two starting learned Formulas. These are not interchangeable generic Captain Commands.

### P.5.1 Summons: Necromancer Thralls

A Legacy Necromancer may control **one temporary Thrall Unit** through *Raise Thrall*. It initially contains up to `min(3, Mind)` Models. Reinforcement adds one Model only if the Unit is not Engaged and remains below three Models. A newly raised Thrall cannot activate in its creation Round. Apply its existing Wounds, Saves, Claw profile, Coherency, and Reanimated rule.

A summoned Thrall is **not a permanently recruited Owned Roster formation** and disappears at the end of the encounter. It does not independently control or score scenario objectives unless the mission explicitly permits summoned Units to do so. Its presence is part of the Necromancer's included Subclass Signature, not an extra paid army slot or a second personal CP pool. It nevertheless activates as the original summon rules permit, so this signature warrants specific playtesting.

### P.5.2 Artificer Field Specialty

Choose the **two starting Formulas originally known**, not any two of the current catalogue. Designate one as the always-prepared Field Specialty; the second must be prepared in an optional Command slot before battle. During normal prebattle Command preparation, the Artificer may designate a different **actually known** Formula as its Field Specialty, as in the original Class rules. Later Formulas bought through *Formula Study* or *Cross-Discipline* must be selected and paid for under P.6. Their original CP costs, attack-action costs, Skill Checks, timing, and Formula-specific caps continue to apply. An Artificer does **not** gain access to all fifteen Formulas merely by returning as a Legacy Hero.

## P.6 Purchased Talents and Additional Abilities

A Legacy Captain may retain a **small, deliberately selected subset** of Talents and learned options purchased in the earlier campaign. The option must appear on the former Character Sheet or be a directly approved replacement for something it actually possessed. Class and Subclass abilities already included in P.5 do not consume these selections.

### P.6.1 Selection limits

| Legacy Hero role | Maximum additional selections | Selection guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Warrior / martial Rogue** | **2 purchased Commands + 2 purchased passives** | A command or passive may use fewer than its available categories; extras are not compulsory. |
| **Bard, Mage, Priest, Artificer, or other support-focused build** | **3 purchased Commands, Formulas, or additional known Spell Attacks + 1 purchased passive** | May trade one of those three additional active options for a second passive. |

For these limits, a permanently learned extra Spell Attack counts as an additional selected option even though a Spell Attack does not consume a prepared Command slot. A ranked Talent consolidated into one package counts as one passive selection. Additional selection slots are **not free Points**; all choices must still fit the 225-point ceiling.

**Preparation:** A Legacy Hero may prepare at most **four optional Command Abilities**, as for the original PC. Included Subclass Signature Commands and an Artificer's Field Specialty remain always prepared outside those four slots. Known extra Spell Attack profiles and passive bonuses do not occupy Command slots.

### P.6.2 Point guidelines

Use the nearest current printed military or PC upgrade cost when the same effect exists. Otherwise use this table; these are **Legacy conversion prices**, not new general-purpose Talent prices.

| Retained option | Typical cost | Examples / notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Minor passive | **+10** | +1" Move, Brace +1, narrow range or utility improvement. |
| Standard passive | **+15** | +1 Wound, Accurate +1 on a designated Attack, Tough +1, Cover +1. |
| Advanced passive | **+25** | Improve Evade one step, a stronger conditional defense, or a similarly substantial benefit. |
| Minor Command | **+15** | Short reposition or situational protection comparable to printed early-tier options. |
| Standard Command | **+20** | *Bodyguard*, *Second Wind*, *Haste*, *Smoke Bomb*, *Barrier*, or a comparable learned Command, when appropriately scaled. |
| Major Command | **+30–35** | Strong teleportation, area control, delayed damage, or a restricted high-tier Command. |
| Additional learned Spell Attack | **Printed Spell Catalogue price** | Usually 10, 20, or 30 Points; use the actual learned profile and normal Attack action. |
| Additional learned Artificer Formula | **+15–25** | Price by range, targeting, defensive utility, area effect, and improvements; Formula Attack still costs its Assault action and stated CP. |
| Prowess-style Attack training | **+15 for one Attack die or one Hit-step improvement** | Select one existing Attack profile; do not raise the same Hit target beyond legal limits. |
| Exceptional attack or defensive modification | **Nearest printed comparable cost, normally +25 or more** | Includes strong Damage, Ward, or repeated-target enhancements; requires explicit approval. |

A retained Command retains its **original CP cost**; buying it for military Points never makes it free to activate. A previously learned passive is not an additional independent Attack and does not improve every Attack unless its printed wording already says so. A copied Support Command must retain its actual target, timing, range, and Model limits.

### P.6.3 Ranked Talents and overlapping effects

Ranked Talents should be represented compactly rather than importing an entire character's accumulated progression:

- **Mobility Training / Toughened:** Pay the normal Legacy bonus for each retained +1" Move or +1 Wound, to a maximum of two retained ranks of a given Talent. The associated Skill improvement may be preserved only when relevant to a retained ability and does not create extra free battlefield profile improvements.
- **Martial / Spell / Combat Prowess:** Choose one eligible original Attack profile and pay for up to two individual improvements total (each +1 Attack die **or** one better Hit step). A second original Prowess line may affect another legal profile if selection limits allow. Do not apply the same rank more than once.
- **Sharpeye, Nimble, Critical Prowess, Favored Enemy:** Preserve an applicable ranked keyword, but purchase its effective value. Use the combined Unit-pool stacking and reroll rules; if several `Anti-[Type]` bonuses apply to one Attack, use the strongest applicable Anti value rather than stacking separate types.
- **Weapon Mastery, Evasive Training, Warded, Greater Ward, Exceptional Development:** These are substantial effects. Preserve only specifically purchased and approved ranks at an appropriate printed-equivalent cost. Do not obtain both a large profile increase and the same advancement again through a free Characteristic import.
- **No duplicate benefit:** If a Class, Ancestry, spell, or purchased Talent already grants a keyword or modifier, normal ranked stacking applies only to the extent expressly permitted by the current rules. Repeating an unranked passive does not create a second copy.

A retained high-tier Talent that cannot be expressed without dominating a battle may be **reduced to a clearly written limited version** or omitted in favor of a different Talent the former Character actually possessed. Such alterations should be agreed before the game and recorded on the datasheet. Do not leave conversion pricing or effects to improvisation once deployment begins.

### P.6.4 Commands requiring Skill Checks

Where a retained Command uses original Characteristic and Skill ratings, it may keep the original roll. For example, *Bodyguard* uses **Body + Athletics** and redirects up to X Hits where X is the number of successes. This need not be replaced with a fixed value of X, because only one Characteristic and one Skill threshold are needed on the compact datasheet.

A group that prefers fixed effects may replace a specific Skill-based Command with a **predeclared limited version**, such as X = 1–2, but must agree on the fixed value and adjust Points if necessary. This is an optional simplification, not the default method.

## P.7 Exceptional Abilities and Battlefield Balance

Legacy Captains should preserve identity without importing abilities that evade army-building rules or generate excessive extra actions.

- **Additional Commands do not grant more CP.** The controlling side pays from its ordinary CP allowance; Command frequency and legal timing remain in force.
- **Each Legacy Captain still activates as one Unit.** A Command does not grant an extra normal Move, Assault Attack, or Charge unless its actual text expressly allows that effect. Mission Actions still consume the Unit's regular action in Movement or Assault.
- **No free permanent reinforcements:** Summons and temporary effects use their converted signature rules. Animal Companions or permanently recruited allied units require an eligible independent profile, Point allocation, and legal slot/permission unless the underlying rule specifically establishes otherwise.
- **No unlimited healing:** Heal normally removes accumulated Damage from surviving Models; it does not restore Defeated Models, resurrect an army formation, or remove a PC's Critical Injuries without express text.
- **No unlimited damage-area conversions:** Delayed area damage, extra attack instances, special Ward-piercing effects, and Tier 4–5 Talents must be priced and capped against equivalent current Units before being allowed.
- **Unit-wide keywords:** Accurate, Tough, Cover, Brace, and related ranked effects follow the current combined Unit roll rules. A Legacy Captain's personal ancestry or attack keyword does not multiply with its allied formation Models.
- **Separate Army Limits:** A Legacy Captain may have powerful abilities, but its presence does not increase permitted army Points, the Hero maximum, scenario Unit counts, or a participating PC's deployment ceiling.

**Balance guideline:** The 225-point cap is a limit, not a target. A straightforward converted Rogue or Warrior may cost 165–200 Points and remain mechanically complete. Particularly complex casters and support Heroes may approach 225 by purchasing additional familiar Commands and spells.

## P.8 Equipment, Gold Cost, and Recruitment

A Legacy Hero may equip one primary melee weapon, one ranged weapon, one set of armor, appropriate off-hand equipment, and any permitted foci or artifacts, following the original equipment-slot restrictions. Weapons and Spell Attacks are alternate profiles; they do not add activations.

The original Character's formerly owned magic item may be carried forward **only when the campaign establishes that the item remains available**, and its complete approved Points cost is still included. When an artifact's effect has no supported military equivalent, use one specified comparable printed effect or leave the artifact as narrative equipment. Do not assign free combat keywords because an item was once rare or expensive.

**Recruitment Gold** is calculated from the completed Hero's Point Value:

**Gold Cost = Legacy Hero Points ÷ 25, rounded up.**

| Finished Points | Gold |
| ---: | ---: |
| 150 | 6 |
| 175 | 7 |
| 200 | 8 |
| 225 | 9 |

Discounts and special recruitment rights apply only when an actual campaign reward or agreement allows them. Purchasing or recruiting a Legacy Captain never grants the rest of their original faction's roster.

## P.10 Casualties and Long-Term Status

A Legacy Captain remains a named persistent military Unit. Keep its legal recruitment source, loyalty or agreement terms, final Points, known Commands, and casualty result on the Company's Owned Roster (or the GM's corresponding roster).

After combat:

- **Surviving but damaged:** Use the normal surviving-formation replenishment rule, paying the ordinary flat half-recruitment-cost fee when replenishment is required.
- **Defeated:** Roll the campaign formation casualty table: **1 = Destroyed; 2–4 = Mauled; 5–6 = Scattered**. Apply the corresponding legal recovery or replacement rules.
- **Destroyed:** Do not return the Character automatically. Their identity and past history may support a future narrative rescue or resurrection only if established by an actual legal campaign event.

Legacy Captains do not use the current PCs' three Critical Injury slots and do not receive free recovery because they were once Player Characters.
