# GENERIC UNITS

**Rebalanced Draft v3 — Rounded Upgrade Pricing**

## General Rules

Generic Units provide the standard battlefield profiles available to most armies. Their equipment, appearance, and origins may vary by faction without requiring separate rules.

Faction-specific troops supplement these profiles. They may have different Model counts, Wounds, characteristics, equipment, and abilities, provided they are balanced against comparable generic Units.

### Unit Conventions

- M: Movement in inches.
- W: Wounds per Model.
- Arm: Armor Save.
- Eva: Evade Save.
- Ward: Additional applicable Ward Save.
- Weapon profiles: Range / Attacks / Hit / Damage / Keywords.
- Attacks are made per eligible Model unless specified otherwise.
- Each Unit has at least one melee weapon.
- Units with multiple weapons do not automatically make additional Attacks.
- Unless specified, weapon packages apply to every Model in the Unit.
- All Standard Unit-wide Keyword values and modifiers use the shared keyword rules below; they do not multiply by the Model count.

### Unit-Wide Keyword Values

A combined Attack roll uses a weapon's printed **Accurate X, Critical X, and other shared Attack Keywords once for the entire Unit Attack pool**, not once per contributing Model. A five-Model Unit whose weapon has Accurate 1 rolls its combined Attacks with **Accurate 1**, not Accurate 5. If it gains Accurate 2 from another eligible source, it has **Accurate 3** for that Attack. The same additive principle applies to Critical, Tough, Cover, Brace, and other compatible ranked Keywords unless an explicit exception states otherwise.

If a bonus says **+1 Attack per Model**, add one die for every eligible contributing Model; if it says **+2 Attacks to a Unit's next Attack**, add only two dice total. When different Model groups use different weapons or benefits, resolve separate pools as necessary. Each die may be rerolled no more than once. Critical X follows the current rule: after rerolls, convert up to X **failed Hit dice** to Critical Hits without requiring a Surge unless a particular source says it does. No per-Model multiplication of a keyword's X value occurs by default.

### Equipment and Upgrade Costs

Base Unit costs include their standard equipment.

Weapon and equipment options marked +0 Points are included without additional cost. Other upgrades increase the Unit's total price by the amount listed.

Weapon-package costs cover the Unit's initial Model count. When adding Models to an upgraded Unit, pay an additional **+5 Points per new Model for each paid weapon or shield package** the new Model carries, in addition to the Model's normal cost. A weapon-package surcharge is not charged for unit-wide training or range upgrades. Some formations also list a **Veteran reinforcement surcharge**, reflecting the scaling of Attack dice and Hit improvements with additional Models; those surcharges are separate and cumulative.

Upgrades may be selected only when listed in the Unit's profile or permitted by another rule.

**Point increments:** Significant multi-model weapon packages and training upgrades favor 25-Point steps where their effects warrant them. Smaller utility effects and individual-Model costs may still use 5-, 10-, 15-, or 20-Point increments. Veterans are generally a flat **+50 Points per starting formation** with the listed surcharge for extra Models; generic Support Veterans are the lower-cost exception. Damage 2 ranged packages pay a higher upfront weapon premium rather than a different Veteran price.

### Matched-Play Command Points and Cards

A standard **2,000-point matched-play army selects eight army Commands** during construction: two Faction Commands and three from each of its two Doctrines. Each selected Command is represented by a card if playing physically. All eight selected cards remain available as the army's **Command repertoire**; players do **not** prepare a separate two-card hand each Round.

- Each matched-play army has **8 Command Points (CP) per Round**, refreshed at the **beginning** of the Round. Unspent CP are lost rather than carried forward.
- To use a printed Faction or Doctrine Command, exhaust its card to spend 1 CP, or exhaust that card plus additional available cards if the printed cost exceeds 1 CP. Exhausting the card **represents** spending 1 CP for a 1 CP Command; it is **not** a second cost.
- To use a Unit's personal Command instead, exhaust any available selected Command card as the physical representation of the CP spent. The card's printed Command is **not** also resolved. For a 2 CP personal Command, exhaust two available cards and resolve that Command once.
- An exhausted card cannot be exhausted again until the next Round, when all cards become ready. Each selected card's printed Command can therefore be used no more than once per Round unless a rule explicitly says otherwise.
- A 0 CP ability does not consume or exhaust a card; its own timing and usage restrictions still apply.
- Personal phase Commands normally occur only **during that Unit's own activation**. Faction and Doctrine phase Commands are used while the controlling side has **activation priority**. Reactions are used at their stated triggers without requiring priority; explicitly timed End Step Commands resolve in the End Step.
- Extra Attacks or activations occur only when the ability explicitly grants them. A Command does not normally replace the Unit's chosen Phase action unless stated otherwise.

**Campaign and RPG combat:** The eight-card repertoire and 2,000-point roster selection belong to matched play; they are **not** prerequisites for RPG or campaign encounters. Players normally have 2 CP each per Round, including early campaign encounters with **no physical Command cards**. The GM/DM uses the applicable encounter CP allowance (by default **max(2, 2 × number of PCs − 1)**). CP can be tracked as imaginary points, tokens, or cards when available; Unit Commands and Artificer Formulas do not require a Doctrine card in encounters without them.

# 1. INFANTRY

## Line Infantry — 75 Points

**5 Models · Infantry, Unarmored · Recruitment C**  
**M4" / W2 / Arm 6+ / Eva 6+**

Regular soldiers, militia, footmen, skeleton warriors, and other conventional infantry. Line Infantry are inexpensive screening and objective-holding troops that may be trained for greater offense, improved defense against Charges, or more reliable Charges of their own. In their base equipment they carry no substantial armor or shield, gaining the **Unarmored** tag instead; purchasing the Armor upgrade below removes Unarmored and grants **Armored**.

### Melee Weapon Packages

Choose **one** melee weapon package. All listed Attacks are **per Model**.

| Weapon | Points | Profile / Effect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Hand Weapons | +0 | Engaged / 2 / 5+ / 1. Standard sword, axe, or similar weapon. |
| Spears | +10 | Engaged 2" / 2 / 5+ / 1 / Brace 1. Defensive reach and resistance to Charge Impact. |
| Polearms | +25 | Engaged 2" / 1 / 6+ / 2 / Brace 1 / Anti-Cavalry 2. Less reliable and no added Attack volume, but devastating reach weapons against mounted and multi-Wound Charging threats. |
| Heavy Weapons | +25 | Engaged / 1 / 5+ / 2 / Heavy / Anti-Armored 2. Stronger individual Hits and extra dice against Armored targets; the Heavy restriction balances its circumstantial extra-dice potential on a multi-Model Unit. |
| Dual Weapons | +40 | Engaged / 3 / 5+ / 1. Greater volume of Hits; cannot take Shields. |

**Melee Heavy clarification:** Polearms above do **not** possess the **Heavy** keyword despite its weight; Heavy Weapons **do** possess Heavy. Their physical weight is represented by their printed Hit, Damage, Anti-[Type], and costs; normal melee combat after movement remains legal except where Heavy restricts it.

### Defensive Upgrades

| Upgrade | Points | Effect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Armor | +15 | Improve the Unit's Armor one step (6+ to 5+ alone, or 5+ to 4+ if combined with Shields). Gain the **Armored** tag; lose **Unarmored**. Available with any melee weapon package. |
| Shields | +15 | Improve the Unit's Armor one step (6+ to 5+ alone, or 5+ to 4+ if combined with Armor). Available only with Hand Weapons or Spears. Does not itself grant or remove Armored/Unarmored. |

Armor and Shields may be purchased independently or together; together they improve Armor by two total steps (6+ to 4+), matching the previous single Shields ceiling. Each is a paid defensive package subject to the normal +5-Point per-added-Model surcharge described in the General Rules.

### Formation and Training Upgrades

| Upgrade | Points | Effect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Standard Formation | +0 | Five Models with the chosen equipment. |
| Additional Model | +25 each | Add a matching Model, up to **10 Models**. |
| Veterans | **+75** | The selected melee weapon gains **+1 Attack per Model**, and its Hit characteristic improves by one step. Each Model added after Veterans is purchased pays an additional **+10 Points** Veteran reinforcement surcharge. |
| Weapon Specialization | +25 | Choose one Faction, Unit Type, or Trait tag. One selected weapon gains **Anti-[chosen tag] 2** against Units possessing it. Follows the normal Anti-[Type] rules (applies once to the Unit's combined Attack pool; does not stack with another applicable Anti-[Type] source on the same target — only the higher value counts). May be purchased once. |
| Charge Drill | +15 | Gain **Charge 1**. Adds 1" to final Charge distance, not to Charge Impact. |
| Shock Training | +25 | Gain **Impact 1** on successful Charges. It increases the Unit's *one* shared Impact pool, not Impact per Model. |
| Brace Training | +15 | Gain **Brace 1**, additive with a spear's Brace 1 or other eligible sources. |
| Deadly Precision | +20 | 1 CP — Assault or Combat, on this Unit's Attack: gain Critical 1 per Surge with **any** weapon. This personal Command can be used again on another eligible Attack for another CP, never twice on the same Attack; no extra Attack is granted. |

A Line Infantry Unit may purchase **one** of Charge Drill, Shock Training, or Brace Training. It may purchase Veterans, Weapon Specialization, Armor, and Shields independently. Training effects and ranked Keywords apply once to the Unit, not once per Model; Charge distance and Impact follow the standard combat rules.

**Reinforcement example:** A seven-Model Veteran Unit with Hand Weapons costs **75 + 75 + (2 × 25) + (2 × 10) = 220 Points**. Paid weapon, Armor, and Shield packages incur their normal +5-Point per-added-Model surcharge in addition.

**Battlefield role:** Screens, objective control, massed melee, anti-Charge formations, and modest shock infantry.

## Ranged Infantry — 100 Points

**4 Models · Infantry, Ranged Infantry · Recruitment R**  
**M3" / W1 / Arm 6+ / Eva 6+**

Ranged Infantry specialize in mobile short-range shooting, massed arrows, prepared crossbow fire, and specialized firearms. Their starting formation is four Models, but can grow to **ten** at an increasing price.

### Ranged Weapon Packages

Choose **one** primary ranged weapon package. Attacks are **per Model**; Heavy and Firearm have their ordinary shared keyword meanings.

| Weapon | Points | Profile / Role |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **Shortbows** | **+0** | **8" / 2 / 5+ / 1**. Affordable, mobile baseline weapon. |
| Longbows | +35 | **12" / 3 / 5+ / 1 / Long Range 6"**. Mobile volleys with extended reach; beyond 12" reduce the combined Attack pool by 1 die for single-model Units or 3 dice for multi-model Units (minimum 1). |
| Crossbows | +25 | **9" / 1 / 4+ / 2 / Heavy / Anti-Armored 2**. Compact, accurate shots that punch through armor plating; stationary firing. |
| Heavy Crossbows | +50 | **11" / 2 / 5+ / 2 / Heavy / Anti-Elite 2**. Sustained anti-elite fire, at high cost and with Heavy restrictions. |
| Muskets | +50 | **11" / 1 / 4+ / 2 / Firearm, Heavy, Anti-Unique 1**. Accurate, reaction-proof shooting against valuable individual targets. |
| Pistols | +25 | **6" / 3 / 5+ / 1 / Firearm**. Mobile close-range shooting with Instant from Firearm. |
| Javelins | +10 | **6" / 3 / 5+ / 1**. Thrown volleys; also gain **Javelin Melee: Engaged 2" / 2 / 5+ / 1** instead of the default melee weapon. |
| Slings | +10 | **7" / 4 / 6+ / 1**. High-volume, low-accuracy short-range attacks. |

**Distinctions:** Longbows have greater effective reach and do not require stationary firing; Crossbows trade movement flexibility for concentrated Damage 2 Hits with extra dice against Armored targets; Heavy Crossbows deliver more dice against tough and Elite targets; Muskets gain Firearm's **Instant** and extra dice against Characters; Pistols and Javelins favor close-range mobile play. Weapon range does not modify Hit unless **Long Range X** is used beyond the printed range.

### Melee Weapon

| Weapon | Points | Profile |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Short Blade | +0 | Engaged / 1 / 6+ / 1. All Ranged Infantry receive this unless using Javelin Melee. |

A Unit carrying multiple profiles selects one eligible Attack when it uses an Attack action; it does not combine its ranged and melee profiles.

### Formation and Shooting Upgrades

| Upgrade | Points | Effect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Standard Formation | +0 | Four Models with the selected ranged package. |
| Additional Model | **+25 each** | Add a matching Model, up to **9 Models**. |
| **Rangefinder Training** | **+20** | Increase the primary ranged weapon's **printed normal Range by 2"**. It remains at its listed Hit within this improved range; existing Long Range X begins beyond the improved normal Range. |
| Longshot Training | +10 | Gain **Long Range 2"**. If the weapon already has Long Range X, increase that X by 1 instead. Shots beyond normal Range reduce the combined Attack pool by 1 die for single-model Units or 3 dice for multi-model Units (minimum 1). |
| Marksman Training | +30 | Primary ranged weapon gains Accurate 1 and improves Hit by one step. |
| Sharpeye | +20 | Primary ranged weapon gains Pinpoint. |
| Weapon Specialization | +25 | Choose one Faction, Unit Type, or Trait tag. The primary ranged weapon gains **Anti-[chosen tag] 2** against Units possessing it. Follows the normal Anti-[Type] rules (applies once to the Unit's combined Attack pool; does not stack with another applicable Anti-[Type] source on the same target — only the higher value counts). May be purchased once. |
| **Veterans** | **+75** | The selected ranged weapon gains **+1 Attack per Model** and improves Hit by one step. |

Deadly Precision +20 is a 1 CP personal Command that grants Critical 1 per Surge on one eligible Attack with any weapon. It does not require an extra-Model surcharge.

Veterans and Weapon Specialization may each be purchased once and apply only to the selected primary ranged weapon. Rangefinder and Longshot Training **may be combined**; they alter different portions of a weapon's range. Rangefinder increases **normal** Range and does not grant or remove the Long Range Attack-pool reduction; Longshot affects only the additional extended band. These range upgrades apply once to the chosen primary weapon profile, regardless of Models.

**Veteran pricing:** The upgrade costs **75 Points for any primary weapon**, reflecting the strong scaling effect of a flat +1 Attack per Model across a four-to-nine-Model formation. Veteran reinforcements pay +15 Points each because the extra die and improved Hit apply to every added Model. Stronger Damage 2 weapons instead pay higher **initial weapon-package prices**, so Veterans do not require a second price table.

**Formation example:** Eight Veteran Shortbow Models cost **100 + 75 + (4 × 25) + (4 × 15) = 335 Points** before other packages. Eight Veteran Musket Models cost **100 + 75 + 50 + (4 × 25) + (4 × 15) + (4 × 5) = 405 Points**, including the paid-weapon reinforcement surcharge.

**Battlefield role:** Formed shooting, precision ranged damage, mobile skirmish volleys, and specialized heavy fire.

## Elite Infantry — 105 Points

**3 Models · Elite, Infantry · Recruitment E**  
**M4" / W3 / Arm 5+ / Eva 5+**

Elite Infantry are durable close-combat specialists with nine total starting Wounds (W3 per Model), with an optional paid upgrade to W4. Weapon packages emphasize a clear choice between melee volume, reach, defensive Brace, and stronger Hits.

### Melee Weapon Packages

Choose **one** melee weapon package. Shields may accompany only Hand Weapons or Spears.

| Weapon | Points | Profile / Effect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Hand Weapons | +0 | Engaged / 3 / 5+ / 1. Reliable baseline. |
| Spears | +10 | Engaged 2" / 3 / 5+ / 1 / Brace 1. Reach and defense. |
| Polearms | +25 | Engaged 2" / 2 / 6+ / 2 / Brace 1 / Anti-Cavalry 2. Heavy Hits, reach, extra dice against mounted Charges, and Charge protection at reduced accuracy. |
| Dual Weapons | +25 | Engaged / 4 / 5+ / 1 / Accurate 1. High-volume specialist melee. |
| Heavy Weapons | +25 | Engaged / 2 / 5+ / 2 / Anti-Armored 2. Reliable high-Damage melee Hits with extra dice against Armored targets. |
| Shields | +25 | Improve Armor from 5+ to 4+. Hand Weapons or Spears only. |

These melee weapons do **not** inherently gain Heavy. The higher-Damage profiles already trade off Points, accuracy, or defensive equipment.

### Optional Ranged Weapon Packages

Any Elite Infantry Unit may buy **one** eligible ranged package **without first buying Veterans**. This provides a secondary Assault Attack profile and adds the **Ranged** tag, but **does not** change the Unit's default **E recruitment eligibility**.

| Weapon | Points | Profile / Effect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Shortbow | +25 | 8" / 2 / 5+ / 1. Inexpensive secondary fire. |
| Longbow | +50 | 10" / 3 / 5+ / 1 / Long Range 3". Mobile, longer-range fire. |
| Pistols | +25 | 6" / 3 / 5+ / 1 / Firearm, Pinpoint. Short-range ranged utility. |
| Musket | +50 | 11" / 1 / 4+ / 2 / Firearm, Heavy, Anti-Elite 2. Stationary anti-elite fire. |

Shields may be combined with Pistols, but **not** with Shortbows, Longbows or Muskets, which are two-handed ranged weapons.

These are **Elite Infantry equipment costs**, not an alternative Ranged Infantry roster. A Unit makes one eligible weapon Attack when selected to Attack in its phase; carrying a ranged weapon does not add its dice to a melee Attack.

| Weapon Master | +30 | Selected melee weapon gains Accurate 1 and improves Hit by one step. |
| Armored | +25 | Improve Armor one step; gain the Armored tag. |
| Quick | +50 | Increase Move by 2" and improve Evade one step; gain Mobile. |
| Deadly Precision | +20 | 1 CP — Assault or Combat, on this Unit's Attack: gain Critical 1 per Surge with **any** weapon. This personal Command can be used again on another eligible Attack for another CP, never twice on the same Attack; no extra Attack is granted. |
| Hunters | +25 | Choose one Faction or Trait tag. This unit gains Anti-[chosen tag] 3. |

Weapon Master and Deadly Precision are independent upgrades; the latter is a personal CP Command, not a passive keyword. Veterans and Weapon Specialization each affect **one selected weapon profile**, chosen when the upgrade is purchased. Veterans improves every weapon the Unit carries. Bonuses and ranked Keywords are never multiplied by its Model count unless expressly written **per Model**.

**Battlefield role:** Durable infantry, tactical melee specialists, and resilient mixed-weapon formations.

# 3. CAPTAINS

Captains are combat-capable officers who provide leadership, tactical coordination, and access to Command Abilities.

Infantry, Elite; Recruitment: E (**not Hero** by default; Heroic Commission adds Hero and H eligibility)

The standard Captain is an Elite Unit that occupies an E Recruitment Slot and **does not count toward the Hero maximum**. A Captain receives **two** eligible generic Captain Commands without additional cost; the Heroic Commission upgrade below makes it a Hero and lets it occupy an H Recruitment Slot.

# 10. DESIGN NOTES AND PLAYTEST PRIORITIES

These profiles are provisional balance targets. The main aim is to retain recognizable battlefield roles **without making paid options strictly better than every alternative**.

- **v4 Damage-output rebalance:** Following playtesting that found units took too long to inflict meaningful damage, most Damage 1 weapon profiles across Generic Units (and PC equipment, see Equipment and Crafting) gained **+1 Attack**. Damage 2+ and other already-high-volume profiles instead kept their listed Attacks and gained a thematic **Anti-[Type] 2** (occasionally 3), so stronger single Hits remain distinct from higher-volume weapons rather than simply becoming strictly better. See each weapon table for its specific Anti-[Type] assignment.
- **Shortbows:** The standard Ranged Infantry Shortbow is now **8" / 2 / 5+ / 1**. Other weapons purchase greater attack volume, more range, Damage 2, or Firearm traits, usually with a mobility or price trade-off.
- **Range:** Infantry ranged weapons primarily operate within **6–11" normal Range**, with Longbows extending farther at the cost of a combined Attack pool reduction (1 die for single-model Units, 3 dice for multi-model Units). **Rangefinder Training adds +2" normal Range**, while Longshot Training uses **Long Range** and the same pool reduction. Artillery remains the main long-range specialist at approximately 15–18".
- **Large ranged formations:** Up to **10 Models**, but additional Models cost 25 Points and **Veterans adds +15 more per added Model**. A paid ranged weapon also adds +5 per added Model. W1 defenses, concentrated deployment, and one activation per Unit remain important costs.
- **Veterans pricing is per-unit, not uniform:** Veterans grants a flat **+1 Attack per Model** plus a Hit improvement, so its value scales directly with a Unit's Model count. Larger formations therefore pay more: **Line Infantry and Ranged Infantry pay +75** (5–10 and 4–9 Models respectively), each with its own printed **per-added-Model Veteran reinforcement surcharge** (+10 for Line Infantry, +15 for Ranged Infantry) reflecting that every additional Model also benefits. Smaller or single-Model formations — **Elite Infantry (3–5 Models), Cavalry (3–5 Models), Captains and Wizards (1 Model)** — pay a flat **+50** with no comparable scaling risk. Generic Supports retain a reduced **+25** combat-training option. Veteran bonuses increase the **selected weapon's** Attacks per Model and improve Hit one step; they do not buff unrelated profiles.
- **Weapon Specialization (new, +25):** A broadly available upgrade (Line Infantry, Ranged Infantry, Elite Infantry, Cavalry, Captains, Wizards) letting a Unit choose **any one Faction, Unit Type, or Trait tag** and grant one selected weapon **Anti-[chosen tag] 2**. Because Anti-[Type] bonuses apply once to the whole Unit's combined Attack pool rather than per Model (see Unit Tags), this upgrade does not scale with Model count the way Veterans does — it stays a flat, situational bonus regardless of formation size, which is why it is priced as a single flat cost rather than per-unit like Veterans.
- **Line Infantry:** Rebuilt around a **W2 / Arm 6+ / Eva 6+, Unarmored** baseline for the same 75 Points, trading the old flat 5+ Armor for more Wounds and a cheaper, individually purchasable path back to better Armor (see Defensive Upgrades: Armor and Shields, each +15, stacking to the old Arm 4+ ceiling). Spears and Polearms have Charge defenses, Heavy Weapons provide Damage 2 with Anti-Armored 2 **and the Heavy restriction** (its circumstantial extra-dice potential is strong enough on a multi-Model Unit to warrant the movement tradeoff), and Dual Weapons provide volume. Charge Drill, Shock Training, and Brace Training add independent tactical choices. **Polearms remain the only melee weapon not implicitly Heavy** despite their weight.
- **Unarmored tag:** New Trait tag, the counterpart to Armored. Granted by baseline Line Infantry; removed once the Armor upgrade is purchased. See Unit Tags for full rules, including **Anti-Unarmored X**.
- **Elite Infantry:** Strong W4 profiles remain their principal distinction. Secondary ranged weapons are allowed without buying Veterans, and their Weapon Mastery/Critical upgrades modify one combined Unit pool rather than multiplying with Model count.
- **Faction Horde units:** Sneaks, Thralls, and Corpse Horde were buffed by adding Models at unchanged Points (Sneaks +1, Thralls +2, Corpse Horde +3) rather than by adding Attacks, preserving their characteristically fragile, high-casualty-tolerance profile while improving their staying power and board presence.
- **Supports:** Two W3 Models remain standard. Additional Models cost +50 but **never multiply Commands**. Engineer Pistols and the Alchemist/Cleric options are deliberately limited compared with PC class Formulas and spells. Heralds, Scouts/Spotters, Engineers, Alchemists, and Clerics may each learn **Target Analysis** (+20 Points), a 1 CP Assault Command granting a friendly Unit's next Attack a specialization-appropriate **Anti-[Type] 3** (see each specialization's upgrade table for its available categories). It is priced higher than a weapon's own Anti-[Type] 2 precisely because, unlike an owned weapon, a granted Command bonus doesn't scale with the receiving Unit's size or equipment.
- **Keyword stacking:** The entire Unit Attack pool receives its printed Accurate X, Critical X, etc. once. Distinct applicable keyword bonuses add their X values unless a specific rule states otherwise. An explicit **per-Model Attack** bonus is different from a **Unit Attack-pool** bonus.

## Current Generic Unit Balance — Playtest Clarifications

This balance pass supersedes the older historical Deadly Precision and Wizard default spell wording. **Deadly Precision +20** is now a personal 1 CP Command granting Critical 1 per Surge on any eligible Attack, repeatable on another Attack for another CP (never twice on the same Attack); no purchased Deadly Precision upgrade grants a permanent Critical keyword or adds a reinforcement surcharge. **Elite/Captain Weapon Master +30** and **Ranged Marksman Training +30** grant Accurate 1 plus one Hit step. **Sharpeye +20** grants Pinpoint.

Captains choose their secondary ranged weapon separately from Combat Training, Veterans improve all their weapons, Monster Hunter is removed, and Armored +25 is available. Shields cannot be paired with two-handed ranged weapons. Light/standard/heavy Cavalry gain Charge 3/2/1 and Impact +0/+1/+2. Charge Formation costs +50 to unlock a 1 CP Charge Command granting +3 Impact. Regular artillery mostly fires without Heavy; heavy artillery keeps Heavy and improves Hit. Wizards select one free Primary Spell and use Arcana slots for additional offensive spells or Commands. The JSON roster profiles remain authoritative for point values and weapon stat lines.
