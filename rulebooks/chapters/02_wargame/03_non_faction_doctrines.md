# NON-FACTION DOCTRINES

## Consolidated Rules — Playtest Draft v6.1

### General Rules

Non-Faction Doctrines represent the military strategies, tactics, and battlefield philosophies available to different armies.

Each army selects two distinct eligible Doctrines according to its faction's Doctrine restrictions.

Each Doctrine grants one Foundation Recruitment Slot and provides five Command options. The player selects three Commands from each chosen Doctrine, gaining the Recruitment Slot listed beside each selected Command.

Non-Reaction Doctrine Commands normally apply to the **active Unit** during the relevant Phase. Only an ability that explicitly names another friendly Unit, multiple Units, a Reserve Unit, or a designated enemy permits a different target. Recruitment Slots determine army composition, not Command eligibility. Recruitment Slots determine army composition; they do not restrict who may benefit from a Command.

### Command Usage

**Active-unit convention:** Unless a non-Reaction Phase Command explicitly names another friendly Unit, multiple Units, a Reserve Unit, or a designated enemy target, its benefit applies to the Unit currently activated. Coordination, multi-Unit Commands and triggered Reactions retain their stated target rules. Ordinary buffs cannot be assigned to arbitrary allies while an unrelated Unit activates.

**One Command per activation:** The acting side may use at most one personal Unit, Faction, or Doctrine Command during each Unit activation; the opposing side may use one eligible Reaction. Each Attack, Charge, or Save sequence benefits from at most one friendly Command effect, including one prepared during an earlier Phase. Passives remain compatible.


- Each Command costs 1 CP and one Doctrine-card exhaustion, unless otherwise stated.
- Commands are activated during the specified Movement, Assault, or Combat Phase.
- Reactions are used when their specified trigger occurs.
- Commands do not grant additional Unit activations or Attacks unless explicitly stated.
- Bonuses to Attacks add dice to one Attack made by the entire Unit, not to each Model.
- An effect applying to one Attack expires when that Attack is fully resolved.
- Effects lasting a Phase expire at the end of that Phase.
- Effects lasting a Round expire during that Round's End Step.
- An effect referring to a Unit's next Attack or activation remains available until that event occurs or its stated duration expires.
- Multiple applications of the same Command do not stack on one Unit.

### Movement and Positioning

Charge X: Add X inches to the final Charge distance after rolling and selecting the required dice. Standard Units roll 2D6 and add the results. Charge X does not modify the dice themselves or the Charge's Impact. Multiple Charge X effects do not stack; use the highest value. The normal 12" maximum distance for declaring a Charge still applies.

Blink X: An unengaged Unit may reposition within X inches. For multi-model Units, choose a lead Model and reposition it within X inches of its original position, then reposition the remaining Models according to normal formation movement and coherency rules. Every Model must end legally, and the Unit cannot finish Engaged. Blink is not a Charge.

Holding Position: Where an ability requires a Unit to have remained in position, its wording specifies the applicable period. A Unit that voluntarily Moved, Disengaged, Charged, or Blinked during that period does not qualify. Enemy-forced movement does not count unless specifically stated.

No separate Stationary keyword is used.

### Defensive Effects

Accurate X: Reroll up to X natural 1s during a Hit roll.

Tough X: Reroll up to X natural 1s during an Armor Save roll.

Brace X: Reduce the incoming Charge Impact pool by X automatic Hits, minimum zero. Brace does not provide retaliatory Attack dice.

Hit Cancellation: When an ability discards a successful non-6 Hit die, resolve it after Hit rerolls but before saves. Natural 6s, automatic Hits, and Charge Impact cannot be discarded this way.

Attack Reduction: When an ability removes an Attack die, remove it before the Hit roll. An Attack cannot be reduced below one die unless an ability explicitly allows it.

### Surges and Blunders

A roll generates a Surge if it contains at least two natural 6s and a Blunder if it contains at least two natural 1s, determined after permitted rerolls. Ordinary rolls generate at most one of each unless otherwise specified.

Reckless Roll: A roll designated Reckless cannot benefit from any rerolls. Its Surges and Blunders resolve normally, and any listed Gambit Blunder consequence applies in addition to other applicable effects.

### Staggered Deployment and Reserves

These universal rules apply to all armies in standard five-Round matched battles unless a Mission explicitly overrides them.

**Initial deployment.** During army deployment, each player places half of their purchased Units in Reserve, rounded down; the remainder deploy normally in the player's deployment zone. Count Units, not Points or Models. A player must deploy at least one Headquarters Unit normally if their army includes one.

**Escalating reinforcements.** Beginning in Round 1, each player may deploy up to a number of Reserve Units equal to the current Round number (one in Round 1, two in Round 2, and so on). This is a maximum, not a requirement. Unused allowances do not carry forward. No Unit may enter more than once.

**Ordinary arrival.** Ordinary Reserve Units are placed wholly within their owner's deployment zone at the end of the Movement Phase, in legal formation and coherency. If both players have Units arriving, alternate placement one Unit at a time, following the Round's normal player order. Reserve placement does not itself grant a Movement activation.

**Arrival restrictions.** A Unit entering from Reserve counts as having Moved this Round. It cannot perform a Movement Phase action or declare a Charge during the Round it arrives. It may otherwise make eligible Assault or Combat Phase attacks. It cannot use a Heavy Attack requiring it not to have Moved unless another rule explicitly permits this.

**Exceptions.** A specific Command may override only the arrival rules it explicitly mentions. Unless stated otherwise, an arrival granted by a Command counts toward the normal reinforcement allowance. A Unit still in Reserve cannot activate, use Commands, or control or contest objectives.

# 9. Alpha Strike

Foundation Recruitment Slot: V

Strategy: Overwhelm the enemy during the opening Rounds through rapid mobilization, accurate attacks, concentrated volleys, and decisive Charges. Its offensive bonuses decline as the battle develops.

| 3 | Accurate 1 | +2 Attacks | Impact +1 | Critical 1 |
| 4 | Accurate 1 | +1 Attack | Impact +1 | Accurate 1 |

### Alpha Strike Commands

| Command | Slot | Timing | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First Strike Orders | H | Assault | If the active Unit is within Short of friendly Headquarters, its next Attack this Round gains Accurate X, where X depends on the current Round. |
| Opening Volley | R | Assault | The active Unit gains +X Attacks on its next ranged Attack this Phase, where X is determined by the current Round. |
| Shock Assault | E | Assault | When the active Unit declares a Charge, increase its Charge Impact by X for that Charge, where X depends on the current Round. This does not increase Charge distance. |
| Rapid Mobilization | A | Movement / Assault / Combat | Choose either Rapid Deployment (Movement, once per battle, Round 1 only) or Standard Orders (Assault/Combat). See rapidMobilizationModes. |
| Ferocious Opening | C | Combat | Choose the active Unit making a Melee Attack this Phase. Gain Critical 2 in Round 1, Critical 1 in Rounds 2–3, or Accurate 1 in Rounds 4–5. CP-granted Critical requires no Surge and does not modify Charge Impact. |

### Rapid Mobilization — Two Modes

**Rapid Deployment — Movement; once per battle; Round 1 only.** When you would activate a friendly Unit during the Round 1 Movement Phase, you may instead select one Unit in Reserve. Deploy it wholly within your deployment zone, then perform its normal Movement activation. This arrival is **in addition to** your normal Round 1 reinforcement allowance, and it ignores the ordinary restrictions preventing Reserve Units from performing Movement actions or declaring Charges on the Round they arrive. The Unit may declare a legal Charge during the ensuing Assault Phase. It does not receive an additional activation. Rapid Deployment may be used only once per battle, even if this Command card later becomes available again.

**Standard Orders — Assault/Combat.** Choose one friendly Unit. It gains +1 Attack on its next eligible Attack this Phase.

Both modes use the normal Command CP and card-exhaustion cost. Each use resolves only one of the two modes. Once Rapid Deployment has been used, Standard Orders remains available on later uses of the Command.

### Command Clarifications

- **First Strike Orders** provides reliable Hits, but only while the recipient is within Short of a friendly Headquarters when the Command is used. Its bonus can apply to an otherwise eligible Attack later in the same Round.
- **Opening Volley** adds Attack dice to a single ranged Attack by the Unit, not to every Model.
- **Shock Assault** modifies only the Charge Impact of one declared Charge. It does not increase the Charge's distance or the Unit's later Melee Attack dice.
- **Ferocious Opening** applies to one Melee Attack, not Charge Impact. Its Critical bypasses Armor and Evade, but not Ward.
- None of the four offensive Commands requires that the Unit or its target be attacked for the first time. Only the Round-dependent bonuses decline.

### Doctrine Identity

Alpha Strike rewards seizing the initiative early in the battle. Its Round 1 offense is strongest, its benefits fall progressively through Round 3, and it retains smaller offensive benefits in Rounds 4–5. Rapid Mobilization offers a once-per-battle way to add one mobile reinforcement during the first Round without changing ordinary Reserves for the rest of the army.

It differs from **Firing Line** through its decreasing ranged bonuses, from **Breakthrough** through its immediate offensive emphasis rather than sustained Charge reliability, and from **Deep Strike** because it adds a single early reinforcement instead of placing Units in unexpected locations.

# 11. Grinding

Foundation Recruitment Slot: C

Strategy: Outlast opponents through endurance, attrition, battlefield repairs, and reorganizing damaged forces.

Grinding combines the former Grinding and Recursion doctrines. Its commands may emphasize either surviving prolonged engagements or becoming increasingly effective as the battle continues.

| Command | Slot | Timing | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unbroken Line | C | Reaction | When a friendly Unit that has already suffered an Attack this Phase is attacked again, it gains Tough 2 against the incoming Attack. |
| Wear Them Down | H | Combat | Choose an enemy Engaged with the active Unit that was already attacked by a different friendly Unit this Round. Reduce that enemy's next Melee Attack by up to two Attack dice, minimum one die remaining, before the end of its next Combat activation. |
| Reform and Recover | S | Movement | Either Heal 2 accumulated Damage from the active Unit, or remove Withdrawn and one temporary Hit, Move, or Evade penalty from it. Defeated Models are not restored. |
| Attrition Engine | A | Assault/Combat | The active Unit gains Accurate 2 on its next Attack against an enemy that has already suffered unsaved Damage from a different friendly Unit this Round. |
| Relentless Veterans | E | Combat | When the active Unit makes a Melee Attack against an enemy that has lost a Model or has accumulated unsaved Damage, its next Melee Attack against that enemy this Combat Phase gains +1 Attack die and Critical 1. This checks the defender's current casualties/Damage, not any previous attacker-target history. |

### Doctrine Identity

Grinding focuses on endurance and cumulative advantage.

Its commands support three approaches:

- Endurance: Unbroken Line and Reform and Recover help Units withstand damage and maintain effectiveness.
- Attrition: Wear Them Down and Attrition Engine reward applying sustained pressure to enemy formations.
- Prolonged Engagements: Relentless Veterans improves a Unit's performance against opponents it has already fought.

A player can lean toward defensive durability, offensive attrition, or a combination of both depending on which three Commands are selected.

Grinding does not restore defeated Models or Units. Those effects remain available through faction-specific abilities and other exceptional rules.

### Distinction from Other Doctrines

- Castling: Rewards holding defensive positions and preparing for attacks.
- Counter Strike: Rewards reactions and retaliation against enemy attacks.
- Grinding: Rewards enduring damage, reorganizing forces, and repeatedly engaging the same enemies.
- Horde: Rewards maintaining large formations and overwhelming enemies numerically.

# 12. Deep Strike

Foundation Recruitment Slot: S

Strategy: Deploy reserve formations in unexpected positions at the end of the Round, trading immediate action and scoring for position during the following Round.

## Shared Reserve Pool and End Step Insertion

Deep Strike draws from the same Reserve pool and Round-based reinforcement allowance as ordinary Reserves. From **Round 2**, declare at most **one** Deep Strike insertion Command during a friendly Movement activation, pay CP/exhaust its card, and designate an eligible Unit still in Reserve. It consumes one arrival from this Round's allowance and is **not placed during ordinary end-of-Movement reinforcement**.

During the **End Step after Scoring**, before expiring other End-of-Round effects, alternate placing designated Deep Strike Units beginning with the side that held First Activation in Movement. Placement must be legally coherent, unengaged, and satisfy the selected Command's restrictions. If a legal position is unavailable, the Unit remains in Reserve, but CP/card cost is not refunded. A designated Unit cannot act or score in its arrival Round, which is already ending; it acts normally from the following Round. Large Assets require Heavy Descent or an explicit exception.

Normal insertion requires a position **wholly more than 8" from every enemy Model** and outside the enemy Deployment Zone. Flanking Insertion allows a board-edge entry inside the enemy Deployment Zone but requires more than 6" from enemies. At most one Unit may use Deep Strike insertion per Round; ordinary Reserves use any other available reinforcement slots.

## Deep Strike Commands

| Command | Slot | Timing | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Forward Insertion | C | Movement | Designate one eligible Reserve Unit for End Step insertion after scoring using normal Deep Strike restrictions (more than 8 inches from enemies, outside enemy Deployment Zone), counting toward normal reinforcement allowance. |
| Landing Fire | R | Assault | If the active Unit arrived through Deep Strike at the end of the previous Round, it gains Accurate 2 and Pinpoint on its next ranged Attack this Assault Phase. Heavy restrictions still apply. |
| Flanking Insertion | V | Movement | Designate one eligible Reserve Unit for End Step placement within 3 inches of a battlefield edge and more than 6 inches from enemies. May enter the enemy Deployment Zone; other requirements apply. |
| Heavy Descent | A | Movement | Designate one eligible Reserve Unit, including a Large Asset, for End Step placement with normal Deep Strike restrictions (more than 8 inches from enemies). Gain Tough 2 against the first Attack suffered in the following Round, then expire. |
| Signal Beacon | H | Movement | Designate the active friendly Unit, which must already be on the battlefield, as a beacon until this Round’s insertion step. A separately designated Deep Strike Unit placed wholly within Short of this beacon may use a more-than-6" enemy exclusion distance instead of 8". Its insertion Command must be used during a different friendly Movement activation; Signal Beacon itself does not insert a Unit. |

## Command Clarifications

- The insertion Commands each cost CP/card use and share the normal reinforcement allowance. They create no extra Reserve pool or immediate Attack.
- **Landing Fire** improves ranged attacks in the Round after insertion. Heavy still applies normally.
- **Heavy Descent** allows Large Asset insertion. Its Tough 2 expires after the first following-Round Attack or at the end of that Round.
- **Signal Beacon** persists through the End Step but cannot insert a Unit itself. It requires a separate Command during another friendly Movement activation.
- The designated Unit stays off the battlefield, providing no auras, targets, or other effects until placement.

Doctrine Identity: Delayed, tactical forward insertion. Unlike ordinary Reserves and Alpha Strike's early reinforcement, Deep Strike trades immediate action for better next-Round positioning.

### Double Down Clarification

Double Down rolls one doubled Armor Save pool, not pairs selecting better results. If the Attack inflicts three Hits, roll six Armor Save dice; at most three successes cancel Hits. Each complete pair of natural 1s in that pool generates one Double Down Backlash and inflicts 1 unpreventable Damage after the Attack. A lone natural 1 has no Backlash effect, and the dice cannot be rerolled. Ordinary Armor Save rolls do not generate Blunders.

### All or Nothing Clarification

The declared target must be an otherwise legal Charge target, within the normal 12" declaration limit and at least 6" away.

If the Charge fails because of insufficient distance or an illegal path, move along a legal path toward the target as far as the modified Charge distance permits without entering Engagement. If no legal movement is possible, the Unit remains in place and still suffers the failed-Charge Damage.

The failed movement does not generate Charge Impact.

Doctrine Identity: High-risk, high-reward dice manipulation.

Unlike Alpha Strike, Gambit accepts substantial uncertainty. Unlike Breakthrough, its Charge enhancement can leave the attacking Unit stranded and damaged.

## Playtesting Priorities

The following effects should be monitored particularly closely.

Gambit — Double Down: Evaluate its performance against both low-Damage attacks and high-Damage attacks. Backlash is independent of Armor quality, so heavily armored Units may benefit disproportionately.

Gambit — Expend the Reserve: Three additional Attack dice can generate substantial damage with powerful single-model weapons. Its lack of rerolls and Blunder penalty should be evaluated against this upside.

Heavy Support — Fire Control: Two friendly Attacks gaining Accurate 1 may be efficient, particularly when one attacks with a large dice pool.

Breakthrough — Momentum Drive: Charge 2 on standard 2D6 Charges substantially improves reliability while extending their maximum reach. Monitor its effects on the frequency of successful Engagements.

Horde — Massed Volley: Standard four-model generic Ranged Infantry now qualify. Test whether its conditional +3 Attacks creates too much ranged output when paired with inexpensive formations.

Castling — Hold Formation: Tough 2 over an entire Combat Phase can prevent considerable damage if one Unit is attacked multiple times. Compare it against one-Attack defensive Reactions.

General Command Targeting: Most commands are usable by any suitable Unit, regardless of its recruitment category. Monitor whether certain expensive Characters and high-Damage Assets consistently become the optimal recipients of generic offensive buffs.

Staggered Deployment — Reinforcement Pacing: Monitor whether ordinary Reserves arriving at the end of Movement can meaningfully affect objectives by Round 4–5, especially on larger maps and for slow melee Units.

Alpha Strike — Round 1 Command Stacking: Test combinations of Opening Volley's +4 Attacks, Shock Assault's Impact +4, and Rapid Mobilization's extra mobile Unit. These Command effects target different aspects of offense but can produce concentrated opening pressure.

Alpha Strike — Ferocious Opening: Round 1 Critical 2 bypasses Armor and Evade without requiring a Surge and can be severe against well-armored Units. Confirm that the Round 1 → Rounds 2–3 → Rounds 4–5 decline (Critical 2 → Critical 1 → Accurate 1) is meaningful without being overwhelming.

Deep Strike — Reinforcement Competition: Confirm that forward insertion is worth selecting despite consuming the same reinforcement allowance as ordinary arrival, and that Deep Strike + Signal Beacon is not too difficult to screen on smaller maps.

### Revision v6.1 — Key Changes

- **Command limits:** one Command (personal Unit, Faction, or Doctrine) per friendly Unit activation, with one eligible opposing Reaction; an Attack, Charge, or Save sequence benefits from at most one friendly Command effect, including effects prepared in an earlier Phase.
- **Critical X:** printed and passive sources now require a Surge; Critical explicitly granted by a CP-paid Command does not.
- **Long Range X:** beyond normal Range the combined Attack pool loses 1 die for single-model Units or 3 dice for multi-model Units (minimum 1) instead of worsening Hit.
- **Elite:** replaced the five Commands with revisions emphasizing Critical, Accurate, Tough, Brace and defensive keyword manipulation; Veteran Spearhead applies to Elite and Elite-subtype Units.
- **Alpha Strike:** revised Opening Volley and Shock Assault; Ferocious Opening is again a Round-based keyword grant (Critical 2 in Round 1, Critical 1 in Rounds 2–3, Accurate 1 in Rounds 4–5) on one Melee Attack, replacing the v6 reroll effect.
- **Horde:** Massed Volley grants +2 Attacks and Accurate 1 in formation instead of +3 Attacks.
- **Gambit:** Expend the Reserve keeps +3 Attacks and its risk; No Quarter's Blunder downside is stronger; All or Nothing grants Charge 2 and Impact +2.
- **Deep Strike:** designation occurs during Movement and placement during the End Step after Scoring, with an 8-inch default enemy exclusion; Landing Fire and Heavy Descent apply in the following Round.
- **PC Charges:** Player Characters use a Body + Athletics Skill Check (Rogues may use Agility + Athletics); Athletics Successes become +1 inch Charge distance or +1 Impact. Added Tier 2 talents Brace Training (Warrior) and Daring Entry (Rogue).
- **Terminology:** Attack names now consistently use Melee Attack / Ranged Attack wording (formerly "Engagement" Attack); corrected the stale Brace description in the Doctrine introduction.

### Revision v6 — Key Changes

- Added universal Staggered Deployment: half the army's Units (rounded down) begin in Reserve; each Round allows up to that Round number of arrivals, beginning in Round 1.
- Reworked Alpha Strike to use declining Round-based bonuses without first-attack restrictions. Opening Barrage was replaced by Rapid Mobilization, which combines a once-per-battle extra Round 1 deployment with a reusable +1 Attack mode.
- Updated Shock Assault to grant Charge Impact rather than Melee Attack dice; updated Ferocious Opening to force rerolls of successful Armor or Evade saves rather than granting Accurate and Critical.
- Updated Deep Strike to draw from the shared mandatory Reserve pool and count its insertions toward the normal reinforcement allowance; the once-per-Round Deep Strike insertion limit remains.
- Updated three obsolete references to Cover granting Tough, and corrected the Doctrine Summary to list Deep Strike instead of the retired Recursion doctrine.

Draft Status: All sixteen doctrines and eighty selectable commands are included. Existing recruitment-slot distributions are preserved. Numerical balance remains provisional pending tabletop testing.

## v0.7 Doctrine Tactical Anti-tag Notes

**Breakthrough — Siege Breaker:** the next eligible Attack gains temporary **Anti-[one chosen applicable defender type] 4** against an Armored Unit, Monster, Vehicle, War Machine, or Fortification; when attacking a Structure/Fortification the printed +1 Damage rider remains. Select the matching type when using the Command. **Horde — Mob Tactics:** instead of a generic +2 Attack dice, the selected friendly Unit gains **Anti-Infantry 4** on its next Melee Attack against enemy Infantry also Engaged by a second friendly Unit with at least four surviving Models. Faction and doctrine tactical counters use highest applicable Anti only, never additive stacks. The other doctrines retain their existing Command choices for this playtest.
